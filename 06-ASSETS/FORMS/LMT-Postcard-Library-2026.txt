================================================================================
  DESIGN NOTES FOR CANVA (4x6 Postcard)
================================================================================

  Front:
  - Dark background (navy or black), white bold headline
  - Orange accent on "Free" or key words
  - Real photo takes up 50-60% of front (top or full bleed)
  - Bottom strip: org name + URL in clean white text

  Back:
  - Left 60%: all content
  - Right 40%: standard mailing format (postage box top right, address lines)
  - QR code bottom left corner of content area
  - Keep font size minimum 9pt for readability at postcard scale
  - Export: PDF Print, 300 DPI, with 0.125" bleed

  Print vendor tips:
  - GotPrint.com or 4over.com — affordable postcard printing
  - Order 500-1000 per library branch for 6-month supply
  - Matte finish back (easier to write on), gloss front
