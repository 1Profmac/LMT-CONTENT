## HOW TO USE
1. Open gemini.google.com (Brian McKinney Work account)
2. Copy the full prompt — Style Block AND Scene
3. Paste into Gemini and generate
4. If off: "Try again. More warm light, more photorealistic, more dignified."
5. Save with the file name listed

## SPONSOR IMAGE 1 — Community Impact
**Save as:** sponsor-community-impact.jpg
**Use for:** Sponsor deck, grant applications, LinkedIn

STYLE: Photorealistic. Warm and professional. Mature dignity — never condescending.
COLOR PALETTE: Deep navy blues, warm oranges, clean whites, soft cyans. No bright primary colors. No neon.
LIGHTING: Warm natural light. Soft window light or golden hour. Never harsh flash or cold fluorescent.
PEOPLE: Adults 50 and older. Diverse — Black, Latino, White, Asian. Men and women. Real faces with age, not filtered or smoothed. Confident expressions. Engaged, focused, calm. Never confused, never helpless, never being assisted by a younger person.
SETTING: Libraries, community centers, home offices, kitchen tables, comfortable indoor spaces. Clean, uncluttered, warm.
FEELING: Safe. Capable. Dignified. "I can do this." Not tech-overwhelmed. Not a stock photo.
AVOID: Cheesy stock photo poses. Exaggerated smiles. Condescending helpers. Cold corporate offices. Young people teaching old people. Cartoon or illustrated style. Watermarks. Text overlaid on image.
BRAND: 50+TechBridge by Learn More Technologies. Austin, Texas. Mission: Digital independence for adults 50 and older.

SCENE: A diverse group of 5 adults aged 55-75 sitting around a large library table with laptops and tablets open. Mix of Black, Latino, and White adults, men and women. All engaged, focused, and confident. Warm overhead and window light. A 65-year-old Black man at the head of the table — not pointing or correcting anyone, just speaking naturally. Community center or library setting. Wide shot. No text in the image.
