# LinkedIn Carousel Template — 10 Slides (Canva)

## Canva Setup
- Size: 1080 x 1080 px (square)
- Font 1 (Headlines): Bold serif — use "Playfair Display" or "DM Serif Display"
- Font 2 (Body): Clean sans — use "Inter" or "DM Sans"
- Brand Colors:
  - Navy: #1B2A4A (backgrounds, headline text)
  - Gold: #D4A843 (accents, highlights, slide numbers)
  - White: #FFFFFF (text on dark backgrounds)
  - Light: #F5F5F0 (alternate slide backgrounds)

## Slide-by-Slide Template

### Slide 1 — HOOK (Navy background)
- Large bold headline (2 lines max)
- Subline: "Swipe to learn more" or "10 slides. Zero jargon."
- Bottom: Brian McKinney | 50+TechBridge
- Gold accent line under headline

### Slide 2 — THE PROBLEM (White background)
- "Here's the problem..." or stat-driven opener
- One bold statement
- Keep to 15-20 words max

### Slide 3 — WHY IT MATTERS (Light background)
- Connect problem to the 50+ audience
- 1-2 short sentences
- Gold highlight on key phrase

### Slide 4 — THE INSIGHT (Navy background)
- The contrarian take or "what most people miss"
- White text, large font
- This is the "aha" slide

### Slide 5 — PROOF / DATA (White background)
- One stat or proof point (big number, bold)
- Source or context below in smaller text
- Example: "targeted 3x higher completion rates"

### Slide 6 — HOW IT WORKS (Light background)
- Step 1 / Step 2 / Step 3 format
- Or: "What we do differently"
- Keep each step to one line

### Slide 7 — REAL EXAMPLE (White background)
- Story, case study, or Pioneer quote
- "One of our Pioneers said..."
- Anonymize if needed

### Slide 8 — THE LESSON (Navy background)
- One sentence takeaway
- Big, bold, centered
- This is the screenshot slide — make it quotable

### Slide 9 — WHAT YOU CAN DO (Light background)
- One actionable step the reader can take today
- Specific enough to use immediately
- "Try this today:" format

### Slide 10 — CTA (Navy background)
- "Follow Brian McKinney for more"
- "Join Adults 50+ across 13 locations: learnmoretechnologies.com/join-now"
- #agentic50 #AgeTech #DigitalInclusion
- Gold accent line

## Weekly Usage (from Content Calendar)
- Tuesday: Build carousel from Monday's WP article
- Wednesday: Post to LinkedIn + FB, send Agentic50 newsletter
- Thursday: Repost to FB Groups

## Tips
- Save as a Canva template — duplicate it each week
- Export as PDF for LinkedIn upload
- Every slide should stand alone as a screenshot
- Max 25 words per slide (except Slide 7 story)
- Alternate navy/white/light backgrounds for visual rhythm
