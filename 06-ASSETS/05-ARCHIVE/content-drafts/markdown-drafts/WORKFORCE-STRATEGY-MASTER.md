**This is wrong on two levels:**
1. It's factually incorrect — Adults 50+ across 13 locations, 13 locations, targeted 3x completion rate
2. Selection committees fund traction, not potential

### The Real Rule
AARP and similar organizations do NOT fund startups looking to begin.
They fund programs already working that need scale.

**You are already working. You were presenting yourself as not working.**

### Fix for Next Application
Never say "just starting." Lead with proof:
- "We have Adults 50+ across 13 locations. We need to reach 3,470."
- "13 locations trust us. We need 230."
- "Our targeted completion rate is 3x the industry. Help us prove it at scale."

### Track 1 — Community Banks (CRA Funding)

**Why this works:** The Community Reinvestment Act (CRA) requires banks to invest in low- and moderate-income communities. Digital literacy for adults 50+ in East Austin qualifies as a **community development service** under OCC CRA guidelines. This is not a donation request — it's a compliance opportunity for the bank.

**Priority targets:**
1. **Frost Bank** — Austin-headquartered, strong community roots, CRA obligations
   - Ask: $10,000 Pioneer Sponsor
   - Pitch email ready: `06-FUNDING/Email-Frost-Bank-Partnership.txt`
   - Contact: Ask for CRA Officer or Community Development Officer
2. **Broadway Bank** — San Antonio/Austin community bank, active in local nonprofits
3. **Amplify Credit Union** — Austin-based, explicit equity mission
4. **Brazos National Bank** — Austin area, smaller ask ($5,000 tier)

**CRA pitch angle:**
> "Adults 50+ trained in digital literacy across LMI census tracts in East Austin. This qualifies for CRA community development credit and addresses your financial empowerment goals directly."

### Track 3 — Federal & State Workforce Funding (WIOA)

**Why this works:** TEGL 03-25 (2025) explicitly authorizes WIOA Adult funds for AI and digital literacy training. Austin's workforce board is actively looking for qualifying providers.

**Gate to clear first:** Industry-recognized credential partner (Google Career Certificates, Microsoft Fundamentals, or CompTIA IT Fundamentals+). Program currently issues certificates of participation only.

## AARP PITCH REWRITE FRAMEWORK

### Old Script (what didn't work)
"We're just starting and building our program..."

### New Script (lead with proof)
Opening line:
"Adults 50+ across 13 locations completed our program. targeted 3x the industry completion rate. 13 locations deployed. We're not asking you to bet on a idea — we're asking you to scale what's already working."

Structure:
1. THE PROBLEM — 38M adults 50+ being locked out of AI economy
2. THE PROOF — Adults 50+ across 13 locations, targeted 3x completion, increased confidence
3. THE ASK — specific dollar amount for specific outcome
4. THE ROI — what AARP gets: impact data, co-branding, reach

Key rules:
- Never use the word "hope" or "plan to"
- Never say "we're building" — say "we built"
- Every claim needs a number
- End with a specific ask, not an open question
