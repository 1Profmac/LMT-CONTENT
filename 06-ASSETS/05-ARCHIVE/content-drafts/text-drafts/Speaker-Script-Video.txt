50+TECHBRIDGE — COMMITTEE PITCH VIDEO SCRIPT
10 Slides | Target runtime: 8–10 minutes | ~60–90 seconds per slide

RECORDING TIPS:
- Open Pitch-Deck-10Slides.html in Chrome, fullscreen (F11)
- Record screen + face (OBS or Zoom record-to-computer)
- Advance slides manually with spacebar or arrow keys
- Pause briefly before each new slide — gives you a clean edit point
- Say the slide number in your head before speaking — don't rush

---

SLIDE 1 — TITLE
[Look at camera. Pause 2 seconds before speaking.]

"Three hundred and forty-seven adults in Austin, Texas — people in their 60s, 70s, and 80s — changed their relationship with technology in one hour.

My name is Brian McKinney. I'm the founder of Learn More Technologies and the creator of 50+TechBridge. I'm a former AARP Community Development Manager, I'm MBE certified, and I've spent the last two years doing one thing: making sure adults 50+ don't get left behind by the digital world.

Today I'm going to show you exactly what we built, why it works, and what we're asking you to help us do next."

---

SLIDE 2 — THE PROBLEM
[Lean in slightly. This is the "why it matters" moment.]

"Thirty-eight million Americans 50 and older are effectively locked out of the digital economy.

Not because they're not smart. Not because they don't want to learn. But because nobody built something for them the right way.

Thirty-four percent of adults 65 and older don't use the internet at all — and that number is from 2024, before AI made the gap even wider.

Here in Austin, there are 280,000 adults 50 and older. That's the fastest-growing demographic in Travis County. And they are underserved, overlooked, and running out of time to catch up."

---

SLIDE 3 — WHY NOW
[Steady. Confident. This is urgency, not fear.]

"This isn't a future problem. It's happening right now.

AI is the new literacy. ChatGPT, Alexa, telehealth portals, prescription apps — every new tool that launches is one more thing an adult 50 plus doesn't know how to use.

The stakes are independence. Scheduling a doctor's appointment online. Refilling a prescription from the couch. Video calling a grandchild across the country. These are not optional anymore. They require a device and the confidence to use it.

And the cost of inaction is real. Adults who lack digital skills pay more for services, miss benefits they qualify for, and become more dependent — more quickly. We can change that."

---

SLIDE 4 — THE SOLUTION
[Open body language. This is your product. Be proud.]

"50+TechBridge is a free AI and digital literacy program built exclusively for adults 50 plus.

Here's what makes it different: the format. We don't invite seniors to a classroom. We come to them — during lunch, at places they already trust. Senior centers. Churches. Libraries. Community organizations.

Forty-five minutes. No laptops required. No sign-in sheets. No pressure. Just a practical, relaxed conversation about technology during a meal they were already having.

Then every participant gets free access to our full online course at learnmoretechnologies.com. Zero cost to them. Zero cost to the host organization."

---

SLIDE 5 — HOW IT WORKS
[Walk through the steps. Use your hands. This is the business model.]

"The model is simple and it's replicable anywhere there's a cafeteria and a schedule.

Step one: a partner organization — a senior center, church, or library — hosts us during their regular lunch hour. No extra setup needed. We bring the presentation.

Step two: I deliver a 45-minute presentation. People can bring their phone, their tablet, or just themselves. No technology required to attend.

Step three: everyone who wants to keep learning gets a free link to our full three-lesson online course.

Step four: completers earn their Pioneer certificate and join the 50+TechBridge community.

Step five: the host organization gets an impact summary. They know exactly how many of their members we trained, what we covered, and how they felt about it.

That's the whole system. It runs on relationships and trust — two things seniors respond to better than any marketing campaign."

---

SLIDE 6 — PROOF
[This is your moment. Slow down. Let the numbers land.]

"Now here's what I want you to hear most carefully, because this is the thing that separates us from every other digital literacy program you'll see today.

We are not asking you to fund an experiment.

Three hundred and forty-seven Pioneers trained. Self-funded. Three times the industry course completion rate. Seventy-four percent of participants say they feel more confident with technology after a single session. Twenty-three organizations have deployed this program.

We built this with essentially no budget — just the right format, the right environment, and the right respect for the people we serve.

The model works. We've proven it. What we need now is the scale."

---

SLIDE 7 — THE SCALE PLAN
[This is strategy. Be specific. Funders love specifics.]

"Getting from adults 50+ across 13 locations to 1,000 Pioneers is not a theory. It's a logistics plan.

Austin has nine City of Austin senior centers. Every single one of them serves congregate lunch daily. The Lunch and Learn format is built into their existing schedule. We can deploy to all nine this year.

We have 23 existing partner organizations we can re-engage for recurring quarterly sessions. That's over 90 additional events per year just from current relationships.

We have a target list of 18 Austin churches with active 50 plus ministries and 21 Austin Public Library branches with senior programming.

Phase one — getting to 500 Pioneers — costs $35,600. Phase two, getting to 1,000, costs about $97,000 a year once we have a program coordinator in place.

For context: we've been running this entire program on roughly $1,000 a year in out-of-pocket costs. Every dollar you invest goes directly into reach."

---

SLIDE 8 — WHO WE SERVE
[Slow down here. These are real people. Let them feel it.]

"I want to tell you about three Pioneers.

Gloria is 68 years old. She couldn't figure out how to video call her grandchildren in Dallas. After one session, she's using FaceTime every Sunday.

Robert is 72. He was driving 40 minutes round trip to refill prescriptions. He uses the CVS app now. The first time he opened it successfully, he called his daughter just to tell her.

And Margaret is 61. She takes care of her 84-year-old mother. She learned how to set up telehealth appointments and medication reminders using a $40 Amazon Echo.

These are not tech stories. These are independence stories. And there are 280,000 more of them waiting in Austin alone."

---

SLIDE 9 — THE ASK
[Direct. Clear. No hedging. This is the close.]

"Here's what I'm asking for.

Ten thousand dollars. That's our Pioneer Sponsor tier.

Here's exactly what that $10,000 does: it funds four months of a part-time program coordinator — the operational piece we don't currently have — and it deploys the program to at least 20 Lunch and Learn events across Austin, training a minimum of 43 new Pioneers with your organization's name on every piece of material.

You receive full impact documentation, CRA credit documentation if you're a financial institution, quarterly briefings, and a community investment story you can actually tell.

This is not a charitable donation. This is a proven program with a proven model asking for the resources to serve more people. The outcomes are documented. The method is tested. The Pioneers are real."

---

SLIDE 10 — CLOSE
[Look straight at camera. Warm but confident. End with the ask in the air.]

"Three hundred and forty-seven Pioneers crossed the bridge.

The next 153 are waiting at their lunch tables right now — at Conley-Guerrero, at Dove Springs, at First Baptist, at the Austin Public Library on Cesar Chavez.

They're not waiting for a new program. They're waiting for this one to show up.

The question I'm leaving you with today is simple: will you help us take the next 153 Pioneers across the bridge?

I'm Brian McKinney. I'm at 512-200-4241. I'm at hello@learnmoretechnologies.com. And I'm ready to talk about what a partnership looks like — today, this week, on your schedule.

Thank you."
