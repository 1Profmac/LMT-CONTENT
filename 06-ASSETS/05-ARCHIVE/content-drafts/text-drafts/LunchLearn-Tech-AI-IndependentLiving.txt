===========================
WHAT "INDEPENDENT LIVING" MEANS IN THIS CONTEXT
===========================

When we say tech helps with independent living, we mean:

1. HEALTH MANAGEMENT
   - Medication reminders (voice assistant)
   - Telehealth appointments (video calls)
   - Health questions answered instantly (AI assistant)
   - Tracking blood pressure, glucose, steps (phone apps)

2. STAYING CONNECTED
   - Video calls with family (no driving required)
   - Texting with voice-to-text (no tiny keyboard)
   - Sharing photos with grandkids
   - Joining community groups online

3. DAILY TASKS
   - Grocery delivery (Instacart, HEB)
   - Ride services (Uber, Lyft, GoGo Grandparent)
   - Bill pay from your phone
   - AI-generated grocery lists, meal plans, budgets

4. SAFETY
   - Emergency SOS on every smartphone
   - Scam detection — what to look for
   - Location sharing with family
   - Fall detection (Apple Watch, Samsung)

5. MENTAL SHARPNESS
   - Learning new skills keeps your brain active
   - AI as a conversation partner
   - Audiobooks and podcasts
   - Brain games and puzzles
