Now You Do It:

  1. Write the fridge rule on a sticky note: Unexpected + hurry + money or a code = hang up and verify.
  2. Pick a family code word — one phrase only your people know. Text it to them today. Not on Facebook.
  3. Go to your email app → Settings → Security → turn on Two-factor authentication.
  4. Check your email password. Is it the same as your bank? Change one to a long phrase like maple-porch-radio-Thursday.
  5. If a scam already happened: call the bank now, then report at ReportFraud.ftc.gov and ic3.gov.
