The Headline

The Real Reason Tech Training Fails Adults 50+

Every organization we've worked with told us the same thing: "Our older participants just aren't motivated." After 23 deployments, I can tell you — that's wrong. Most tech training was built for younger learners and handed to older ones without changing a thing. When we changed the model — peer instructors, project-based learning, no jargon — completion rates hit targeted 3X the industry average. Same zip codes. Same demographics. Different approach.

Adults 50+ don't have a motivation problem. They have a training problem.
