## Example — Edition 3 (Special Launch)

### THE PIONEER BRIDGE REPORT
*News from the front lines of the 50+ tech revolution*

🔵 **AI adoption among adults 50+ nearly doubled** — From 18% to 30% in one year. Voice assistants and health AI are leading the way.
→ [AARP 2026 Tech Trends](https://www.aarp.org/pri/topics/technology/internet-media-devices/2026-technology-trends-older-adults/)

🔵 **AARP leads CES 2026 with AgeTech vision** — The AgeTech Collaborative unveiled how AI will shape the future of aging at the world's biggest tech conference.
→ [AARP at CES 2026](https://www.aarp.org/press/releases/2025-12-22-the-agetech-collaborative-from-aarp-leads-ces-2026-with-visionary-look-at-how-ai-will-shape-the-future-of-aging/)

🟡 **Two-thirds of workers 50+ have seen age discrimination** — New AARP survey: 64% experienced ageism at work. 22% say they're being pushed out.
→ [AARP Age Bias Survey 2026](https://www.aarp.org/work/age-discrimination/age-bias-survey-2026/)

🟡 **Ageism complaints skyrocketed on Glassdoor** — Older job seekers are speaking up in record numbers about being passed over for younger candidates.
→ [HR Dive Report](https://www.hrdive.com/news/older-job-seekers-complaints-about-ageism-skyrocket-glassdoor/759879/)

🟢 **Older adults driving workforce change in digital world** — World Economic Forum: adults 50+ are not leaving the workforce — they're reshaping it.
→ [WEF Article](https://www.weforum.org/stories/2025/02/older-adults-workforce-innovation-digital-economy/)

💡 **Brian's Pick: The hidden cost of ageism in America's workforce** — This article from The Hill breaks down exactly what we've been saying — the $850B problem is real, and it's getting worse.
→ [The Hill](https://thehill.com/lobbying/5536916-age-discrimination-impacts-workforce/)

## How to Curate Each Week

1. Check Google Alerts (already set up — 21 alerts)
2. Check Talkwalker Alerts (5 alerts)
3. Scan the source list above for fresh articles
4. Pick 4-6 links across at least 3 categories
5. Write 1-sentence summary for each
6. Add "Brian's Pick" with a personal note
7. Paste into newsletter template
