# AGENTIC50 — 4-Question Subscriber Questionnaire (Revenue-Driven)

**Purpose:** Sort new subscribers into the right sales path. Every answer maps to an action.
**Research basis:** Craig Hewitt's onboarding segmentation + newsletter conversion best practices.

---

## THE 4 QUESTIONS

### Q1: WHO ARE YOU? (Segment → Checklist Rotation)

**Which one sounds most like you?**
- A. I'm working and want to stay relevant with AI
- B. I run a business and need to modernize
- C. I'm building something new
- D. I want AI for daily life and independence

| Answer | Mailchimp Tag | Action |
|---|---|---|
| A | agentic50-workforce | Send workforce checklist |
| B | agentic50-entrepreneur | Send business owner checklist |
| C | agentic50-startup | Send startup checklist |
| D | agentic50-pioneer | Send everyday pioneer checklist |

---

### Q2: WHAT'S YOUR BIGGEST CHALLENGE? (Pain Point → Email Tone)

**What's holding you back with technology?**
- A. I don't know where to start
- B. I've tried but it didn't stick
- C. I need it for work but no one's teaching me
- D. I want to learn but I don't have time

| Answer | Mailchimp Tag | What It Means |
|---|---|---|
| A | pain-starting | Needs the Lunch & Learn. Warm them up. |
| B | pain-burned | Bad experience with other training. Lead with "we're different." |
| C | pain-work | Workforce partner lead. Their employer should be paying for this. |
| D | pain-time | Needs self-paced. Push the online course hard. |

---

### Q3: WHAT WOULD HELP MOST? (Buy Signal → Sales Path)

**What would help you most right now?**
- A. A free course I can do on my phone
- B. A live workshop at my church, library, or center
- C. Someone to train my organization's members
- D. One-on-one coaching

| Answer | Mailchimp Tag | Brian's Next Move | Revenue? |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | ready-self-serve | Newsletter + course drip | Not yet |
| B | ready-workshop | Invite to next Lunch & Learn | Lead gen |
| C | ready-partner | Personal email within 48 hours | YES — contract |
| D | ready-private | Send offer within 24 hours | YES — private client |

**Q3-C and Q3-D are revenue. Prioritize these people.**

## FOUR CHECKLISTS (Rotate Weekly by Q1 Segment)

### Week 1: EVERYDAY PIONEER (Q1-D)
**"Use AI for Real Life This Week — 5 Moves"**

- [ ] Ask your phone's voice assistant to set a daily medication or appointment reminder
- [ ] Ask ChatGPT: "Write a grocery list for a healthy dinner for two under $15"
- [ ] Ask AI: "Explain [any health condition] in simple terms — what should I ask my doctor?"
- [ ] Use AI to write a birthday message, thank you note, or letter to a friend
- [ ] Ask ChatGPT: "What's one new hobby I could start that doesn't cost money?"

### Week 2: WORKFORCE (Q1-A)
**"Stay Relevant at Work This Week — 5 AI Moves"**

- [ ] Open ChatGPT and ask it to summarize your last meeting notes in 3 bullet points
- [ ] Ask AI to draft a reply to the most annoying email in your inbox right now
- [ ] Search your company's job board — do any new postings mention "AI skills"?
- [ ] Ask ChatGPT: "What AI skills are most in demand for [your job title]?"
- [ ] Spend 10 minutes on one free AI course (LinkedIn Learning, Coursera, or 50+TechBridge)

### Week 3: BUSINESS OWNER (Q1-B)
**"Modernize Your Business This Week — 5 AI Moves"**

- [ ] Ask ChatGPT to write a social media post about your business — then edit it in your voice
- [ ] Upload your last customer complaint and ask AI: "How should I respond professionally?"
- [ ] Ask AI: "What are 3 ways a [your type of business] could save time using AI?"
- [ ] Try Canva's AI to create a quick flyer or social graphic for your business
- [ ] Ask ChatGPT: "Write me a 30-second elevator pitch for [your business name]"

### Week 4: STARTUP (Q1-C)
**"Build Your Idea This Week — 5 AI Moves"**

- [ ] Describe your business idea to ChatGPT in 2 sentences. Ask: "What are the first 3 things I should do?"
- [ ] Ask AI: "Who is my ideal customer for [your idea]?"
- [ ] Use ChatGPT to write a one-page business plan outline
- [ ] Ask AI: "What free tools can I use to build a landing page for my business?"
- [ ] Ask ChatGPT: "What grants or funding exist for [your demographic] starting a business in [your city]?"
