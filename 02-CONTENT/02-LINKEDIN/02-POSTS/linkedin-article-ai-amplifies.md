### What It Used to Take

Not long ago, launching an online business meant hiring a team before you earned a dollar:

- A social media manager: $1,500–$3,000 a month
- A bookkeeper: $300–$500 a month
- A customer service rep: $1,500–$2,500 a month
- A content writer: $400–$2,000 a month
- A virtual assistant: $800–$2,000 a month

Total: $5,000–$13,000 a month. $60,000–$156,000 a year.

For most adults 50+ with a lifetime of expertise and a business idea, that number ended the conversation before it started.

### What It Takes Now

- ChatGPT for content, email, and strategy: $20 a month
- Canva for design: $13 a month
- Buffer for social media: $15–$100 a month
- QuickBooks AI for bookkeeping: $20 a month
- Calendly for scheduling: $12 a month
- Zapier for automation: $20–$70 a month

Total: $300–$625 a month. Under $7,500 a year.

That is a 95 percent reduction in the cost of running a business.

This is not opinion. This is research.

McKinsey found that AI can automate 60–70 percent of employee work activities.

The tools are here. The data is clear. The cost barrier that kept experienced professionals from building businesses no longer exists.

### What AI Cannot Do

AI cannot replace your judgment.

It cannot replace 30 years of knowing which client is bluffing. It cannot replace the instinct that tells you when a deal feels wrong. It cannot replace the relationships you built over decades.

AI handles the repetitive work. You handle the decisions that matter.

Your decades of experience give you an edge — AI simply amplifies your strengths.
