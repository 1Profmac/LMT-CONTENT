---

### The 5 AI Tools Adults 50+ Actually Use (And What Each One Is Good For)

#### 1. ChatGPT (OpenAI)
**Best for:** Writing, explaining things, answering questions
**Free version:** Yes
**Ease of use:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (4/5)

ChatGPT is the most versatile AI tool available. You type a question or request, and it responds in plain English. Adults 50+ use it to:
- Draft emails and letters
- Explain medical terms in simple language
- Plan trips and create packing lists
- Write birthday messages and speeches
- Summarize long articles

**What it doesn't do well:** It can't browse the internet in real-time on the free plan. It sometimes gives outdated information. Always verify important facts.

---

#### 2. Google Gemini
**Best for:** Research, real-time information, Google integration
**Free version:** Yes
**Ease of use:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (5/5 — especially if you use Gmail)

If you already use Google, Gemini is the easiest starting point. It connects directly to your Gmail, Google Docs, and Google Calendar. Adults 50+ use it to:
- Search for current information (unlike ChatGPT free, it browses the web)
- Summarize emails
- Get directions and local recommendations
- Find recent news without ads

**What it doesn't do well:** Less creative than ChatGPT for writing tasks. Better for finding information than generating content.

---

#### 3. Microsoft Copilot
**Best for:** Windows users, Word and Excel integration
**Free version:** Yes (built into Windows 11)
**Ease of use:** ⭐⭐⭐ (3/5)

If you use Microsoft Word or Excel, Copilot is already on your computer. Adults 50+ use it to:
- Fix spelling and grammar in Word documents
- Summarize long documents
- Create simple spreadsheets
- Search the web from inside Word

**What it doesn't do well:** More complex to navigate. Best if you already use Microsoft Office regularly.

---

#### 4. Claude (Anthropic)
**Best for:** Long documents, careful explanations, nuanced conversation
**Free version:** Yes
**Ease of use:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (4/5)

Claude is known for giving thoughtful, careful responses. Adults 50+ use it to:
- Read and summarize long contracts or medical documents
- Have a back-and-forth conversation about a topic
- Get step-by-step explanations of complex topics
- Write formal letters and business documents

**What it doesn't do well:** No image generation. Less integrated with everyday apps than Google Gemini.

---

#### 5. Perplexity AI
**Best for:** Research and fact-checking with sources
**Free version:** Yes
**Ease of use:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (4/5)

Perplexity is like a search engine that actually explains what it found — and cites its sources. Adults 50+ use it to:
- Research health conditions with real sources
- Check news stories
- Compare products and services
- Get answers with links to verify

**What it doesn't do well:** Not great for creative writing or document drafting.

### Which One Should You Start With?

**If you use Gmail → Start with Google Gemini.**
**If you use Windows → Start with Microsoft Copilot.**
**If you want the most powerful writing tool → Start with ChatGPT.**
**If you read a lot of long documents → Try Claude.**
**If you want sourced answers → Try Perplexity.**

Our recommendation for most adults 50+: **Start with Google Gemini.** It's free, it connects to tools you already use, and it's the easiest to get value from on day one.
