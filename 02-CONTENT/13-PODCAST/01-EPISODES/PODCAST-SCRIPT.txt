[SEGMENT 1 — MARGARET'S STORY | ~4 minutes]

"Let me tell you about Margaret.

Margaret is 61. She grew up in Austin.
She spent the last six years as the primary caregiver
for her mother, who had complex health needs —
multiple doctors, complicated medications,
insurance paperwork that would make your head spin,
family members who needed everything translated
from medical language into plain English,
and a system that was not designed
to help anyone navigate it alone.

Margaret did that. For six years.

Her mother passed in 2024.

And a few weeks later, Margaret came to one of our
Lunch and Learn sessions at a senior center.
She sat in the back. She was quiet.
I remember noticing her because she was
paying attention differently than most people do —
not just listening, but processing.
Like she was filing everything away.

After the session she found me.

And she said: 'I've been doing this my whole life.
Taking care of people. Managing complicated situations.
Solving problems nobody else wanted to touch.
I'm good at it. I know I'm good at it.
Is there a way to do that and actually get paid?'

And I said: yes.

The answer is yes.

The answer has always been yes.

But here's what's different today:

AI is the infrastructure that Margaret was missing.
AI is the infrastructure that a lot of people over 50 are missing.

Let me tell you what I mean."

[SEGMENT 3 — THE 7 STEPS | ~10 minutes]

"Seven steps. I'm going to go through all of them.
If you're driving, don't worry — this episode has
a full transcript at learnmoretechnologies.com.

---

Step one: Name what you know.

Before you open any app or tool —
grab a piece of paper and write down
five things you've done in your life
that other people found hard or confusing.

Not things you were paid for.
Things you just did — because you knew how,
because someone needed help,
because you were the person in the room
who understood it.

For Margaret: managing complex medical schedules.
Coordinating between multiple specialists.
Translating medical language for families in crisis.
Navigating insurance systems.

For Robert — one of our Adults 50+ across 13 locations:
running a construction crew, reading building plans,
negotiating with suppliers.

For Gloria — another Pioneer, 68 years old:
thirty years as a home health aide.
Knowing what families in crisis need before they ask.
Knowing how to calm a frightened patient.
Knowing what questions to ask the doctor
when the family doesn't know what to ask.

These are not hobbies.
These are businesses.

The only thing missing was the infrastructure.

---

Step two: Build your business plan in 30 minutes.

Open ChatGPT — free at chat.openai.com.
Or Claude — free at claude.ai.
Both work on your phone.

Type this:
'I want to start a small business helping [describe what you do].
My customers are [describe who needs it].
I want to work from home and charge enough to make it worth my time.
Help me build a simple one-page business plan.'

In five minutes you have a business plan.

Service name. Target customer. Pricing.
First five marketing ideas. A professional summary.

Print it. Put it on the wall.
You are now a business owner.

---

Step three: Name your business and build your brand.

Same tool. Next prompt:
'Help me come up with five names for my business.
Then write a one-sentence description of what I offer.
Then write a short professional bio I can use on a website.'

You give it the raw material — your age, your experience,
your reason for starting.
It shapes it into language that works.

Twenty minutes. Done.

---

Step four: Get online for free.

Website: go to Wix.com or Squarespace.com.
Both have free plans. Templates you fill in like a form.
Ask the AI to write every word that goes on the page.

Google: go to business.google.com.
Create a free business profile.
This puts you on Google Maps and Google Search.

Website and Google presence. Cost: zero.
Time: one afternoon.

---

Step five: Find your first client.

Not with an ad campaign.
Not with a social media strategy.

With a list of ten people who already know you.

Ask the AI to help you write a personal message —
not salesy, just honest —
explaining what you're now offering
and who you'd love an introduction to.

Send it to ten people.

One of those ten knows someone who needs exactly what you do.

That's your first client.
I've seen this work every single time.

---

Step six: The free business stack.

Everything you need to run a legitimate solo business —
completely free:

Google Calendar: your schedule.
Gmail: your professional email.
Google Docs: proposals, contracts, client notes.
Wave.com: invoicing and accounting. Completely free.
Canva: business cards, flyers, social graphics. Free.
ChatGPT or Claude: your writer and strategist. Free.

Monthly cost: zero dollars.
Complete business infrastructure.

---

Step seven: The daily habit.

Every morning, before you start work,
open your AI tool and ask one question.

Could be:
'I have a client meeting today. What should I ask to understand their needs?'

Or: 'Help me write a follow-up email that's professional but not pushy.'

Or: 'How do I price a project like this? Here's what's involved.'

The AI gives you a starting point.
Something to react to rather than create from scratch.

One question. Every morning.
That habit runs the business."
