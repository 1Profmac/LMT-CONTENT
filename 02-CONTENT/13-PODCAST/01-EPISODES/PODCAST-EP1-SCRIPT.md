## SCRIPT (spoken words only — conversational, not read)

Hey, welcome to Agentic50. I'm Brian McKinney.

If you're over 50 and you've ever felt like technology left you behind, this podcast is for you. Not tech news. Not jargon. Just real tools explained by someone who gets it.

Today I want to talk about the phone in your pocket.

You probably use it for calls. Texts. Maybe Facebook. And that's about it.

Here's the thing. That phone can do about a hundred things you've never been shown. And I'm not talking about fancy stuff. I'm talking about making the text bigger so you can actually read it. Setting a reminder so you don't forget your medication. Ordering groceries without getting in the car.

Basic stuff. Life stuff. But nobody ever sat down and showed you.

That's not your fault.

I run a program called 50+TechBridge. We teach adults over 50 how to use their phones and how to use AI. And I noticed something. People were signing up for our lessons but nobody was finishing them.

So I asked myself why. And the answer was embarrassing. My lessons looked like a college course. Long videos. Walls of text. A quiz at the end. It felt like school. And nobody over 50 wants to go back to school.

So I blew it up and started over.

I built a new version of Lesson 1. It takes five minutes. A friendly guide named Barb walks you through it step by step. You actually do something on your phone. And when you're done, instead of a quiz, you get a celebration. Confetti on the screen. A message that says you're a Digital Pioneer.

That's what we call our learners. Not students. Not users. Digital Pioneers. Because that's what you are when you pick up your phone and decide to learn something new at 55 or 65 or 75. You're pioneering.

So here's what Lesson 1 teaches you. Two things.

First, you make your text bigger. You go into Settings, tap Display, find the Text Size slider, and drag it to the right. Everything on your phone gets bigger. Easier to read. No squinting.

Second, you turn on Bold Text. Same area. Settings, Accessibility, Bold Text, toggle it on. Now everything is bigger AND bolder.

That's it. Two minutes. And your phone is now customized for your eyes.

I know that sounds simple. It is simple. But here's why it matters.

Most people over 50 are using a phone that was set up by default for a 25 year old with perfect vision. The text is tiny. The buttons are small. And every time you pick it up, you feel a little frustrated. A little like this thing wasn't made for you.

But it was. You just need to adjust it. And once you do, something shifts. You realize oh, I can change this. I have control here. And that's the beginning of everything.

That's why Lesson 1 isn't about text size. It's about permission. Permission to touch the settings. Permission to make the phone yours. Permission to be a Digital Pioneer.

If you want to try it, go to learnmoretechnologies.com. Click Start Free Lessons. Lesson 1 takes five minutes. Barb will walk you through it. And when you're done, show someone. Say look, I just made my text bigger. Watch their face.

That's the assignment. Five minutes and one show and tell.

Next week on Agentic50, I'm going to tell you about three tools on your phone that save you time every single week. Voice reminders, weather, and your pharmacy app. You already have all of them. You just haven't turned them on.

I'm Brian McKinney. This is Agentic50. And you're not done yet.
