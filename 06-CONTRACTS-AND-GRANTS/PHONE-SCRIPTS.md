## SCRIPT 3 — "The Workforce & Grant Manager"
**Use for:** Calling workforce boards, WIOA contacts, government grant managers, HR departments

> "Hi [Name], this is Brian McKinney from 50+TechBridge — 50plustechbridge.com. We deliver free AI and digital literacy training for adults 50+ through a workforce re-entry lens. We're WIOA-eligible, SAM.gov registered, and MBE certified. I'm reaching out about [grant name / partnership opportunity] and would love 15 minutes to connect. You can reach me at [your number]. Thank you."

**Best for:** MassHire Greater Lowell, Workforce Solutions Capital Area, TWC contacts, MCOA
