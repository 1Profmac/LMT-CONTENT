## ITEMIZED OPPORTUNITIES FOR LMT

### Opportunity 1: OWN THE AI LITERACY SPACE FOR 50+
- **What:** No one — not AARP, not Senior Planet, not libraries — is teaching AI literacy to 50+ adults in a structured curriculum
- **Size:** 117M adults 50+ in the US. Zero dedicated AI education programs for them.
- **LMT advantage:** First mover. The curriculum exists. The model is proven.
- **Revenue path:** License curriculum to libraries, senior centers, workforce boards

### Opportunity 2: BECOME AN AARP SENIOR PLANET PARTNER SITE
- **What:** Apply to join AARP's 425+ partner network. Run their basic curriculum AND your AI curriculum on top.
- **Benefit:** AARP brand credibility + access to their referral pipeline
- **How:** Apply at oats.org/senior-planet-from-aarp
- **LMT angle:** "We already run the same model at the same venues. Adding Senior Planet designation validates us nationally."

### Opportunity 3: AARP COMMUNITY CHALLENGE GRANT

- **Status:** 2026 applications were open (deadline was March 4, 2026 — missed this cycle)
- **Next cycle:** Watch for 2027 announcement (likely January 2027)
- **Ask:** $10K–$25K for AI workshop expansion at Austin libraries

### Opportunity 4: DIGITAL SKILLS READY@50+ PARTNERSHIP
- **What:** AARP Foundation's $10M Google-funded program trains 50+ adults in digital skills
- **Model:** They provide curriculum + instructor training OR send their own instructor
- **LMT play:** Partner with them for basic skills delivery, then upsell to LMT's AI curriculum
- **Contact:** signup.aarpfoundation.org

### Opportunity 5: WIOA AI LITERACY FUNDING
- **What:** TEGL 03-25 authorizes WIOA funds for AI literacy training
- **Why LMT wins:** AARP doesn't position for WIOA. LMT does.
- **Size:** $50K+ per workforce board partnership
- **Contact:** Workforce Solutions Capital Area (Austin's WIOA board)

### Opportunity 6: LIBRARY SYSTEM CONTRACTS
- **What:** Austin Public Library + surrounding systems need digital programming
- **AARP's model:** Free, volunteer-driven, basic
- **LMT's model:** Structured, AI-focused, measurable outcomes
- **Revenue path:** Paid facilitator contracts with library systems, not just volunteer workshops

### Opportunity 7: FRANCHISE / LICENSE THE CURRICULUM
- **What:** Once Austin is proven at scale (Elevate grant), package the Digital Pioneer curriculum for other cities
- **Model:** Train-the-Trainer — same as AARP but focused on AI

- **Timeline:** Post-Elevate (2027–2028)

- **What:** Ask Barb + learning modules as a standalone app
