## WHAT THIS DOES

Once approved on TrainingPro, 50+TechBridge courses become **WIOA-funded** — meaning adults can use their Individual Training Accounts (ITAs) to pay for LMT training. Instead of chasing grants, eligible participants bring the funding directly to you.

### Accreditation Pathway Options

TrainingPro requires providers to be licensed or accredited by one of:
- MA Department of Elementary and Secondary Education (DESE)
- MA Board of Higher Education
- NEASC (New England Association of Schools and Colleges)
- Other recognized agency

**Recommended path for LMT:**
1. **Contact MassHire** (local Workforce Board) — they can advise on the fastest approval path for a digital literacy nonprofit
2. Check if WIOA "on-the-job training" or "customized training" designation applies
3. Inquire about **non-traditional provider** approval pathway through the local Workforce Development Board

## COURSE DESCRIPTIONS FOR TRAININGPRO

### Course 1: AI Literacy for Adults 50+ (Foundation)
- **Level:** Beginner
- **Duration:** 6 sessions × 90 minutes = 9 hours
- **Format:** In-person, hands-on
- **Cost:** Free (grant-funded) or ITA-eligible
- **Credential:** Digital Pioneer Certificate of Completion
- **Description:** A hands-on introduction to AI tools for adults 50+. Participants learn to use AI assistants for everyday tasks, stay safe online, communicate with family, and manage health information. No prior tech experience required.
- **Outcomes:** Participants demonstrate ability to use AI tools independently for 5 practical use cases

### Course 2: Digital Safety and AI Literacy (Intermediate)
- **Level:** Intermediate
- **Duration:** 4 sessions × 90 minutes = 6 hours
- **Format:** In-person or online (LearnDash)
- **Credential:** Digital Pioneer Advanced Certificate
- **Description:** Builds on Foundation course. Covers AI-generated misinformation detection, privacy settings, cybersecurity basics, and advanced AI tools for independent living.

### Course 3: AI for Workforce Re-entry (Workforce Track)
- **Level:** Intermediate
- **Duration:** 8 sessions × 90 minutes = 12 hours
- **Format:** In-person
- **Credential:** Digital Pioneer Workforce Ready Certificate
- **Description:** Designed for adults 50+ re-entering the workforce. Covers AI tools for job searching, resume writing, LinkedIn, online applications, and workplace technology.
- **WIOA alignment:** Directly supports workforce re-entry goals for dislocated workers 50+

## REGISTRATION STEPS

1. Go to mass.gov/how-to/how-to-register-on-trainingpro
2. Create organization account under Learn More Technologies, LLC
3. Submit Course 1 first (simplest, broadest eligibility)
4. Attach: org overview, course curriculum outline, facilitator credentials (Brian McKinney bio)
5. Contact MassHire Greater Lowell to discuss ITA eligibility and accreditation pathway

## FLAG: 501(c)(3) TIMING

TrainingPro approval is easier with 501(c)(3) in hand. If the EIN application completes in July 2026 and the 501(c)(3) follows, target TrainingPro registration Q4 2026 after nonprofit status is confirmed.

In the meantime, register as an LLC provider — it is still eligible if courses meet WIOA criteria.
