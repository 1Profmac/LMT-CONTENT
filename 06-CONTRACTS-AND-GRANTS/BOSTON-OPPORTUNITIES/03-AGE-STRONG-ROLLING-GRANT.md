## PROPOSED PROJECT

**Project Title:** AI Literacy Workshops for Boston's Older Adults — Pilot Series

**Description:**
A 3-month pilot workshop series delivering free AI literacy training to adults 50+ at Boston senior centers and community spaces. Each workshop is 60–90 minutes, hands-on, and customized to the host location's participants.

**What participants will learn:**
- How to use AI tools for everyday tasks (finding information, writing messages, staying safe online)
- How to spot AI-generated misinformation
- How to use voice AI and accessibility features for independence
- Practical "Digital Pioneer" skills they can use the same day

**Format:** In-person, hands-on workshops — no prior tech experience required

**Locations:** 2–3 BCYF senior centers or Age Strong partner sites (specific locations to be confirmed with Age Strong staff)

**Schedule:** 6 workshops over 3 months (2 per month)

**Participants:** 15–25 adults per workshop, target 80–120 participants total for pilot
