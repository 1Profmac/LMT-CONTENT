### The 5 Narrative Questions

1. **Mission & Vision** — What is your mission and what do you do to support it? (artistic values, audience impact)
2. **Proposed Activity** — What is your activity and how does it reflect Austin's people, places, or cultures? (community rootedness, underrepresented histories)
3. **Experience** — Describe your experience producing creative work or offering public experiences in Austin. (team intro, prior examples)
4. **Budget Narrative** — Explain artist payments, administrative support, and all expenses.
5. **Marketing Plan** — How will you reach Austin residents, visitors, and tourists? (outreach to underserved communities)

## Framing Strategy for 50+TechBridge

**Do NOT write:** "AI training course," "digital literacy curriculum," "workforce development program"

**DO write:** "Public cultural experience," "community gathering," "AI literacy as a form of cultural empowerment for Austin's 50+ community," "open public workshops celebrating the stories and wisdom of older Austinites"

**Anchor the narrative here:**
- Adults 50+ are an underserved community with limited access to the cultural and economic tools of the AI era
- 50+TechBridge creates public spaces where older Austinites can engage with AI on their own terms — reflecting Austin's values of inclusion and creative independence
- Each cohort is a community gathering, not a classroom — built around storytelling, practical tools, and shared experience
- Austin's older adult population represents a living cultural history of the city — this program helps them stay connected, visible, and empowered
