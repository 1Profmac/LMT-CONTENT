# BYLAWS OF LEARN MORE TECHNOLOGIES
## (Doing Business As 50+TechBridge)
### A Texas Nonprofit Corporation

---

## ARTICLE I — NAME AND PURPOSE

### Section 1.1 — Name
The name of this corporation is Learn More Technologies, doing business as 50+TechBridge (hereinafter "the Organization").

### Section 1.2 — Purpose
The Organization is organized exclusively for charitable and educational purposes within the meaning of Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended. Specifically, the Organization exists to close the digital divide for adults aged 50 and older by providing free, hands-on artificial intelligence and digital skills training through public libraries, senior centers, and community-based organizations.

### Section 1.3 — Limitations
No part of the net earnings of the Organization shall inure to the benefit of any director, officer, or private individual, except that the Organization shall be authorized to pay reasonable compensation for services rendered. No substantial part of the activities of the Organization shall be the carrying on of propaganda or otherwise attempting to influence legislation, and the Organization shall not participate in any political campaign on behalf of or in opposition to any candidate for public office.

---

## ARTICLE II — BOARD OF DIRECTORS

### Section 2.1 — General Powers
The affairs of the Organization shall be managed by its Board of Directors.

### Section 2.2 — Number
The Board of Directors shall consist of no fewer than three (3) and no more than seven (7) directors.

### Section 2.3 — Qualifications
Directors must be at least 18 years of age. No two directors may be related by blood or marriage.

### Section 2.4 — Term of Office
Each director shall serve a term of two (2) years and may be re-elected for consecutive terms without limitation.

### Section 2.5 — Regular Meetings
The Board shall hold at least two (2) regular meetings per year. Meetings may be held in person or by video/telephone conference.

### Section 2.6 — Special Meetings
Special meetings may be called by the President or by any two directors with at least five (5) days' written notice.

### Section 2.7 — Quorum
A majority of the directors then in office shall constitute a quorum for the transaction of business.

### Section 2.8 — Action Without Meeting
Any action required or permitted to be taken at a meeting of the Board may be taken without a meeting if all directors consent in writing (including email) to the action.

### Section 2.9 — Vacancies
Vacancies on the Board shall be filled by a majority vote of the remaining directors.

### Section 2.10 — Removal
A director may be removed with or without cause by a two-thirds vote of the other directors.

### Section 2.11 — Compensation
Directors shall not receive compensation for their service as directors but may be reimbursed for reasonable expenses incurred in the performance of their duties.

---

## ARTICLE III — OFFICERS

### Section 3.1 — Officers
The officers of the Organization shall be a President, a Secretary, and a Treasurer. One person may hold more than one office, except that the President may not also serve as Secretary.

### Section 3.2 — President
The President shall be the chief executive officer of the Organization, shall preside at all meetings of the Board, and shall have general supervision of the affairs of the Organization.

### Section 3.3 — Secretary
The Secretary shall keep minutes of all Board meetings, maintain corporate records, and handle correspondence as directed by the Board.

### Section 3.4 — Treasurer
The Treasurer shall have custody of all funds and financial records, shall keep full and accurate accounts of receipts and disbursements, and shall present financial reports to the Board at each regular meeting.

### Section 3.5 — Election and Term
Officers shall be elected by the Board of Directors at the annual meeting and shall serve for a term of two (2) years.

---

## ARTICLE IV — FISCAL YEAR

The fiscal year of the Organization shall begin on January 1 and end on December 31 of each year.

---

## ARTICLE V — CONFLICT OF INTEREST

The Organization shall maintain a Conflict of Interest Policy (attached separately) that applies to all directors, officers, and key employees.

---

## ARTICLE VI — INDEMNIFICATION

The Organization shall indemnify any director, officer, or former director or officer to the fullest extent permitted by the Texas Business Organizations Code.

---

## ARTICLE VII — DISSOLUTION

Upon dissolution of the Organization, all remaining assets shall be distributed to one or more organizations organized and operated exclusively for charitable or educational purposes and qualified as exempt under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code. No assets shall be distributed to any director, officer, or private individual.

---

## ARTICLE VIII — AMENDMENTS

These Bylaws may be amended by a two-thirds vote of the Board of Directors at any regular or special meeting, provided that notice of the proposed amendment has been given to all directors at least ten (10) days prior to the meeting.
