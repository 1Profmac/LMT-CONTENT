50+TechBridge is a cohort-based AI and digital arts program
designed for adults 50 and older. We don't teach technology for
technology's sake — we teach it so that older adults can create original work,
exhibit it publicly, and stand next to it on a gallery wall.

Our participants are digital artists. Every person who comes through a
50+TechBridge cohort uses AI tools to produce an original piece of artwork —
a visual piece, a digital story, a community narrative — and then presents
that work at a public AI Art Gallery Walk, held at Austin's cultural institutions
and open to residents and tourists alike. The final product of every cohort is
not a test score. It is original art, displayed publicly, created by an adult
50+ who is contributing to Austin's creative ecosystem.

This matters because Austin's 50+ population is largely invisible in the city's
creative community. These residents carry deep cultural knowledge, lived
experience, and authentic Austin stories. They are ready to create. They have
simply never had access to the tools or the on-ramp. 50+TechBridge is that
on-ramp.

We deliver Digital Pioneer gatherings across 9 in-person Central Texas locations —
6 Austin Public Library branches (St. John, Rundberg, Southeast, Milwood,
Manchaca Road, Pleasant Hill), AARP-affiliated senior centers, and a Foundation
Communities veterans program. Our program is structured around 5 cohorts per grant year, each
organized by artistic discipline: Digital Storytelling, Digital Visual Art,
Community Media, and Creative Technology. Each cohort culminates in a public
Gallery Walk exhibition at a rotating Austin cultural venue.

We received a prior ACME Nexus Grant (FY24, $5,000) that launched this library-
based series. All reporting was completed and the contract is closed. The Elevate
Grant expands what that investment built: more cohorts, more exhibitions, more
guest artists, deeper mentorship — and 50+ digital artists on Austin's gallery
walls every quarter.

Adults 50+ are not a niche. They are Austin's most experienced, most rooted
creative community. 50+TechBridge puts AI tools in their hands and puts their
work in front of the city.
