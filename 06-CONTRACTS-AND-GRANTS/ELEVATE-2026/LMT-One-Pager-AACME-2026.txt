WHO WE ARE

Learn More Technologies (LMT) delivers structured AI and digital skills training exclusively to adults age 50 and older — with a focused commitment to Black and Hispanic communities most impacted by digital exclusion in Austin.

We go to participants. They do not come to us.

Our Technology Assisted Trainings are delivered free of charge inside Austin Public Library branches, senior centers, and faith community sites. Participants leave with practical AI skills — and work they created and are proud of.

================================================================================

THE GAP WE FILL

No current AACME-funded program serves Black and Hispanic adults 50+ at the intersection of arts and technology.

- 60,000-70,000 Hispanic adults 50+ in Austin — only 58% have home broadband
- 18,000-22,000 Black adults 50+ in Austin — only 62% have home broadband
  (U.S. Census ACS 2023 | Pew Research 2023-2024)

Existing programs teach email. We teach AI — as a creative tool for expression, storytelling, and community participation.
