## ABOUT

Learn More Technologies operates 50+TechBridge — Digital Pioneer gatherings that teach AI skills to adults 50+. We deliver free, hands-on sessions at public libraries, senior centers, and community organizations across Central Texas.

Founded and led by Brian McKinney — a 65-year-old solo CEO who manages 30+ AI-powered workflows — 50+TechBridge proves that age is not a barrier to innovation. While other programs teach basic email and internet safety, we teach AI: practical, project-based skills that help older adults stay independent, connected, and competitive.
