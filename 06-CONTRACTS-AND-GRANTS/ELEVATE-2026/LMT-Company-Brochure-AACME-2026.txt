PAGE 1 — ORGANIZATIONAL OVERVIEW
================================================================================

Learn More Technologies (LMT), doing business as 50+TechBridge, is an Austin, Texas-based workforce development and AI education organization dedicated to closing the digital skills gap for adults age 50 and older — with a focused commitment to Black and Hispanic communities most impacted by digital exclusion.

LMT operates 50+TechBridge, delivering structured, AI-focused Digital Pioneer gatherings for adults 50+, using a creative output model that positions technology as a tool for artistic expression, storytelling, and community participation — not merely employment preparation.

Our Technology Assisted Trainings are embedded inside the community institutions participants already trust: Austin Public Library branches, senior centers, and faith community sites in East Austin. We go to participants. They do not come to us.

PAGE 2 — THE PROBLEM & OUR RESPONSE
================================================================================

The Equity Gap AACME Has Not Yet Filled

No current AACME-funded program serves Black and Hispanic adults 50+ at the intersection of arts and technology. This is the gap LMT was built to fill.

Austin's 50+ population includes an estimated 60,000-70,000 Hispanic adults and 18,000-22,000 Black adults (U.S. Census ACS 2023). These communities face compounding barriers to digital participation:

- Hispanic adults 50+: only 58% have home broadband access vs. 82% of white adults
- Black adults 50+: only 62% have home broadband access vs. 82% of white adults
(Pew Research Center, 2023-2024)

Existing digital literacy programs address email and basic internet use. No program in Austin is teaching this population AI tools — the technology now reshaping employment, independent living, healthcare navigation, and creative production across every sector.

LMT's Response

LMT does not teach email. We teach AI — and we frame it as a creative tool.

Our program model treats AI as a medium for expression: participants use AI tools to write, design, tell their stories, and produce work they are proud of. The program culminates in a Community Showcase — a public arts event where 50+ participants present the work they created using AI during the program year.

This is arts programming. It is also workforce development. It is also digital equity. LMT is the only organization in Austin doing all three — for the populations who need it most.

50+TechBridge: How It Works

Delivery Model:
Technology Assisted Trainings are delivered free of charge, in-person, at community sites. All materials, instruction, and technology access are provided at no cost to participants. Spanish-language sessions are available at select sites, with bilingual delivery and translated materials.

Current Footprint (Verified, Central Texas):

  Site Type                              Active Locations
  Austin Public Library Branches         5-7
  Senior Centers                         3
  Veterans Programs                      1
  Faith Community Sites (target)         1-2

Participants Served to Date: ~100 adults 50+
Documentation: Signed attendance sheets + session photographs

Grant-Year Outcome Targets ($25,500 Ask):

  Outcome                                          Target
  Adults 50+ served                                150
  Active delivery sites                            10-12
  Training cohorts completed                       20-24
  Participants completing full track               100 (65%)
  Participants producing creative output           75
  Community Showcase attendees                     60+
  Spanish-language sessions delivered              10
  Faith community sites added                      1-2
  Participant satisfaction rate                    85%+

Community Showcase:
The grant year concludes with a public Community Showcase — an arts event featuring participant-created digital work produced using AI tools during the program. The Showcase serves as both a public celebration of participant achievement and a demonstration of what becomes possible when older adults are given access to emerging creative technologies.
