## SECTION 2 — WIOA REGISTRATION: MASSACHUSETTS

### MA TrainingPro — WIOA Eligible Training Provider List
**What it does:** Once approved, Boston-area adults can use Individual Training Accounts (ITAs) to pay for LMT courses. No grant needed — participants bring their own WIOA funding.

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Website** | mass.gov/how-to/how-to-register-on-trainingpro |
| **System name** | TrainingPro |
| **Who approves** | MA Executive Office of Labor and Workforce Development (EOLWD) |
| **Local contact** | MassHire Greater Lowell Workforce Board — masshiregreaterlowellwb.com |
| **Phone** | 978-454-0000 (MassHire Greater Lowell) |
| **Covers** | Ayer, MA and all Middlesex County |

#### Step-by-Step Registration
1. Go to mass.gov/how-to/how-to-register-on-trainingpro
2. Create an account as a training provider organization
3. Submit Course 1 first (AI Literacy for Adults 50+ — Foundation, 9 hours)
4. **Call MassHire Greater Lowell first** — ask about non-traditional provider approval pathway (fastest route for digital literacy providers)
5. Submit curriculum outline for each course
6. Attach: Brian McKinney bio/credentials, org overview, course descriptions

#### Documents Required
- [ ] EIN (have it)
- [ ] SAM.gov registration confirmation (have it)
- [ ] Course descriptions (written — see `BOSTON-OPPORTUNITIES/07-WIOA-TRAININGPRO-REGISTRATION.md`)
- [ ] Facilitator credentials / bio (Brian McKinney)
- [ ] Accreditation OR Workforce Board approval letter
- [ ] Program cost per participant (for ITA pricing)
- [ ] Curriculum outline (learning objectives, hours, outcomes)
- [ ] Certificate of completion description

#### Accreditation Requirement (the main blocker)
TrainingPro requires ONE of the following:
- MA Dept of Elementary and Secondary Education (DESE) license
- MA Board of Higher Education approval
- NEASC accreditation
- **OR: Local Workforce Development Board approval** ← fastest path for LMT

**Recommended path:** Call MassHire Greater Lowell → explain you are a digital literacy / AI literacy provider for adults 50+ → ask about the non-traditional provider approval pathway → get a letter of endorsement from WDB → submit to TrainingPro with that letter.

#### ITA Funding Potential
- Typical ITA award: $2,000–$10,000 per eligible participant
- LMT courses: 9–12 hours each
- At 15 participants per cohort: $30,000–$150,000 per cohort in ITA funding
- No grant writing required — participants bring their own approved funding

## SECTION 3 — WIOA REGISTRATION: TEXAS

### TWC ETPL — Eligible Training Provider List
**What it does:** LMT courses become eligible for WIOA-funded ITAs through Texas Workforce Commission. Adults use workforce funds to pay for courses directly.

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Website** | twc.texas.gov/partners/eligible-training-providers |
| **System name** | ETPL (Eligible Training Provider List) |
| **Who approves** | Texas Workforce Commission (TWC) |
| **Local contact** | Workforce Solutions Capital Area — workforcesolutionscapitalarea.com |
| **Phone** | 512-597-7100 (Workforce Solutions Capital Area) |
| **Email** | info@wfscapitalarea.com |
| **Covers** | Austin / Travis County and surrounding area |

#### Step-by-Step Registration
1. Go to twc.texas.gov/partners/eligible-training-providers
2. Review TWC ETPL requirements for new providers
3. **Call Workforce Solutions Capital Area first** — ask to speak with training provider services
4. Complete TWC online provider application (requires TWC portal account)
5. Submit each course separately for ETPL listing
6. Await approval (typically 60–90 days)

#### Documents Required
- [ ] EIN (have it)
- [ ] SAM.gov registration (have it)
- [ ] Business registration in Texas (LLC — have it)
- [ ] Course descriptions with learning objectives
- [ ] Curriculum outline (hours, sessions, outcomes)
- [ ] Completion credential description (Digital Pioneer Certificate)
- [ ] Employment / wage outcome plan (how does training lead to employment or wage gain?)
- [ ] Facilitator qualifications
- [ ] Cost per participant (required for ITA pricing)
- [ ] Performance data — completion rates, employment outcomes (use Austin library data)

#### Key Difference from MA: Outcomes Required
Texas TWC requires **employment or wage outcome data** — they want to see that training leads to jobs or wage increases. Frame the AI Workforce Re-entry track (Course 3) as the primary ETPL submission since it directly aligns with workforce re-entry outcomes for adults 50+.

#### TWC Accreditation Note
Texas accepts:
- SACSCOC (Southern Association of Colleges and Schools)
- THECB (Texas Higher Education Coordinating Board)
- TWC direct provider approval ← available for non-accredited providers
- Industry-recognized certifications as supporting evidence

**Recommended path:** Call Workforce Solutions Capital Area → describe the workforce re-entry track → ask about TWC direct provider approval pathway → they can sponsor your ETPL application locally.
