# Elevate Grant 2026 — Application Checklist

**Application Opens:** July 7, 2026
**Deadline:** TBD (check https://www.austintexas.gov/arts-culture/elevate)
**Award:** Up to $30,000–$40,000 (individual artists / arts groups)
**Fiscal Sponsor:** NOT required (optional)

---

## Eligibility Confirmation
- [ ] At least 2 years of creative production history in Austin
- [ ] Headquartered in Austin MSA
- [ ] Over 50% of creative output within Austin Council Districts
- [ ] Annual operating budget under $500,000
- [ ] Activities are publicly accessible

## Application Materials
- [ ] Narrative Question 1 — Mission & artistic vision (see 02-NARRATIVES.md)
- [ ] Narrative Question 2 — Project description & activities
- [ ] Narrative Question 3 — Community impact & audience
- [ ] Narrative Question 4 — Organizational capacity
- [ ] Narrative Question 5 — Equity & inclusion
- [ ] Budget table with expense categories (see 03-BUDGET.md)
- [ ] Budget narrative

## Supporting Materials
- [ ] Bio / résumé (already have — see 04-BIO.md)
- [ ] Work samples — photos, videos, flyers from past events
- [ ] Letters of support from partner organizations
- [ ] Proof of 2+ years Austin-based creative production

## Before You Submit
- [ ] All narratives under 3,000 characters each
- [ ] Budget matches narrative (numbers align)
- [ ] Verified stats only (see 05-PROOF-POINTS.md)
- [ ] Have someone proofread
