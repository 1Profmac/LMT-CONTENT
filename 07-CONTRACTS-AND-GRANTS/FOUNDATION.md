## 1. ONE-LINE DESCRIPTION
50+TechBridge is an Austin-based community program that brings AI literacy to adults 50+ through free in-person gatherings and ongoing online community — hosted at public libraries and senior centers across the city.

---

## 2. WHO WE ARE (SHORT — ~300 words)
LearnMoreTechnologies.com operates 50+TechBridge — an Austin-based community development program that brings AI literacy to adults 50+ through a structured blend of in-person public gatherings and ongoing online community. We are not a class. We are not a course. We are a cohort — a community of Austinites who learn together, grow together, and stay connected long after the first session ends.

Our founder, Brian McKinney, has spent his career at the intersection of technology, community, and adult learning. After watching the rise of AI leave an entire generation behind — not for lack of intelligence, but for lack of access and invitation — he built 50+TechBridge to change that.

Since launching in Austin, we have reached 264+ adults across 13 community locations, including 9 public libraries and 3 senior centers. Every session is free. Every door is open. Every Austinite 50+ is welcome.

---

## 3. MISSION & VISION (~200 words)
50+TechBridge exists to close the gap between Austin's older adults and the AI-driven world they live in — not by pushing technology on people, but by building community around it.

Our vision is a city where age is not a barrier to creative and cultural participation. Artificial intelligence is the defining creative medium of this era — it is reshaping how art is made, how stories are told, how people connect, and how communities protect themselves. If older Austinites cannot access or understand these tools, they are effectively excluded from the cultural life of the city they built.

Our values are accessibility, authenticity, and dignity. We meet every person at their own level. We honor the wisdom they already carry.

## 7. PROGRAM MODEL — SUMMARY
Each 50+TechBridge cohort is a six-week community experience built on three layers:

- **Layer 1 — In-Person Gathering:** A 2-hour free public event at an Austin library or senior center. Walk-ins welcome. No device or prior experience required.
- **Layer 2 — Weekly Online Mastermind:** Six weeks of Tuesday or Thursday evening gatherings where cohort members reconnect, share, and go deeper together.
- **Layer 3 — Continuing Education + SecureStart.app:** Self-paced online content plus free lifetime access to SecureStart.app — our AI-powered digital safety platform built for adults 50+.

## 9. THE DIGITAL PIONEER GALLERY
*Source: Robin's revisions — July 2026*

**Taglines:**
- The Digital Pioneer Gallery
- Where art meets technology.
- Art has no age.

**What the Gallery Is:**
The Digital Pioneer Gallery is a public exhibition where participants display completed AI-assisted artwork created during the program. The exhibit is open to the public. Attendees are invited to view the work and participate in a silent auction. Proceeds from the auction go directly to the individual artist who created the piece.

The Gallery is hosted in partnership with Austin's cultural institutions — not libraries. Libraries are where we meet, greet, and train. The Gallery is where participants are celebrated as artists.

**Venue Rule:**
Austin Public Libraries = meet, greet, and train only.

## 10. PROGRAM STRUCTURE — 8-WEEK DIGITAL PIONEER COHORT
*Source: Robin's revisions — July 2026*

**Week 1**
Introduction to the Digital Pioneer program. Participants meet their cohort, learn what's ahead, and take their first step into AI-assisted creativity.

**Weeks 2–3: Learn Your Tools**
Hands-on training with the AI tools you'll use throughout the program. You'll also meet the people who will support you — a one-on-one volunteer mentor assigned to walk alongside you from here forward.

**Weeks 4–6: Create**
You make something. With your mentor's support and your cohort beside you, you move from learning the tools to using them with intention — building the piece you'll one day display.

**Week 7**
Refinement. Participants finalize their work, prepare for the public exhibition, and reflect on what they've built.

**Week 8: You Did It.**
Your completed design will be displayed at one of Austin's cultural venues as part of the Digital Pioneer Gallery — a public exhibition open to the community. A silent auction gives attendees the opportunity to purchase your work, with all proceeds going directly to you, the artist.

## 11. AGE ELIGIBILITY & PARTICIPATION

### Primary Audience
50+TechBridge is designed for adults age 50 and older. All core programming, curriculum, and services are tailored to meet the unique needs, goals, and lived experiences of older adults navigating technology and digital independence.

### Intergenerational Participation
We also welcome individuals age 20 and older who are actively pursuing education or careers in fields that serve older adults — including senior care, healthcare, mental health, gerontology, social work, and independent living services — to participate as support staff and interns.

Through our partnership with UT geriatric programs and aligned academic institutions, students and young professionals gain supervised, hands-on experience working directly with the 50+ community. This intergenerational model creates meaningful outcomes for all participants:

- **Older adults** receive additional peer engagement and personalized support
- **Students and interns** build professional skills, field experience, and industry connections in a real-world service environment
- **Participating institutions** may offer CSU or program credit for qualified internship hours

### Building the Next Generation of Senior-Serving Professionals
We are actively developing partnerships with universities, workforce programs, and professional networks to connect young professionals who are passionate about working with older adults to opportunities that accelerate their career growth — while strengthening the communities they serve.
