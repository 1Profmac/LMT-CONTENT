## What to Say When You Ask

> "I'm incorporating 50+TechBridge as a nonprofit so we can apply for grants and grow the program. I'm looking for 2–3 board members — it's a light commitment (2–4 meetings a year, virtual is fine). You already know the work. Would you be open to serving on the board?"

## Board Member Responsibilities (Keep It Simple)
- Attend 2–4 meetings per year
- Review and approve annual budget
- Support the mission (not required to fundraise)
- No personal financial liability (covered by bylaws and D&O insurance)
