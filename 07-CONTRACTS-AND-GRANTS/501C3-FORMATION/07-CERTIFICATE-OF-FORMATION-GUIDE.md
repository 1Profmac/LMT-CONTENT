# Certificate of Formation — Texas Filing Guide

**File online:** https://www.sos.state.tx.us/corp/forms_702.shtml
**Cost:** $25
**Timeline:** 2 days – 2 weeks

---

### Organization Name
**Learn More Technologies** (or "50+TechBridge Inc." — check availability first)
- Search name availability: https://mycpa.cpa.state.tx.us/coa/

### Type of Entity
Nonprofit Corporation (Form 202)

"The corporation is organized exclusively for charitable and educational purposes within the meaning of Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended, including but not limited to providing free artificial intelligence and digital skills training to adults aged 50 and older through public libraries, senior centers, and community-based organizations."

### Directors (Minimum 3)

### Organizer
- Brian McKinney — [Address]

### Required IRS Language (Include in Articles)

**Dissolution Clause:**
"Upon the dissolution of the corporation, assets shall be distributed for one or more exempt purposes within the meaning of Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code, or the corresponding section of any future federal tax code, or shall be distributed to the federal government, or to a state or local government, for a public purpose."

**Inurement Clause:**
"No part of the net earnings of the corporation shall inure to the benefit of, or be distributable to its members, trustees, officers, or other private persons, except that the corporation shall be authorized and empowered to pay reasonable compensation for services rendered."

---

## Steps to File Online

1. Go to https://www.sos.state.tx.us/corp/sosda/index.shtml
2. Select "Nonprofit Corporation" → Form 202
3. Fill in all fields using the info above
4. Pay $25 by credit card
5. Save your confirmation/filing number
6. Wait for stamped Certificate of Formation (email or mail)

---

## After Filing
- [ ] Receive stamped Certificate of Formation
- [ ] Apply for EIN immediately (irs.gov — free, instant)
- [ ] Then file Form 1023-EZ ($275)
