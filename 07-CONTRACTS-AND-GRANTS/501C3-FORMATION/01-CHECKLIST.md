# 501(c)(3) Formation Checklist — Learn More Technologies / 50+TechBridge

**Goal:** Obtain federal tax-exempt nonprofit status
**Estimated Cost:** ~$300
**Estimated Timeline:** 3–6 months from filing

---

## Phase 1 — This Week (June 13–20, 2026)

- [ ] Identify 2 board members (you are the 3rd) — see 02-BOARD-WORKSHEET.md
- [ ] Choose official nonprofit name (likely: "Learn More Technologies" or "50+TechBridge Inc.")
- [ ] Review and finalize mission statement (see 03-MISSION-STATEMENT.md)
- [ ] Review bylaws draft (see 04-BYLAWS.md)
- [ ] Review conflict of interest policy (see 05-CONFLICT-OF-INTEREST.md)

## Phase 2 — File with Texas (Week of June 20)

- [ ] File Certificate of Formation online with Texas Secretary of State — $25
  - https://www.sos.state.tx.us/corp/forms_702.shtml
- [ ] Receive stamped Certificate of Formation back (2 days – 2 weeks)

## Phase 3 — Federal (Once Texas approves)

- [ ] Apply for EIN at irs.gov (free, instant)
  - https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/apply-for-an-employer-identification-number-ein-online
- [ ] File IRS Form 1023-EZ — $275
  - https://www.pay.gov (search "1023-EZ")
  - Requires: EIN, mission statement, bylaws, conflict of interest policy
- [ ] Wait for IRS determination letter (2–6 months)

## Phase 4 — Texas Registration

- [ ] Register with Texas Attorney General for charitable solicitation (free)
  - https://www.texasattorneygeneral.gov/divisions/charitable-trusts/registration-and-filings
- [ ] Apply for Texas state tax exemption with Comptroller (free)
  - https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/exempt/

## Phase 5 — After Approval

- [ ] Update website and materials with nonprofit status
- [ ] Open nonprofit bank account
- [ ] Set up donation processing (Zeffy = free for nonprofits)
- [ ] Apply for grants that require 501(c)(3)!

---

- [ ] Research Austin fiscal sponsors (see 06-FISCAL-SPONSORS.md)
