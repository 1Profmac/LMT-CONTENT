## ORGANIZATION OVERVIEW

Learn More Technologies operates 50+TechBridge — the only program in America teaching AI skills specifically to adults 50+. We deliver free, hands-on AI and digital literacy workshops inside trusted community spaces: senior centers, public libraries, and neighborhood organizations.

Every session is:
- **Free** — zero cost to participants
- **Hands-on** — participants build and do, not just watch
- **Customized** — tailored to each partner community's specific needs and culture
- **Accessible** — no prior tech experience required

**Founded and led by Brian McKinney**, a 65-year-old solo CEO who runs his organization on 30+ AI-powered workflows. He teaches what he lives.

### Project Description

50+TechBridge will deliver a series of free AI literacy workshops inside Age Strong partner sites and BCYF senior centers across Boston's neighborhoods. Each workshop is a social engagement event as much as a learning experience — participants work together, help each other, and leave with a shared identity: *Digital Pioneer.*

The program addresses two overlapping challenges among older Bostonians:
1. **Social isolation** — workshops are community gatherings, not one-on-one training
2. **Digital exclusion** — AI is reshaping every aspect of daily life; older adults are being left behind

---

### What Participants Will Do

Every workshop is hands-on and project-based:

| Session | What Participants Build |
|---|---|
| Session 1: AI Basics | Use an AI assistant to answer a real question from their life |
| Session 2: Staying Safe Online | Spot fake content, create a personal safety checklist |
| Session 3: AI for Health & Independence | Use AI to research medications, find services, draft doctor questions |
| Session 4: AI for Communication | Use voice-to-text, translate, write messages to family |
| Session 5: AI for Fun & Creativity | Create a digital story, photo project, or memory book |
| Session 6: Graduate Showcase | Share what they built with family, friends, and the community |

---

### The Digital Pioneer Identity

Every participant earns the "Digital Pioneer" identity — a framing that repositions them from *confused by technology* to *leading the way.* This is not a participation trophy. It is a confidence-building tool embedded into every session, story, and interaction.

---

### Community Reach

**Target:** 4 Boston neighborhoods, 2 workshops per neighborhood, 15–25 participants each = **120–200 Boston adults**

Sites to be confirmed with Age Strong staff — preference for neighborhoods with highest 50+ population and lowest current tech training access.
