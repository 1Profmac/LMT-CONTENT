## Step 2: Create ONE New Property — Google Business Profile

This is the only new account you need to create. It is free, takes 10 minutes, and is what shows up when anyone Googles "Digital Pioneer Gallery Austin."

**Why this one matters:**
- Grant reviewers Google programs before reviewing applications
- Library partners search Google to find you
- Applicants search "art programs Austin 50+" and you appear
- It verifies your Austin, TX presence for the HOT funding requirement

**Setup steps:**
1. Go to **business.google.com**
2. Sign in with your Google/Gmail business account
3. Business name: **Digital Pioneer Gallery**
4. Category: **Art Gallery** (primary) · Add **Community Center** as secondary
5. Location type: *I serve customers at their location* (no storefront)
6. Service area: Austin, TX — Travis County
7. Phone: your business number
8. Website: `digitalpioneer.ai`
9. Add photos: Pioneers Gallery image + any cohort photos
10. Write the description below

**Google Business Description (750 character limit):**
```
The Digital Pioneer Gallery is Austin's public gallery program for adults 50+. Free bi-weekly in-person sessions at Austin Public Library branches and live online training teach digital creative tools and portfolio development. Every cohort ends with a public gallery opening — participant artwork displayed at The Carver Museum, Austin Central Library, and partner venues. No tech experience required. All creative disciplines welcome. Apply free at digitalpioneer.ai. A program of 50+TechBridge by Learn More Technologies. Funded by City of Austin ACME Elevate.
```

## Posting Checklist — First 6 Posts to Create

These 6 posts establish your Digital Pioneer Gallery presence across all platforms before the grant deadline:

**Post 1 — Program Announcement**
Image: Pioneers Gallery photo
Caption: Introduce the program, what it is, who it's for, how to apply. Link: digitalpioneer.ai

**Post 2 — "What is a Digital Pioneer?"**
Image: Brian or cohort in session
Caption: Define the Digital Pioneer identity. Who are these artists. What makes them different.

**Post 3 — Library Partner Highlight**
Image: Austin Public Library exterior or session photo
Caption: Tag @AustinPublicLibrary. Explain the partnership. Show the in-person component is real.

**Post 4 — "Start Where You Are"**
Image: Close-up of hands on tablet, sketchbook, or creative tool
Caption: Address the fear of starting. All disciplines welcome. No tech experience needed.

**Post 5 — Gallery Walk Teaser**
Image: Carver Museum exterior or gallery wall
Caption: Where your work ends up. The destination. The public stage. The opening night.

**Post 6 — Apply Now**
Image: Application form screenshot or program graphic
Caption: Applications open. Free. Link in bio. Deadline information.
