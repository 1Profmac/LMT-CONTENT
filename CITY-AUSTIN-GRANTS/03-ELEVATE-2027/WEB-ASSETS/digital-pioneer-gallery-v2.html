About the Program

Artists don't have
age limits.

The Digital Pioneer Gallery is a cohort-based digital arts program by 50+TechBridge that puts AI tools in the hands of adults 50+ — and puts their finished work on Austin's gallery walls.

Each participant creates an original piece of AI-assisted digital artwork, builds an artist statement, and exhibits their work at a public Gallery Walk held at Austin's premier cultural institutions.

This is not a classroom exercise. It is a gallery exhibition — a public creative act, open to Austin residents and tourists alike.

The Creative Process

From intake to gallery wall

Every cohort follows a structured 8-week arc — from creative orientation to public exhibition. Here's exactly what participants experience.

1
Week 1

Cohort Intake & Artistic Orientation

Identify your artistic discipline and creative goal. Meet your guest instructor and volunteer mentor. Confirm the venue for your Gallery Walk.

2
Weeks 2–3

Creative Technology Training

Hands-on training in the AI tools you'll use to create your artwork — tailored to your artistic discipline. One-on-one mentor support at every session.

  • Digital storytellers: AI writing and narrative tools
  • Visual artists: AI image generation and design tools
  • Community media artists: AI content and publishing tools
3
Weeks 4–6

Artwork Development Workshop

Create your individual artwork with structured guidance and peer input. Weekly mentor check-ins, mid-project cohort critique, and revision support until your work is gallery-ready.

4
Week 7

Marketing, Presentation & Exhibition Prep

Learn to market, present, and sell your creative work. Write your artist statement. Practice pricing digital work, licensing AI-assisted art, building an online portfolio, and promoting your work on social media.

5
Week 8

Public AI Art Gallery Walk

Your finished work is displayed at an Austin cultural venue. You are present as the artist. The exhibition is open to the community and promoted through VisitAustin.

This is the moment. Not a classroom exercise — a real gallery exhibition, open to Austin residents and tourists. Your name. Your work. Your wall.

Public Benefit & Tourism Impact

Austin's creative community
gets stronger

The Digital Pioneer Gallery directly supports Austin's cultural tourism economy. Every gallery walk is a free, public event at an Austin cultural institution — attracting visitors already engaged in Austin's arts scene.

Exhibitions are submitted to VisitAustin after each Gallery Walk. Participant artwork and artist statements are archived and available for future exhibition.
