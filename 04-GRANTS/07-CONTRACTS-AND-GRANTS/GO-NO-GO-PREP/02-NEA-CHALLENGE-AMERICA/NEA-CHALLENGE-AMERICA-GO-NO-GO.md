## Framing Strategy (For When You Apply)

**Open every narrative answer with this:**

> *"We see all people. Even the ones others ignore."*

That is not a slogan. It is how we operate. When the technology world decided that AI was for the young, the connected, and the already-powerful — we went to the library. We pulled up a chair next to the person who never thought technology was for them. And we stayed.

50+TechBridge was founded on a simple belief: everyone deserves a seat at the table — especially those the technology world has chosen to leave behind. We go where others don't, serve who others won't, and stay long after the session ends.

---

50+ adults qualify as underserved on multiple grounds: economic status, limited arts access, geographic isolation (rural), and age-related exclusion from digital culture.

**Frame AI literacy as arts education:**
- AI tools are the new creative medium — older adults deserve access to them
- Each workshop is a public arts experience rooted in community storytelling and cultural connection
- The work of Brian McKinney is artistic facilitation, not classroom instruction
