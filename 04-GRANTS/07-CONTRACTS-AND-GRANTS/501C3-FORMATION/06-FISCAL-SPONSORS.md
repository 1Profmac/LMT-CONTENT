# Fiscal Sponsor Options — Austin, TX

Use a fiscal sponsor as a BRIDGE while your 501(c)(3) is pending.
A fiscal sponsor lets you apply for grants that require nonprofit status NOW.

---

## What a Fiscal Sponsor Does
- Receives grant funds on your behalf
- Provides tax-exempt status for your project
- Handles financial administration
- Takes a fee (typically 5–10% of funds received)

## Austin-Area Fiscal Sponsors to Research

### 1. Austin Creative Alliance
- Supports Austin arts and creative community
- May sponsor cultural/educational projects
- https://www.austincreativealliance.org

### 2. Community Initiatives (National)
- One of the largest fiscal sponsors in the country
- Accepts projects nationwide
- Fee: typically 7–9%
- https://www.communityinitiatives.org

### 3. Fractured Atlas (National — Arts Focus)
- Popular with individual artists and small arts orgs
- Fee: 5% of revenue
- Fast application process
- https://www.fracturedatlas.org

### 4. Social Good Fund (National)
- Focuses on social justice and community projects
- Fee: 7%
- https://www.socialgoodfund.org

### 5. Create Austin / AACME Partners
- Check if any Austin arts orgs offer fiscal sponsorship
- Ask at Elevate info sessions when they're announced

---

## What to Ask a Fiscal Sponsor
1. What is your fee percentage?
2. How quickly can I be approved?
3. Can I apply for government grants through you?
4. What reporting do you require?
5. Can I transition out once my own 501(c)(3) is approved?
