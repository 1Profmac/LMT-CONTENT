# Mission Statement — 50+TechBridge (Learn More Technologies)

Use ONE of these for the IRS application. The IRS wants it clear, charitable, and educational.

---

## Recommended (IRS-Ready)

**Learn More Technologies, doing business as 50+TechBridge, is organized exclusively for charitable and educational purposes within the meaning of Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code. The organization's mission is to close the digital divide for adults aged 50 and older by providing free, hands-on artificial intelligence and digital skills training through public libraries, senior centers, and community-based organizations. The organization serves historically underserved populations — including older adults, veterans, and low-income individuals — by delivering accessible, jargon-free technology education that promotes digital equity, lifelong learning, and economic participation.**

---

## Short Version (For Website / Marketing)

50+TechBridge closes the digital divide for adults 50+ by providing free, hands-on AI and digital skills training across libraries, senior centers, and community organizations in Central Texas.
