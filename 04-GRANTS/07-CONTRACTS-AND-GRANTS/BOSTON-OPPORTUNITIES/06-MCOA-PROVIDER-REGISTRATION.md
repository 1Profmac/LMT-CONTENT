### 2. Program Description (Use This)

> 50+TechBridge delivers free, hands-on AI and digital literacy workshops specifically for adults 50+. We deploy inside trusted community spaces — senior centers, libraries, and community organizations — and customize every session to the host community's participants. No tech experience required. No cost to participants. Our curriculum is project-based: participants build and do, not just watch. Our founder is a 65-year-old solo CEO who teaches what he lives.

### 3. Services Offered
- In-person AI literacy workshops (60–90 min)
- Customized curriculum per COA/senior center
- Printed takeaway materials (plain language, large print available)
- Follow-up online course access (LearnDash, self-paced)
- Train-the-trainer sessions available for COA staff

### 4. Geographic Coverage
- Currently serving Greater Boston / Central Massachusetts
- Available statewide — travel to any MA location

### 5. Cost to COA/Senior Center
