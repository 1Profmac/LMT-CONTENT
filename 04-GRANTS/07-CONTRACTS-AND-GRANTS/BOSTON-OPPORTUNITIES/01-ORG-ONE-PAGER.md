## Who We Are

50+TechBridge is the **only program in America teaching AI skills specifically to adults 50+.**

While other programs still teach basic email and internet safety, we teach AI — practical, hands-on skills that help older adults stay independent, connected, and competitive. Our workshops meet people where they already gather: libraries, senior centers, and community organizations.

Brian McKinney, our 65-year-old founder, manages 30+ AI-powered workflows as a solo CEO. He is living proof that age is not a barrier to innovation.
