## WHO WE ARE

50+TechBridge is the only program in America teaching AI skills specifically to adults 50+.

We are not a tech company dropping in for a demo. We are a community organization — founded and led by a 65-year-old founder who teaches what he lives — that deploys inside trusted community spaces and delivers curriculum customized to each location's participants.

**Our track record:**
- 264+ adults trained across 13 locations
- 3 senior centers, 9 library branches, 1 veterans program
- 3x completion rate vs. industry average
- City of Austin Nexus Grant awardee (2024)
- MBE-certified, WIOA-eligible

---

## WHAT WE TEACH

We teach AI in plain English. No jargon. No coding. No prerequisites.

Each workshop is **project-based** — participants build something real in the session and leave with a skill they can use that same day.

**Sample Workshop Menu** *(customized to each BCYF site's community):*

| Workshop | What Participants Do |
|---|---|
| AI Basics: What Is It and Why Should I Care? | Use an AI assistant to answer a real question from their own life |
| Staying Safe in the AI Age | Spot fake content, create a personal safety plan |
| AI for Your Health | Research medications, draft doctor questions, find local services |
| AI for Staying Connected | Voice-to-text, video calls, writing messages to family |
| AI for Fun: Your Story, Your Way | Create a digital memory book, photo story, or audio recording |
| Graduate Showcase | Participants share what they built — community celebration |

## THE DIGITAL PIONEER IDENTITY

Every 50+TechBridge participant becomes a **Digital Pioneer.**

This is more than a name. It's a reframe: from *confused by technology* to *leading the way.* It's embedded in every session — how we open, how we close, how participants talk about what they learned.

BCYF centers build community. We build confidence. Together, participants leave as a cohort — people who learned something hard together and can keep supporting each other.

## WHAT WE NEED FROM BCYF

- **Space:** A room with tables, chairs, and a projector or large screen (60–90 min per session)
- **Promotion:** Inclusion in BCYF program calendars, flyers, and center announcements
- **Staff introduction:** One staff member to introduce 50+TechBridge at the first session

**We bring everything else:** curriculum, printed materials, handouts, follow-up online course access.

---

## CUSTOMIZATION COMMITMENT

Every BCYF site gets a curriculum customized to their community. We will meet with site staff before the first session to understand:
- Who are the participants? (background, interests, tech comfort level)
- What do they need AI to help them with most?
- What language(s) do participants speak?
- What devices do they have or can they bring?

No two sites get the same exact program. That is our differentiator.
