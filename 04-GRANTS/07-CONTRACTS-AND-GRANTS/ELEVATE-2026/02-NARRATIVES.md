## Q1: Mission & Artistic/Creative Vision

50+TechBridge (Learn More Technologies) is the only program in America teaching AI and digital skills specifically to adults 50+. Our mission: close the digital divide for the fastest-growing demographic in the country — not by teaching email, but by teaching AI.

We believe technology training for older adults should be free, hands-on, and delivered where people already gather — libraries, senior centers, and community organizations. We're not preparing people for the future. We're catching them up to right now.

Founded by Brian McKinney — a 65-year-old solo CEO who manages 30+ AI-powered workflows — 50+TechBridge proves that age is not a barrier to innovation. It's a barrier that was built. We're tearing it down.

## Q2: Project Description & Proposed Activities

50+TechBridge delivers free, hands-on AI and digital skills workshops to adults 50+ at the places they already trust — Austin Public Library branches, senior centers, and community partner sites. This is a validated, active program. Since 2023, we have delivered training across 9 locations, including 6 Austin Public Library branches, 2 senior centers, and a veterans program in partnership with Foundation Communities. Participants are documented through signed sign-in sheets and session photography, with additional reach not captured due to the privacy preferences common in the 50+ population. Our content reaches 150+ community members across LinkedIn, YouTube, and Facebook. We have received prior public funding through the City of Austin Nexus Grant ($5,000).

With AACME Elevate support, the 2026 program year will expand to include:
- Free AI workshops at 10-12 active sites across Central Texas
- Spanish-language sessions at faith community sites in East Austin
- A **Digital Pioneer Showcase** — a public community arts event where participants present and celebrate what they've built using AI tools
- Online course access via LearnDash for participants unable to attend in person
- 150 adults 50+ served

## Q5: Equity & Inclusion

Ageism is the last socially acceptable form of discrimination. The tech industry spends billions training 20-somethings while ignoring 117 million Americans over 50 — the demographic with the most disposable income and the least digital training investment.

50+TechBridge exists to fix this. Our program is:
- **Free** — no cost barriers
- **Accessible** — delivered at libraries and senior centers people already visit
- **Inclusive** — no jargon, no prerequisites, no age limit
- **Customized** — tailored to each partner organization's community needs

We serve diverse communities across Austin — including veterans, Spanish-speaking adults, and low-income seniors — meeting people where they are, both physically and skill-wise.
