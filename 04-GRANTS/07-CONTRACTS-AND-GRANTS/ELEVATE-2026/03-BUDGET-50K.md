# Elevate Grant — $50,000 Budget Breakdown

## Program: Digital Pioneer — Technology Assisted Trainings for Adults 50+

| Category | Item | Amount |
|----------|------|--------|
| **Personnel** | Program Director (Brian McKinney) — curriculum design, instruction, program management | $15,000 |

| **Production** | Video content production — instructional videos, Digital Pioneer app content, HeyGen avatar (Barb) | $6,000 |
| **Technology** | Digital Pioneer app development — AI-powered learning tool for 50+ adults | $5,000 |
| **Technology** | Workshop equipment — tablets, hotspots, display screens for partner locations | $4,000 |
| **Marketing** | Community outreach — flyers, digital ads, library/senior center promotions, VisitAustin | $3,000 |

## What This Funds (the story)
- Expand from 12 locations to 18+ across Austin
- Serve 500+ adults 50+ in FY27 (up from 200)
- Launch Digital Pioneer app — free AI learning tool
- Produce video workshop series with HeyGen avatar "Barb"
- Host public showcase where Pioneers present digital creations
- Hire local facilitators at 6 partner sites
