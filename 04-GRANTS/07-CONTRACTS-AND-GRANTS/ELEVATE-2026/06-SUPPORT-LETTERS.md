# Elevate Grant 2026 — Letters of Support

Letters of support strengthen your application. Reach out to partners NOW — don't wait until July.

---

## Who to Ask

### Priority (Strongest Impact)
- [ ] **Austin Public Library** — program manager or branch contact who's hosted your workshops
- [ ] **Senior center director** — any of the 3 centers you've worked with
- [ ] **Foundation Communities** — veterans workshop contact

### Secondary
- [ ] **Workshop participant** — a 50+ adult who completed your training and can speak to impact
- [ ] **Workforce board contact** — anyone from Capital Area Workforce Solutions or similar
- [ ] **Community partner** — any organization that's referred people to your program

---

## What to Ask For

A short letter (1 page) that includes:
1. How they know you / 50+TechBridge
2. What programming you delivered at their location
3. The impact they observed (participant engagement, feedback, community benefit)
4. Why continued funding matters
