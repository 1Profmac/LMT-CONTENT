## Key Takeaways for 50+TechBridge FY27 Application

1. **You are the only tech/AI-focused applicant.** No other Arts Group or Creative Business in FY26 taught technology skills. Synth Salon is closest but music-focused.

2. **Your $30,000 ask is conservative.** Most Arts Groups received $30,000–$40,000. You're asking for the lower end, which reduces reviewer risk.

3. **The exhibition component (Digital Pioneer Gallery) is unique.** The Mosaic Workshop is the only comparable org with a public exhibition model. Lean into it.

4. **"Serving those others ignore" resonates.** Urban Echo ($40K), EquityThroughArt ($40K), and Art Spark Texas ($80K) all fund programs for populations mainstream arts overlook. Frame 50+ adults the same way.

5. **University partnership is a differentiator.** Lifelong Learning with Friends ($80K) leveraged a UT partnership. Your UT geriatric program connection adds credibility reviewers recognize and fund.

6. **Library delivery is proven.** Synth Salon used Austin Public Library successfully. Your 9 branches of track record strengthens this.
