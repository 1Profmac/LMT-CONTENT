## OPEN NOW — Apply Today

### 1. WIOA Eligible Training Provider List (ETPL)
- **What:** Get listed as an approved training provider so workforce boards can send you funded students
- **Award:** Students bring up to $10,000 each in Individual Training Accounts (ITAs)
- **Who:** Any training provider with programs aligned to high-demand occupations
- **Status:** Rolling application — apply anytime
- **Why it matters:** Once listed, Workforce Solutions Capital Area can refer and PAY for students to take your program
- **Action:** Contact Workforce Solutions Capital Area: https://wfscapitalarea.com
- **Also check:** https://www.twc.texas.gov/agency/workforce-development-boards/eligible-training-providers
- **You already have:** WIOA eligibility — this is about getting ON the list

### 2. Skills for Small Business (TWC)
- **What:** Training funds for small businesses (under 100 employees)
- **Award:** $2,000 per new employee, $1,000 per existing employee for training
- **Who:** Small businesses partnering with a community/technical college
- **Status:** Open — ongoing program
- **How:** Partner with Austin Community College (ACC) or another eligible college
- **Contact:** TWC at 877-463-1777
- **Link:** https://www.twc.texas.gov/programs/skills-development-fund
- **Note:** This is for training YOUR employees, not your students — but could fund a hire

### 3. PeopleFund (Austin CDFI)
- **What:** Small business loans + coaching for underserved entrepreneurs
- **Award:** Loans (not grants) — but affordable rates, plus free coaching/mentorship
- **Who:** MBE, BIPOC, veteran, and low-income business owners — you qualify
- **Status:** Open — apply anytime
- **Why it matters:** Could fund equipment, marketing, or bridge costs while waiting for grants
- **Location:** 2921 E 17th St, Building D, Suite 1, Austin TX 78702
- **Phone:** (888) 222-0017
- **Link:** https://peoplefund.org

### 4. City of Austin Small & Minority Business Resources
- **What:** Certification support, contract opportunities, business development
- **Award:** Access to City of Austin contracts set aside for MBE businesses
- **Who:** MBE-certified businesses (you have this)
- **Status:** Ongoing
- **Action:** Check for open solicitations and contract opportunities
- **Phone:** 512-974-7600
- **Link:** https://www.austintexas.gov/department/small-and-minority-business-resources-0

## COMING SOON — Watch for Application Windows

### 5. Texas Talent Connection Grant
- **What:** Innovation in workforce development
- **Award:** Up to $350,000/year (up to 3 consecutive years)
- **Who:** Workforce boards, employers, educational institutions, CBOs, faith-based orgs
- **Status:** 2025 cycle closed (deadline was April 30, 2025). Watch for 2026 open call.
- **Action:** Email wpgrants@gov.texas.gov to get on the notification list
- **Link:** https://gov.texas.gov/uploads/files/organization/twic/TTC-Application-Form.pdf
- **Note:** This is BIG money — worth preparing for when it opens

### 6. TRUE Grant Program (Texas Higher Ed)
- **What:** $12.5 million for 2026–28 to support workforce credentials
- **Award:** Varies
- **Who:** Educational institutions and training providers
- **Status:** Applications opening July 2026
- **Action:** Watch for announcement
- **Note:** May require partnership with a college/university

### 7. Skills Development Fund (TWC)
- **What:** Customized training for businesses partnered with community colleges
- **Award:** Up to $500,000
- **Who:** Must partner with a public community/technical college (ACC, TSTC, etc.)
- **Status:** Ongoing — but requires employer partner + college partner
- **Action:** Talk to ACC about partnering
- **Link:** https://www.twc.texas.gov/programs/skills-development-fund
