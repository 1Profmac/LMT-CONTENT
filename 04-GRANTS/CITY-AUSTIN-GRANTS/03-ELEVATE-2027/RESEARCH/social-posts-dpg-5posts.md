## POST 2 — "What is a Digital Pioneer?"

**Image:** Brian or cohort member in a session — working at a table, tablet in hand, or looking at their artwork on a screen.

**Caption:**
A Digital Pioneer is someone who spent decades mastering their craft — painting, photography, ceramics, writing, design — and is now learning to bring that expertise into the digital world.

Not a beginner. Not a tech person. A pioneer.

The Digital Pioneer Gallery is a free program for adults 50+ in Austin who are ready to take their creative work to the next level — building a digital portfolio, learning new tools, and showing their finished work at a real public gallery opening.

No tech experience required. Your creative experience is the requirement.

Applications open Fall 2026. Learn more at digitalpioneer.ai.
