## The 10 AI Skills

### 1. AI Sales & Outreach Automation
- **What:** Use AI to find, qualify, and contact partners/agencies at scale
- **Tools:** Claude, HubSpot (free), Apollo.io, LinkedIn Sales Navigator
- **Why:** Can't manually email 200 organizations. AI writes personalized outreach, follows up, tracks responses.
- **Current:** Manual emails, one at a time
- **Target:** 50 personalized pitches per week, automated follow-ups

### 2. AI Grant Writing
- **What:** Use AI to scan RFPs, draft proposals, customize per funder
- **Tools:** Claude, Grants.gov alerts, SAM.gov, /rfp-scan skill
- **Why:** One $50K-$200K grant changes everything. AI drafts 80%, you customize 20%.
- **Current:** Manual, infrequent
- **Target:** 2-3 grant applications per month

### 3. AI Course Packaging & Curriculum Design
- **What:** Turn 3 lessons into 28 lessons with quizzes, handouts, facilitator guides
- **Tools:** Claude, Canva, HeyGen, LearnDash
- **Why:** Need 5 cohort tiers (A-E) built out to sell. AI generates everything from outlines.
- **Current:** 3 lessons
- **Target:** 28 lessons, 5 cohort packages, ready to license

### 4. AI Video Production at Scale
- **What:** Use HeyGen + AI scripting to produce lesson videos without a film crew
- **Tools:** Claude (scripts), HeyGen (Barb/Maya), Canva (slides), video pipeline
- **Why:** 28 lessons x 3 styles x 5 languages = 420 videos. AI generates them.
- **Current:** A few lesson videos
- **Target:** Full video library, multi-language, auto-generated

### 5. AI-Powered Funnel & Email Marketing
- **What:** Automated sequences: visitors → cohort signups → paid agency clients
- **Tools:** Mailchimp, Claude (copy), HubSpot (CRM), Google Analytics
- **Why:** Machine that runs while you sleep. Free lessons → nurture → cohort → upsell.
- **Current:** Basic Mailchimp, manual sends
- **Target:** Full automated funnel, 10+ email sequences

### 6. AI Financial Modeling & Pricing
- **What:** Model revenue scenarios, set cohort pricing, forecast cash flow
- **Tools:** Claude, Google Sheets, ChatGPT Advanced Data Analysis
- **Why:** Need to know exactly when and how you hit $1M. AI runs models in seconds.
- **Current:** Guessing
- **Target:** Pricing strategy backed by numbers

### 7. AI Legal & Contract Generation
- **What:** Draft licensing agreements, partner contracts, NDAs, terms of service
- **Tools:** Claude, Texas C-BAR (free legal review), DocuSign
- **Why:** Every agency deal needs a contract. AI drafts, lawyer reviews.
- **Current:** No contracts
- **Target:** Template library for every deal type

### 8. AI Content Engine (LinkedIn + Newsletter + SEO)
- **What:** One topic/week → LinkedIn post, newsletter, blog, carousel, YouTube short
- **Tools:** Claude, Canva, /content-day skill, Buffer
- **Why:** Content builds authority → authority fills speaking → speaking fills cohorts → cohorts fill revenue.
- **Current:** Inconsistent
- **Target:** 5x/week publishing without 5x the work

### 9. AI Systems & Workflow Management
- **What:** AI agents for scheduling, reporting, invoicing, follow-ups
- **Tools:** Claude, Make.com, Zapier, Google Workspace, Cal.com
- **Why:** Solo founder = every hour matters. AI handles operations so you close deals and teach.
- **Current:** 30+ workflows (strong foundation)
- **Target:** Full business OS that runs without daily involvement

### 10. AI Data & Impact Reporting
- **What:** Auto-generate impact reports for funders, partners, board presentations
- **Tools:** Claude, Google Sheets, Canva
- **Why:** Funders renew when they see results. Partners expand when they see data.
- **Current:** Manual tracking
- **Target:** Auto-generated reports per cohort
