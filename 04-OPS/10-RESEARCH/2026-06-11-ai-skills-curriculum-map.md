# AI-Adjacent Skills Curriculum Map — 50+TechBridge
**Date:** June 11, 2026
**Author:** Brian McKinney / Claude
**Purpose:** Full list of AI skills to teach adults 50+, organized by cohort tier and impact

---

## Already Teaching (Intro — Free Lessons 1-3)

| Skill | AI Connection |
|---|---|
| Phone setup & accessibility | AI-powered text sizing, display optimization |
| Voice commands (Siri/Google) | AI voice recognition & natural language processing |
| Maps & navigation | AI-powered real-time routing, traffic prediction |

---

## Tier 1 — High Impact, Easy to Teach

| # | Skill | What They'll Do | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | Talk to ChatGPT/Claude | Ask questions in plain English — recipes, letters, travel tips, "explain my bill" | The #1 AI skill. Removes fear of AI in one session. |
| 5 | Voice-to-text messaging | Dictate texts and emails instead of typing | Arthritis, low vision, fat fingers — solved |
| 6 | AI photo search | Find photos by saying "show me pictures from Christmas" | They have 10,000 photos and can't find any |
| 7 | Medication reminders | Set up AI-powered reminders on phone or Alexa | Health independence — this saves lives |
| 8 | Scam & fraud detection | Recognize AI-generated scam calls, phishing emails, fake texts | #1 fear for adults 50+ online. Teach defense. |
| 9 | Smart speaker setup | Set up Alexa or Google Home — timers, weather, music, calls | Hands-free living for mobility/vision issues |
| 10 | Telehealth appointments | Join a video doctor visit, share symptoms, use patient portals | Post-COVID essential. Most 50+ still struggle. |

## Tier 2 — Independence & Daily Life

| # | Skill | What They'll Do | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| 11 | Online banking & bill pay | Check balances, pay bills, spot fraud alerts | Financial independence — no more asking kids |
| 12 | Grocery delivery & apps | Use Instacart, Walmart, Amazon Fresh with voice or app | Mobility-limited adults get food without help |
| 13 | Ride-sharing (Uber/Lyft) | Book a ride, track the driver, share trip with family | Transportation = freedom |
| 14 | AI-powered translation | Use Google Translate camera, voice, or text for real-time translation | Huge for Spanish-speaking families and travel |
| 15 | Video calling (Zoom/FaceTime) | Call grandkids, join community groups, attend church online | Fights social isolation — the #1 health risk |
| 16 | AI accessibility tools | Live captions, magnifier, hearing aid integration, read-aloud | Vision/hearing loss doesn't mean tech exclusion |
| 17 | Digital calendar & reminders | Appointments, birthdays, recurring reminders | "I forgot" becomes "my phone told me" |

## Tier 3 — Earning, Creating & Thriving

| # | Skill | What They'll Do | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| 18 | AI writing help | Write emails, letters, complaints, cover letters with ChatGPT | "I'm not good with words" — AI handles it |
| 19 | Resume & job search with AI | Build a resume, search jobs, prep for interviews | WIOA-eligible workforce re-entry — fundable |
| 20 | Sell on Facebook Marketplace | List items, take photos, set prices, message buyers | Turn clutter into cash |
| 21 | Start a side hustle with AI | Use AI to write listings, manage a small business, market services | Retirement income gap — AI helps close it |
| 22 | Social media basics | Post on Facebook, join groups, connect on LinkedIn | Community, not vanity — reduces isolation |
| 23 | AI photo editing | Remove backgrounds, enhance old photos, create collages | Preserve family memories, make gifts |
| 24 | Digital legacy & passwords | Password manager, digital will, shared family access | The conversation nobody has until it's too late |

## Key Stats for Pitches

- AI usage among 50+ nearly doubled from 2024 to 2025
- ~30% of older adults have interacted with an AI platform
- ~50% have used a voice assistant in the past year
- Social isolation is the #1 health risk for adults 50+ — cohorts fight it
- Scam targeting of older adults is increasing with AI-generated fraud
