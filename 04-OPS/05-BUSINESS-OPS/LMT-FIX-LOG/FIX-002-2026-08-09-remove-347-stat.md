# FIX-002 — Remove 347+ Pioneers Stat from Proof Bars
Date: 2026-08-09
Site: learnmoretechnologies.com
Fixed by: Claude Code + Brian

---

## Problem
The "347+ Pioneers Trained" stat was appearing in the proof/trust bars on both the homepage and the Services & Pricing page. At this stage of growth, it signals "small" to B2B buyers (brands, workforce boards, conference organizers) who compare against vendors training thousands.

---

## Decision
Replace 347+ with the **$18K ROI stat** — money saved per untrained 50+ worker annually (LSE & Protiviti research). This stat:
- Makes the B2B buyer do math immediately
- Justifies the purchase price without mentioning headcount
- Is sourced from credible third-party research

---

## New 4-Stat Bar (use on all pages)
| Stat | Label |
|---|---|
| $18K | Saved per untrained 50+ worker annually — LSE & Protiviti |
| 3X | Industry Completion Rate |
| 74% | Feel More Confident After Training |
| 23 | Organizations Trust Us |
