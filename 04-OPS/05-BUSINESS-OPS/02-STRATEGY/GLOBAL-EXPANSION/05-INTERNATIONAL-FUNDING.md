# International Funding Roadmap

## Funding Strategy
Layer multiple funding sources. Never depend on one. Mix grants, earned revenue, and strategic partnerships.

---

## Tier 1: Apply Now or Next Cycle (2026–2027)

### Mastercard Foundation EdTech Fellowship
- **What:** Equity-free grant for EdTech startups serving Africa
- **Award:** Up to $60,000 + mentorship + Carnegie Mellon sessions
- **Fit:** Train-the-Trainer deployment in Ghana, Kenya, Egypt
- **Requirement:** Must serve African market, growth-stage
- **Action:** Apply for Cohort 4 when announced (watch mastercardfdn.org)
- **Note:** May need to establish African entity or partner

### UNESCO International Literacy Prize
- **What:** Recognition + $30,000 for outstanding literacy programs
- **Award:** $30,000 per laureate, 6 winners annually
- **Fit:** 2027 theme TBD — digital literacy + aging = strong angle
- **Requirement:** 2+ years operation, measurable impact, UNESCO endorsement
- **Action:** Contact US National Commission for UNESCO for nomination
- **Deadline:** Typically June — apply 2027
- **Link:** https://www.unesco.org/en/prizes/literacy

### AARP Community Challenge (US — Annual)
- **What:** Quick-action community grants including digital connections
- **Award:** Up to $15,000
- **Fit:** Perfect — digital literacy for 50+ adults
- **Deadline:** March 2027 (annual cycle)
- **Action:** Prep application Jan–Feb 2027

### EU Horizon Europe / Digital Europe Programme
- **What:** EU-funded digital skills and inclusion programs
- **Award:** €50,000–€500,000
- **Fit:** Requires EU-based entity or partner — Spain/Portugal base unlocks this
- **Action:** Establish Spain base first, then apply with local partner
- **Note:** This is the BIG money in Europe

## Tier 2: Build Toward (2027–2028)

### USAID Digital Development
- **What:** Grants for ICT4D (Information & Communication Technology for Development)
- **Award:** Varies widely — $50K–$5M
- **Fit:** Digital literacy in developing countries for underserved populations
- **Requirement:** Usually needs US-based 501(c)(3) + in-country partner
- **Action:** Get 501(c)(3) first, then register on SAM.gov, then watch for NOFOs
- **Link:** https://www.usaid.gov/digital-development

### World Bank Digital Skills Programs
- **What:** Large-scale digital skills funding in developing countries
- **Award:** $100K–$10M (usually through implementing partners)
- **Fit:** Consulting/implementation partner role
- **Action:** Register as a vendor, network at World Bank events
- **Entry:** Start as a sub-contractor to a prime partner

### Inter-American Development Bank (IDB)
- **What:** Development funding for Latin America and Caribbean
- **Award:** Varies
- **Fit:** Panama, Colombia, Mexico digital inclusion programs
- **Action:** Register as consultant/vendor at iadb.org

### Ford Foundation / Skoll Foundation / Gates Foundation
- **What:** Private foundations funding digital equity and social innovation
- **Award:** $50K–$500K
- **Fit:** Digital equity, aging, workforce development
- **Action:** Most are invitation-only — build visibility first through UNESCO, Mastercard Foundation, speaking circuit
- **Note:** Winning a UNESCO prize or Mastercard Fellowship opens these doors

## Funding Ladder (The Sequence Matters)

```
Local grants (Elevate, Nexus) → $5K–$40K
         ↓ proves the model
National grants (NEA, AARP, NCOA) → $10K–$100K
         ↓ proves US scale
International recognition (UNESCO Prize) → $30K + credibility
         ↓ opens doors
Foundation funding (Mastercard, Ford, Skoll) → $60K–$500K
         ↓ proves global demand
Government contracts (USAID, EU, World Bank) → $100K–$5M
         ↓ sustainable at scale
Corporate partnerships (Google, Microsoft, Cisco) → $100K–$1M+
```

Each rung makes the next one possible. You can't skip steps, but you can move fast.
