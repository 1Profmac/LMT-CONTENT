# 50+TechBridge Global Vision

## The Mission (Unchanged)
Close the digital divide for adults 50+ — everywhere.

## The Shift
Austin was the proving ground. The world is the product.

We don't teach 50,000 people one at a time. We build the system — curriculum, facilitators, partnerships, and technology — that trains 50,000 people across continents.

## The Identity

**Learn More Technologies** = The company. The global brand. The entity that licenses, speaks, consults, and deploys.

**50+TechBridge** = The program. The deployable, customizable digital literacy training system for adults 50+. Runs in any country, any language, any community institution.

## The Positioning

> "The only scalable, community-deployed AI and digital literacy training system built specifically for adults 50+. Proven across 9 in-person locations in the US. Ready for the world."

## The Numbers

| Milestone | How | When |
|---|---|---|
| 500 trained | Austin direct delivery | End of 2026 |
| 2,500 trained | Train-the-Trainer US pilot (50 facilitators × 50 each) | Mid 2027 |
| 10,000 trained | First international deployments (3–5 countries) | End 2027 |
| 50,000 trained | Full global network (20+ countries, 500+ facilitators) | End 2028 |

## Core Belief
Ageism is global. The digital divide is global. The solution must be global. No one else is doing this at scale for people over 50. We will.
