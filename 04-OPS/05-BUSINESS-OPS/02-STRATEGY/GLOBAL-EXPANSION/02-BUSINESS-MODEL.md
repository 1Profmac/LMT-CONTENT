# Global Business Model — Learn More Technologies

## Four Revenue Pillars

### Pillar 1: Curriculum Licensing
**What:** Organizations pay to deploy the 50+TechBridge program in their community.
**Who buys:** Libraries, senior centers, NGOs, government agencies, workforce boards — globally.
**What they get:**
- Customized curriculum (localized for language, culture, tech access)
- Facilitator training (Train-the-Trainer certification)
- Barb (HeyGen AI avatar) localized instruction videos
- Ongoing support and curriculum updates
- 50+TechBridge branding and co-branding rights

**Pricing tiers:**
| Tier | What | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Community License | 1 location, 1 year, up to 100 learners | $2,500–$5,000 |
| Regional License | Up to 10 locations, 1 year | $15,000–$25,000 |
| National License | Unlimited locations, 1 country, 1 year | $50,000–$100,000 |
| Government Contract | Custom deployment + consulting | $100,000+ |

### Pillar 3: Train-the-Trainer
**What:** Certify local facilitators to deliver 50+TechBridge in their community.
**Model:** Brian trains the trainers. Trainers train the people. That's how you hit 50K.

**Program structure:**
- 3-day intensive (in-person or hybrid)
- Certification exam
- Ongoing community of practice (monthly calls)
- Annual re-certification

**Pricing:**
| Format | Fee |
|---|---|
| Individual certification (online) | $500 |
| Cohort certification (10–20 people, in-person) | $5,000–$10,000 |
| Institutional certification (org-wide) | $15,000–$25,000 |

**Scale math:**
- Train 100 facilitators in Year 1
- Each facilitator trains 50 people/year
- = 5,000 trained in Year 1 of scale
- Train 500 facilitators by end of Year 2
- = 25,000 trained
- By Year 3: 50,000+

**Deliverables:** Needs assessment, curriculum design, facilitator training, program evaluation, policy recommendations.

**Pricing:**

| Full program design + pilot | $25,000–$75,000 |
