## Speaking Fee Structure

Start lower, raise with credibility:

| Stage | Fee | When |
|---|---|---|
| Building credibility | Free–$2,500 | Now → mid 2027 |
| Established speaker | $5,000–$10,000 | Mid 2027 → 2028 |
| In-demand international | $10,000–$25,000 | 2028+ |
| Keynote headliner | $25,000+ | After TEDx, UNESCO, or WEF |

**Rule:** Never speak for free without getting something — video rights, audience email list, partnership introduction, or media coverage. Free talks are marketing, not charity.
