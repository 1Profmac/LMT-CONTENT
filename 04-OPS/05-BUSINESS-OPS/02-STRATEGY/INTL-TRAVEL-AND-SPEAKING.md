## How speaking actually gets booked

Do not cold-apply to Lisbon. Get invited by people who have already watched you in a room.

**This week**

1. List every host who has already put you in front of adults 50+ (APL branches, AARP-affiliated centers, Foundation Communities, chamber or workforce contacts). That is the booking list.
2. Email each one:

> I have a 45-minute talk for your staff / members / annual meeting: why ignoring adults 50+ is a business and service failure, and what to do in the first hour. Honorarium $2,500. If you have a conference or a partner association that needs this, will you introduce me to the program chair?

3. Call program chairs, not speaker platforms:
   - Texas Library Association / a regional library consortium
   - American Society on Aging, or the state aging-services association
   - A workforce board annual meeting (Capital Area or MassHire)

   Ask who books 2027 speakers and what the deadline is. Send the speaker sheet the same day. Do not send a grant packet.

4. Record one 5-minute phone clip: one-liner + one library-room story + the five-step method (Open → Ask → Read → Follow up → Save). No studio required.

**This month**

5. Book two paid or travel-covered talks in Austin, Lowell, or Boston. Film them (phone on a stand, host permission).
6. After each talk, ask the organizer for two introductions to other program chairs.

**Virtual first for “international”**  
A foreign association can buy a Zoom keynote for $2,500 while you sit in Ayer. Do three of those before you buy a transatlantic ticket.

## When you get on a plane

All three must be true:

1. A signed speaker agreement (fee, or at least travel + hotel)
2. Dates and city on their letterhead
3. One extra day used for a paid Train-the-Trainer or a contractor scoping call in that city

Works: ASA or a library conference pays $2,500 + flight; you add a $2,500 SOW conversation in the same city.  
Fails: Web Summit “speaker” badge, you pay Lisbon, you call it strategy.

**Do not apply in 2026 unless you are already in that city**

| Event | Why not (yet) |
|---|---|
| SXSW | Fine *if you already live there that week*. It does not pay. |
| Web Summit / UNESCO / AfricArena | Application ≠ booking. You pay to attend. |
| TEDx | Credibility tape, not income. Do it after two paid talks exist on video. |

Apply after a buyer has already paid you to be in that country, or after a visa and a retainer cover the month.
