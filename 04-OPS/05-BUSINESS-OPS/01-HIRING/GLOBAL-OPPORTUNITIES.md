## Executive Summary

The global market for 50+ digital literacy training is accelerating in every target region. Government funding is flowing — but most programs fund local intermediaries (libraries, councils, nonprofits), not direct foreign vendors. LMT's strongest entry paths are:

1. **Curriculum licensing** to established local partners
2. **Train-the-trainer deals** with national networks
3. **Speaking** at international conferences to build credibility that opens doors
4. **Corporate training** — bypasses government procurement entirely, fastest revenue path
