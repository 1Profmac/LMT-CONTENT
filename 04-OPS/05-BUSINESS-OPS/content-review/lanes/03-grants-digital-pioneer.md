### What a gathering is

A free public experience for adults 50+. Walk-ins welcome. No device and no prior experience required. We meet people in rooms they already trust.

We are not selling a class. We are inviting people into a cohort. Participants are **Digital Pioneers**.

**Five steps in the room:** Open the tool. Ask with a starter prompt. Read for one useful thing. Follow up. Save something that leaves in their pocket.

**One rule in the first fifteen minutes:** AI can be wrong about medicine, law, and money. The human decides.

**9 in-person locations** in Central Texas, including:

Libraries are where we meet, greet, and practice. They are not exhibition venues.

All programming is **free to participants**. The funder or sponsor underwrites the gathering.

### What a host or sponsor provides

- A room, a date, and a trusted invitation to adults 50+  
- Tables, chairs, and (if possible) a few shared computers or a lab  
- Sign-in sheets (accepted). We do **not** publish headcounts.

**What we provide:** facilitation, the five-step method, a close-out note for your file, optional follow-up in the online Pioneer community.

### Funding status (read before you apply)

- City of Austin **Nexus Grant** recipient (past performance).  
- **Texas MBE** and **WIOA-eligible** describe the operating company. They do not make an LLC eligible for City of Boston Age Strong grants.  
- Age Strong Rolling Grant and Expanding Engagement: **501(c)(3) or official fiscal sponsor only.** For-profits are ineligible. The FY26 rolling window closed 7 May 2026.  
- Massachusetts SDO certification is separate from Texas MBE.

Until 501(c)(3) or a Boston fiscal sponsor is in place, the ask is: **host a gathering** or **sponsor the work**. It is not “cut a grant check to the LLC.”
