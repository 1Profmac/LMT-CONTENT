### Course 1 — Foundation (submit first in MA)

**Title:** AI Literacy for Adults 50+ — Foundation  
**Hours:** 9  
**Format:** In-person or hosted lab; device provided at host site if needed  
**Outcome:** Participant can open an approved AI tool, complete the starter prompt, ask a follow-up, save one useful output, and state the verification rule for medicine, law, and money.

*I’m new to AI and I’m over 50. Please explain [topic] in five simple steps, using clear language.*

**Method:** Open → Ask → Read → Follow up → Save.
