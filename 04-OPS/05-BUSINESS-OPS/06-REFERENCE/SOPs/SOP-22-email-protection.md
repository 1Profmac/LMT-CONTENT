# SOP-22 — Email Protection
## Stop spam, protect your business identity, keep inbox clean

**Purpose:** Reduce spam to under 10% of inbox and protect brian@learnmo.com from being on public lists
**Trigger:** Inbox is overwhelmed with non-business email OR setting up a new public-facing form/registration
**Owner:** Brian McKinney
**Time required:** 30–60 min (one-time setup), 5 min/week maintenance
**Frequency:** One-time setup, then ongoing habit

---

## QUICK REFERENCE
1. Bulk unsubscribe with Unroll.me (kills 80% of spam fast)
2. Turn on Gmail Promotions + Social tabs
3. Create filters for repeat offenders
4. Create a public-facing email (info@) for forms and registrations
5. Keep brian@learnmo.com for real people only

---

## STEP-BY-STEP

### STEP 1 — Bulk Unsubscribe (do this first)
- Go to unroll.me
- Sign in with your Gmail account
- Review the list of every subscription you're on
- Unsubscribe from everything not related to business growth
- Takes 15–20 minutes — kills most newsletter spam permanently
- Alternative tools: Cleanfox, Leave.me

### STEP 2 — Turn On Gmail Category Tabs
- Open Gmail → Settings (gear icon) → See All Settings
- Click **Inbox** tab
- Check: **Promotions** and **Social**
- Save — Gmail now auto-sorts marketing email out of Primary

### STEP 3 — Filter Repeat Offenders
- Open a spam email → click the 3-dot menu → "Filter messages like this"
- Set action: Skip inbox + Delete (or Archive)
- Repeat for top 10 recurring senders
- Gmail learns from your behavior over time

### STEP 4 — Create a Public Email Address
- Create: info@learnmoretechnologies.com (or info@50plustechbridge.com)
- Use this address for:
  - Grant applications and registrations
  - Website contact forms
  - WIOA / SAM.gov / TrainingPro registrations
  - Any public-facing signup
- Keep brian@learnmo.com for: real contacts, partners, funders, people you actually talk to

### STEP 5 — Forward Public Email to Gmail (optional)
- Set info@ to auto-forward to brian@learnmo.com
- Apply a Gmail label: "Public Inbox"
- Review that label once a day — not Primary

---

## DECISION POINTS

**If you get a cold sales email:**
- Unsubscribe using the link at the bottom (required by law — they must honor it)
- If no unsubscribe link → mark as spam in Gmail

**If it's a newsletter you signed up for but don't read:**
- Unsubscribe via Unroll.me — don't just delete, or it keeps coming

**If a grant or registration asks for your email:**
- Always use info@ or a public address — never brian@learnmo.com

**If spam keeps coming after unsubscribing:**
- Mark as spam in Gmail — trains the filter
- Use Leave.me: forward the email to unsubscribe@leave.me

---

## COMMON MISTAKES
- Using brian@learnmo.com on public forms — puts you on lists permanently
- Deleting spam without unsubscribing — it keeps coming
- Not turning on Gmail category tabs — everything lands in Primary
- Waiting to do Unroll.me — 15 minutes now saves hours per month
