# SOP-04 — YouTube SEO Package
## Every video gets this before upload. No exceptions.

**Purpose:** Get every video found in YouTube search without paid ads.
**Trigger:** Video is ready to upload.
**Owner:** Brian McKinney
**Time required:** 15 minutes
**Frequency:** Every YouTube upload

---

## QUICK REFERENCE — 7 Items Per Video
1. Title (keyword first, under 60 chars)
2. Description (hook in first 2 lines + template)
3. Tags (15 permanent + 5-8 topic-specific)
4. Thumbnail (face + 3-5 words, high contrast)
5. Chapters (timestamps starting at 0:00)
6. End screen + cards
7. Playlist

---

## THE 7-ITEM PACKAGE

### 1. TITLE
- Under 60 characters
- Front-load the keyword (searchable phrase first)
- Use a number when possible
- Formula: `[Keyword] + [Hook] + [Qualifier]`

Good: `AI Training for Adults 50+ | What 13 Locations Learned`
Bad: `My Thoughts on AI`

### 2. DESCRIPTION
First 2 lines show before "Show More" — must hook AND contain keyword.

```
[Stat or hook with primary keyword]
[What viewer will learn + CTA]

In this video:
- [Key point 1]
- [Key point 2]
- [Key point 3]

Chapters:
0:00 Introduction
0:XX [Next section]

Resources:
- Free course: https://learnmoretechnologies.com/join-now/
- Book a session: https://cal.com/brian-mckinney-mrtu8q

About 50+TechBridge:
AI and digital skills training designed for adults 50+. 13 locations. MBE Certified. WIOA Eligible.

Brian McKinney | Founder, Learn More Technologies
https://learnmoretechnologies.com

#agentic50 #Adults50Plus #AITraining #AgeTech #WorkforceDevelopment #DigitalInclusion #Over50 #WIOA
```
> The About block and Resources links are the same on EVERY video. Copy-paste. Never retype.

### 3. TAGS
**Permanent tags (every video — copy-paste):**
```
50+TechBridge, adults over 50, AI training, digital skills, ageism, AgeTech,
workforce development, older adults, Brian McKinney, Learn More Technologies,
AI for seniors, technology training, WIOA, digital inclusion, over 50
```

**Topic-specific (add 5-8 per video):**

| Topic | Add These |
|---|---|
| Workforce/contracts | WIOA training, workforce board, AI workforce, upskilling older workers |
| Ageism/stats | age discrimination, $850 billion, AARP, workplace ageism |
| AI tools/how-to | Claude AI, AI tools for beginners, AI tutorial, how to use AI |
| Course/lessons | online course, digital literacy, tech training, peer instruction |

### 4. THUMBNAIL
- Your face, large, strong expression
- 3-5 words MAX as text overlay
- High contrast: gold on navy OR white on dark
- Readable on a phone
- Build in Canva: 1280x720px → export PNG

### 5. CHAPTERS (timestamps)
```
0:00 Introduction
0:15 [Section 2]
1:02 [Section 3]
```
- Must start at 0:00
- Each chapter minimum 10 seconds

### 6. END SCREEN + CARDS
- End screen (last 20 sec): Subscribe + best video + newest video
- Cards: 1-2 links to related videos during the video

### 7. PLAYLIST
Every video goes into a playlist. Add immediately on upload.
- 50+TechBridge Course (lessons)
- AI Training for Adults 50+ (general)
- The Numbers Nobody Told You (data/stats series)

---

## COMMON MISTAKES
- Weak title with no keyword (kills discoverability)
- Skipping chapters (free SEO boost in YouTube search results)
- Thumbnail with too much text (unreadable on mobile)
- Forgetting to add to playlist
