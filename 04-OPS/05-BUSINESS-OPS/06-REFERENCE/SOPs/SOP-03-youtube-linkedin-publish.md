# SOP-03 — YouTube + LinkedIn Publish
## Upload → SEO → Thumbnail → LinkedIn post

**Purpose:** Publish a finished video to YouTube and LinkedIn the right way, every time.
**Trigger:** Video MP4 is exported and ready.
**Owner:** Brian McKinney
**Time required:** 30-45 minutes
**Frequency:** Every video published

---

## QUICK REFERENCE
1. YouTube → Upload → fill SEO package (see SOP-04) → thumbnail → playlist → Unlisted or Public
2. Add end screen (last 20 sec) → cards
3. LinkedIn → Write article → same title → same thumbnail → publish → first comment

---

## PREREQUISITES
- [ ] MP4 file ready
- [ ] YouTube SEO package prepared (see SOP-04)
- [ ] Thumbnail built in Canva (1280x720 PNG)
- [ ] LinkedIn article content ready

---

## STEP-BY-STEP

### STEP 1 — YouTube Upload
1. YouTube Studio → **Create → Upload video**
2. Select MP4 file
3. While uploading, fill in Details:
   - **Title:** Use SOP-04 formula (keyword first, under 60 chars)
   - **Description:** Use SOP-04 template
   - **Thumbnail:** Upload Canva PNG (1280x720)
   - **Playlist:** 50+TechBridge Course
   - **Audience:** Not made for kids

### STEP 2 — End Screens (last 20 seconds)
1. Video Elements → **End Screen → Add**
2. Add: **Subscribe** element
3. Add: **Video** element (Best for viewer)
4. Save end screen

### STEP 3 — Cards
1. Add 1-2 cards linking to related videos
2. Place at moments when viewer might click away

### STEP 4 — Playlist
1. Confirm video is added to: **50+TechBridge Course**
2. Playlist visibility: **Public**

### STEP 5 — Visibility
- Lesson videos: **Unlisted** (linked from LearnDash only)
- Marketing videos: **Public** (or Schedule for batch publishing)
- Click **Save** or **Publish**

### STEP 6 — LinkedIn Article
1. LinkedIn → **Write article**
2. Title: match the YouTube video title
3. Body: paste article content
4. Cover image: upload the same Canva thumbnail PNG
5. Intro post line (in the publish box):
   > "Just published a new lesson for 50+TechBridge. If you know an adult 50+ who is ready to learn how AI can support their independence — this is where they start."
6. Click **Publish**
7. Immediately add **first comment** (author comment boosts reach):
   > "This is exactly the kind of resource our community needs. AI skills aren't just for the young — they're for anyone ready to take control of their future."

### STEP 7 — After Publishing
- [ ] YouTube → verify thumbnail is showing correctly
- [ ] Copy YouTube link → add to 50plustechbridge.com welcome page (if applicable)
- [ ] Share article link in MailerLite newsletter (next send)
- [ ] Post excerpt to Facebook (mirror LinkedIn)

---

## COMMON MISTAKES
- Skipping the first LinkedIn comment (loses early reach boost)
- Using a different thumbnail on LinkedIn vs. YouTube (inconsistent brand)
- Publishing lesson videos as Public (should be Unlisted)
- Forgetting end screens (free channel growth tool)
