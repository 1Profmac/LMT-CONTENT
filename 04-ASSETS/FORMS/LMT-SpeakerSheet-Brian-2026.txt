  SPEAKING TOPICS
================================================================================

  ── KEYNOTE 1 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  "The $76 Trillion Mistake: Why Ignoring Adults 50+ Is Your Biggest
   Business Risk"

  The 50+ market controls $76 trillion in global wealth and represents the
  fastest-growing segment of the workforce — yet most organizations treat them
  as an afterthought. Brian makes the economic case for inclusion, shares
  what's actually possible when older adults are given the right tools, and
  delivers a framework for reversing the trend before the opportunity closes.

  Best for: Corporate executives, workforce boards, economic development orgs,
            chambers of commerce, HR and DEI conferences


  ── KEYNOTE 2 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  "Ageism Is the Last Acceptable Bias — And AI Is Making It Worse"

  Ageism costs the U.S. economy $850 billion a year. Now AI is accelerating
  it — automating away jobs held by experienced workers while simultaneously
  creating new ones that nobody is training older adults to fill. Brian
  delivers a clear-eyed look at the crisis, the human cost, and the path
  forward — built on real programs, real people, and real results.

  Best for: Policy conferences, aging services, nonprofits, foundations,
            workforce development, public libraries, city and county government


  ── WORKSHOP / LUNCH & LEARN ────────────────────────────────────────────────

  "AI in One Hour: What Every Adult 50+ Needs to Know Right Now"

  Hands-on. No jargon. No coding. Brian walks any audience through the
  practical AI tools reshaping everyday life — live demonstrations, real
  examples, and a clear action plan participants can use the same day.
  Customized for your audience's industry, role, or community.

  Best for: Employee resource groups (50+/Boomer ERGs), library systems,
            senior centers, community organizations, faith communities,
            workforce boards
