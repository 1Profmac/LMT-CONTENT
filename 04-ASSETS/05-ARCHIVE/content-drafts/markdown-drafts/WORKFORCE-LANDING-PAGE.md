## THE PROBLEM

### Your Workforce is Aging. Your Training Is Not.

Most organizations send experienced workers to AI and digital skills workshops designed for 25-year-olds. Then they wonder why completion rates are under 15%.

The problem is not your experienced workers. The problem is the training.

Adults 50+ learn differently. They need:
- **Context** — Why does this matter to MY job?
- **Pace** — Time to practice, not just watch
- **Respect** — Instruction that builds on what they already know
- **Relevance** — Real tasks, not theoretical exercises

When you train them right, they outperform every other demographic. We have the data to prove it.
