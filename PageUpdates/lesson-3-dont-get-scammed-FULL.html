Lesson 3: Don't Get Scammed

50+TechBridge · Free Lesson 3 of 3

⏳ Takes about 8 minutes

Now You Do It:

  1. The fridge rule. Write this down and put it somewhere visible: Unexpected + hurry + money or a code = hang up and verify.
  2. Pick a family code word. One word or short phrase only your people know. Text it to them today. Not on Facebook.
  3. Turn on 2FA for email. Go to Settings → Security → look for Two-factor or 2-step verification. Turn it on.
  4. Check your email password. Is it the same as your bank? Change one of them to a long phrase — like maple-porch-radio-Thursday.
  5. If a scam already happened: call the bank now, then report at ReportFraud.ftc.gov and ic3.gov.

You are now ahead of most of the country. You did that. 🎉
