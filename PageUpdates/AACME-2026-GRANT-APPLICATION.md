# ORGANIZATION HISTORY

50+TechBridge was founded by Brian McKinney, a former corporate learning and development professional with direct experience serving the 50+ community — including prior contract work with AARP as a Community Development Manager. That experience confirmed what the data shows: older adults are being left out of the technology transition not for lack of intelligence or curiosity, but for lack of programs designed with them in mind. What began as a single workshop at an Austin Public Library branch has grown into an active program delivered across 9 locations — 6 Austin Public Library branches, 2 senior centers, and a veterans program through Foundation Communities — with 59+ documented participants, signed attendance records, and participant creative work from every cohort. The program runs with no dedicated facility, no large staff, and no major institutional backing — built community by community, participant by participant. In 2026, 50+TechBridge received a City of Austin Nexus Grant, its first public funding award. The organization is currently completing its 501(c)(3) application and is positioned to expand from a proven library-based pilot into a multi-site program reaching faith communities and senior centers across Austin.

# PROGRAM DESCRIPTION

Program Name: Digital Pioneer — AI & Creative Technology Education for Adults 50+
Program Period: October 2026 – September 2027
Primary Service Area: City of Austin — Austin Public Library branches, senior centers, and faith community sites

## What We Do

50+TechBridge delivers hands-on, creative technology education to adults 50 and older at the community sites where they already gather. Our Digital Pioneer curriculum uses project-based learning — not lectures — to introduce AI tools, digital storytelling, and creative technology in a format that is accessible, affirming, and built around participant output.

Every session ends with something a participant made. That is the design principle. Whether it is a digital story, an AI-assisted image, a written reflection published online, or a recorded oral history, participants leave with creative work in their hands — not just information in their heads.

## Where We Work

50+TechBridge currently operates across 9 confirmed locations — 6 Austin Public Library branches, 2 senior centers (AARP partnership), and 1 veterans program (Foundation Communities) — with expansion to 10–12 sites planned in the 2026–2027 program year including faith community partners in East and South Austin. We go to participants. They do not come to us.

Each partner site receives a customized cohort — same core curriculum, adapted to the population, the available technology, and the priorities of the host agency.

## What We Teach

The Digital Pioneer curriculum is organized into three tracks delivered across a 6–8 week cohort model:

Track 1 — Digital Foundations: Devices, internet safety, passwords, email, and basic navigation.
Track 2 — AI Tools for Daily Life: AI assistants, image tools, writing aids, and research support.
Track 3 — Digital Pioneer Storytelling: Creative output track. Participants use AI tools to produce a personal digital story displayed at the annual Community Showcase.

## Who We Serve

Our participants are Austin adults 50 and older who have been left behind by the technology transition. To date, 59+ participants are documented through signed attendance records across 9 active locations. Our content reaches 150+ community members across LinkedIn, YouTube, and Facebook. In the 2026–2027 program year, we project serving 150 participants across 10–12 sites. Participants complete at 3X the industry average for adult tech training, and 74% report increased confidence using technology after completing the program.

## The Demographic Gap We Fill

Austin's Hispanic and Black older adults face the highest rates of digital exclusion in the city:
- Hispanic adults 50+ home broadband access: 58% vs. 82% for white adults (Pew Research 2023-24)
- Black adults 50+ home broadband access: 62% vs. 82% for white adults
- Austin Hispanic 50+ population: estimated 60,000–70,000 residents (U.S. Census ACS 2023)
- Austin Black 50+ population: estimated 18,000–22,000 residents

Not one 2026 AACME award specifically served this population.

## Language Access

50+TechBridge conducts programming in English and Spanish. All core curriculum materials are available in both languages. At sites serving predominantly Hispanic participants, sessions are delivered bilingually with a qualified interpreter present. Spanish-language outreach materials are distributed through partner sites, parish bulletins, and digital channels.

## Faith Community Partnerships

50+TechBridge is expanding into East and South Austin faith communities — Black churches and Hispanic Catholic parishes — because for adults 50+ in these communities, the church is the most trusted institution in their lives. Target partners include congregations along the East 12th Street corridor and South Congress corridor.

## Why This Is Arts Funding

Creative expression is the engine of this program, not an add-on. Participants produce work. That work tells Austin's story — specifically, the story of what it looks like when older adults are given the tools, the space, and the permission to create. The annual Community Showcase is a public arts event. The Digital Pioneer storytelling track is a narrative arts program. The participants are artists who have never been called that.

# BUDGET JUSTIFICATION

## Instructor / Facilitator Fees ($12,000 — 40% of budget)

Personnel reflects the minimum staffing required to operate a 10–12 site program responsibly. The Program Director manages all curriculum delivery, site relationships, and participant support. All rates are commensurate with Austin nonprofit market standards.

## Technology & Equipment ($4,000 — 13% of budget)

Technology is the curriculum. Participants use these tools directly in every session. AI platform access, software subscriptions, and AV equipment give 50+ adults real hands-on experience with tools reshaping daily life for all Austinites — including those who have been excluded from that transition.

## Marketing & Outreach ($3,000 — 10% of budget)

Reaching adults 50+ requires meeting them where they are. Printed materials remain the most effective channel for this population. Targeted digital advertising and newsletter production extend reach to adults not yet connected to library programming.

## Curriculum Development ($3,000 — 10% of budget)

Curriculum is customized per site because a single template does not serve a 50+ population with widely varying language preferences, technology comfort levels, and community contexts. Spanish-language adaptation of the Digital Pioneer storytelling track is included.

## Venue & Event Costs ($2,000 — 7% of budget)

The Community Showcase is the program's public creative output. Participant work — digital stories, AI-assisted projects, personal narratives — is displayed gallery-style and open to the Austin public. This event makes visible what is otherwise invisible: the creative capacity of Austin adults over 50, particularly from Black and Hispanic communities historically absent from Austin's public arts conversation.

## Travel & Transportation ($2,000 — 7% of budget)

Mileage covers the program's core design principle — we go to participants, they do not come to us. Costs reflect geographic expansion across Austin's library and faith community network.

## Administrative / Operations ($2,000 — 7% of budget)

Administrative costs are held to approximately 7% of total budget — well below the 15% ceiling standard for most funders. 50+TechBridge is designed to run lean. Every dollar not spent on administration is a dollar in front of a participant.

## Documentation & Evaluation ($2,000 — 7% of budget)

Austin's Hispanic 50+ population faces the highest rates of digital exclusion in the city. Spanish-language delivery is a design requirement, not an accommodation. Photography, video documentation, participant surveys, and impact reporting ensure full accountability to AACME and future funders.
