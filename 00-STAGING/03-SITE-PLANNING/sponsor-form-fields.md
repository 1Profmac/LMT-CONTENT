# Sponsor Inquiry Form — Fields
## Build in WordPress (WPForms, Gravity Forms, or Google Forms)
## Embed on sponsor page or link to Cal.com with pre-fill

---

## Form Fields

**Organization Name** (required — text)

**Your Name** (required — text)

**Your Title/Role** (required — text)

**Email** (required — email)

**Phone** (optional — text)

**Organization Type** (required — dropdown)
- Community Bank
- Corporation
- Foundation / Nonprofit
- Government Agency
- Workforce Board
- Faith-Based Organization
- Other

**Which sponsorship tier are you interested in?** (required — dropdown)
- Community Investor ($5,000)
- Pioneer Sponsor ($10,000)
- Bridge Builder ($25,000)
- Founding Partner ($50,000)
- Not sure yet — let's talk

**What motivated you to reach out?** (optional — text area)

**How did you hear about 50+TechBridge?** (optional — dropdown)
- LinkedIn
- Website
- Referral
- Conference/Event
- News/Media
- Other

**Preferred next step** (required — radio buttons)
- Schedule a 15-minute call
- Send me more information first
- I'm ready to commit — let's move forward

---

## After Submission

1. Auto-email to sponsor: "Thank you for your interest in sponsoring 50+TechBridge. Brian McKinney will follow up within 24 hours."
2. Auto-email to Brian: New sponsor inquiry with all form data
3. Add contact to HubSpot: Pipeline = Sponsors, Stage = Inquiry Received
4. If they selected "Schedule a call" → redirect to https://cal.com/brian-mckinney-mrtu8q

---

## Where to Build

**Option 1: Google Forms (fastest, free)**
- Build form in Google Forms
- Embed on WordPress sponsor page
- Responses go to Google Sheets
- Set up email notification

**Option 2: WPForms (if already installed)**
- Build in WordPress → WPForms
- Embed directly on sponsor page
- Set up email notifications
- Connect to HubSpot if possible

**Option 3: Cal.com (simplest)**
- Skip the form entirely
- Just use the Cal.com booking link
- Add a "notes" field on the Cal.com event for them to share their org info
- This is what you're doing right now — it works

---

## Recommendation

Start with **Option 3 (Cal.com only)** — you're already doing this. The booking link IS the form. Add a custom question to your Cal.com event:

1. Log into Cal.com
2. Edit your meeting type
3. Add custom question: "What organization are you with and what sponsorship tier interests you?"
4. That captures their info before the call

Build the full form later when you have enough sponsor traffic to justify it.
