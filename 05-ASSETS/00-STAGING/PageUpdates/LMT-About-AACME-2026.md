## Official Purpose Statement

> Learn More Technologies exists so adults 50 and older can live, work, create, and connect in a digital world without being left behind. We exist to prove that age is not a barrier to technology — it is an asset — and that digital independence is a right, not a privilege reserved for the already fluent.

Too many capable people are locked out of AI, online services, creative tools, and economic opportunity because training was never designed for them. We built 50+TechBridge to close that gap in the places adults already gather: public libraries, senior centers, and cultural sites — free, in their language, at their pace, with instructors who respect their intelligence.

---

## Mission Statement

> Our mission is to close the digital divide for adults 50+ through free, hands-on AI and technology education delivered in community — so overlooked talent can create, stay safe online, remain employable, and participate fully in civic and cultural life.

We operate 50+TechBridge as that mission in practice: live workshops, bilingual English and Spanish materials, a creative-output model where participants make real work, and a methodology grounded in dignity, accessibility, and community.

Participants are **Digital Pioneers** — not beginners to be talked down to.

## Core Values

**Dignity**
No participant should feel embarrassed for not knowing something. Instruction is patient, respectful, and built for adult intelligence — never for a stereotype of age.

**Accessibility**
Programming is free, offered in English and Spanish, and delivered at neighborhood locations people already trust. Cost, transportation, and jargon should not be the barrier.

**Community**
Technology education is most powerful when it happens together. We teach in libraries and cultural centers, and we treat lived experience as an asset AI can amplify — not replace.

---

## What We Do

**AI Training**
Free, hands-on Technology Assisted Trainings deployed across 9 locations in Central Texas — 6 Austin Public Library branches, 2 AARP-partnered senior centers, and a Foundation Communities veterans program. No coding. No jargon. No age limit. Customized to every community's needs.

**Advocacy**
We work with workforce boards, funders, and policymakers to advance digital equity for adults 50+. We publish Agentic50 Weekly — AI news translated for adults 50+, cutting through the noise to deliver what actually matters.

**Speaking**
Keynotes, Lunch & Learns, and workshops on ageism in tech, AI adoption for older adults, and the $76 trillion economic opportunity the market is ignoring. Available for libraries, conferences, workforce boards, and partner organizations.
