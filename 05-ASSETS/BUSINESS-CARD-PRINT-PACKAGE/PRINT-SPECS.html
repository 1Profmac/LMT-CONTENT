How to Order on Vistaprint

Step 1 — Start Your Order
Go tovistaprint.com → Business Cards
TemplateChoose "Upload your own design" (DO NOT use a Vistaprint template)
SizeStandard 3.5" x 2"
OrientationHorizontal
Step 2 — Upload Front
Option AUse their drag-and-drop editor: upload Final-50+AgenticLogo.png, place on left side, then add text fields on the right using the content from this spec sheet
Option BIf you have a full front design as one image (from Illustrator), upload that instead — export at 3.75" x 2.25" to include bleed
FontVistaprint has Montserrat — select it from their font list
Step 3 — Upload Back
Select"Add design to back" — same process
Left sideAdd text: 50+TechBridge, the quote, and hashtag line
Right sideUpload QR-CODE-learnmoretechnologies.png, add "SCAN TO CONNECT" below it
Step 4 — Select Options
PaperSelect "Heavyweight" or "Ultra Thick" — look for 32pt option (may be called "Premium" or "Luxe")
FinishMatte or Soft Touch Matte
CornersSelect "Rounded Corners"
Quantity250 recommended for first order
Step 5 — Review & Order
ProofALWAYS preview the digital proof before checkout — check for cut-off text, color accuracy, and alignment
TipZoom in on small text (email, phone, LinkedIn) to make sure it's readable
ShippingStandard is fine (7-10 days) unless you need rush
CouponCheck for promo codes — Vistaprint almost always has 25-50% off deals running
