## WHY FROST BANK (AND COMMUNITY BANKS)

### The CRA Case

The **Community Reinvestment Act (CRA)** requires banks to demonstrate investment in low- and moderate-income (LMI) communities. A digital literacy program for adults 50+ qualifies as a **community development service** and **community development investment** under OCC CRA guidelines because:

1. **Target population:** Adults 50+ are disproportionately represented in LMI households in Austin
2. **Digital divide impact:** 34% of adults 65+ do not use the internet (Pew Research, 2024)
3. **Economic mobility:** Digital skills directly reduce dependency on in-person banking services and enable online financial tools
4. **Geographic alignment:** Conley-Guerrero, Dove Springs, and East Austin senior centers serve CRA-targeted census tracts

**Frost Bank's community development priorities (public CRA plan):**
- Financial literacy
- Economic empowerment of underserved populations
- Workforce development

All three are directly served by 50+TechBridge.

### The Business Case for Frost Bank

- Adults 50+ across 13 locations trained = adults 50+ across 13 locations potential new Frost account holders
- Digital banking adoption for seniors reduces branch transaction costs
- Frost brand association with the fastest-growing demographic in Austin
- Austin's 50+ population: 280,000+ adults (and growing fastest in Travis County)

## WIOA FUNDING PATHWAY

**Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act (WIOA) — Title I Adult Program**

TEGL 03-25 (2025 federal guidance) explicitly authorizes WIOA Adult funds for AI and digital literacy training.

**Gate requirement:** ETPL (Eligible Training Provider List) credential
- 50+TechBridge currently issues certificates of participation (not industry-recognized)
- Credential partner needed: Google Career Certificates, Microsoft Fundamentals, or CompTIA IT Fundamentals+

**Contact:** Workforce Solutions Capital Area (Austin WIOA board)
