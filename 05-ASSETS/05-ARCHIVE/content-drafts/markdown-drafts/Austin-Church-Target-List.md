## HOW TO USE THIS LIST

Target: After-service fellowship lunch on Sunday (12–1pm) or Saturday service.
Ask: "Can I do a 45-minute tech talk during your fellowship lunch?"
Lead with: AARP background, Adults 50+ across 13 locations, free for congregation, no equipment needed.
Best contact: Call the church office Mon–Thu, 9am–3pm. Ask for Senior Ministry director or Pastor's admin.

## NOTES ON WEEKEND TIMING

**Sunday sweet spot:** After 11am service. Fellowship lunch runs 12–1pm.
Presentation goes 12:00–12:45pm. Members are seated, fed, nowhere to be.
Exactly the Conley-Guerrero model — proven to work.

**Saturday opportunity:**
- Riverbend: Saturday 5pm service → 6pm fellowship
- Beth Israel: Saturday Kiddush lunch after 10:30am Shabbat
- Austin Masjid: Saturday community events (call to confirm timing)
- Some Black Baptist churches: Saturday senior fellowship events (ask when calling)

**Do not schedule during:**
- Easter weekend
- Mother's Day Sunday (high attendance but chaotic)
- Christmas Eve/Day weekend
- First Sunday of the month (often communion — service runs longer)
