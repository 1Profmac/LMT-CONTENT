WHAT IS 50+TECHBRIDGE?

50+TechBridge is a free AI and digital literacy program for adults 50+ in Austin. We deliver 45-minute Lunch & Learn presentations at senior centers, churches, libraries, and community organizations — right during lunch, before bingo, in places they already trust.

No laptops required. No registration. No cost to attend.

Topics we cover: smartphones, video calling, online safety, telehealth, voice assistants, and using AI tools to stay independent.

After every session, every participant gets a free link to our full online course at learnmoretechnologies.com.

THE ONE THING I NEED

Right now I do everything alone. I schedule every event. I drive to every senior center. I present. I follow up. I manage every enrollment. I answer every email.

It works — Adults 50+ across 13 locations proves it works. But it doesn't scale.

The single thing your donation makes possible is helping me hire a part-time assistant. Twenty hours a week. Someone to help with scheduling, event setup, materials, and follow-up.

Here's what I figured out: senior centers are open Monday through Friday at lunch. But churches, mosques, and synagogues host fellowship lunches on Saturday and Sunday — same format, same captive audience, different day.

That means I can run events 7 days a week if I have the operational support to do it.

Solo, I can realistically do 10 events a month — 5 senior centers on weekdays, 5 faith communities on weekends. That's 1,200 Pioneers a year by myself.

With one part-time assistant handling scheduling, materials, and follow-up — I can double that to 20 events a month and 2,400 Pioneers a year.

That's the entire ask. Not a building. Not a staff of ten. One person, part-time, 20 hours a week.
