# Daily Playbook — Pipeline Contractor

## Who We Are
Learn More Technologies is a Texas MBE that delivers AI and digital-skills work for agencies, libraries, and workforce partners. 50+TechBridge is the training brand: hands-on sessions for adults 50+ across 9 in-person locations in Central Texas — 6 Austin Public Library branches, AARP-affiliated senior centers, and a Foundation Communities veterans program. Founder Brian McKinney is a 65-year-old solo CEO who runs 30+ AI-powered workflows. We are WIOA-eligible and a City of Austin Nexus Grant recipient. Keep contractor language on invoices; do not mix grant or “always free” language into SOWs.

## Why You're Here
Brian is a solo founder. When he stops, everything stops — pipeline, content, outreach, grants. That cannot happen again. You are the person who makes sure the business keeps moving every day, whether Brian is in the office, on the road, or unavailable. You own the daily rhythm. You are not an assistant — you are the pipeline.

## The Job in One Sentence
Keep the pipeline warm, the content visible, and the grants tracked so Brian never loses momentum.

## Daily Routine (35 min)

### 1. HubSpot Pipeline (10 min)
- Open HubSpot. Check every deal in "Active" stages.
- If a contact hasn't been touched in 3+ days, send a follow-up.
- If someone replied, move them to the next stage.
- If a discovery call is warranted, send them the Cal.com link: [INSERT LINK]
- Add any new contacts from yesterday's LinkedIn activity.
- Log every touch. If it's not in HubSpot, it didn't happen.

**Follow-up templates:**

Warm lead (met at event or responded to outreach):
> Hi [Name], just circling back — would love to find 15 minutes to walk you through what we're doing with AI training for adults 50+. Here's my calendar: [Cal.com link]

Workforce board / funder:
> Hi [Name], following up on our conversation about [topic]. Happy to send over our one-pager or jump on a quick call. What works best for you?

Stalled deal (no response in 7+ days):
> Hi [Name], I know things get busy — just wanted to keep this on your radar. We're booking Q3 cohorts now and would love to include [their org]. Worth a quick chat?

### 2. LinkedIn Post (10 min)
- Check the content calendar: `Desktop/LMT-CONTENT/06-MARKETING/master-claude-series/4-WEEK-CONTENT-CALENDAR.md`
- Post today's content. If it's a carousel day, the file is in Canva (Brian will share the Canva team link).
- Copy format: short hook line, then the body, then CTA with booking link.
- Every post ends with a CTA. Always. Options:
  - "Book a free discovery call: [Cal.com link]"
  - "DM me 'AI' and I'll send you the details"
  - "Join the next cohort: [website link]"
- After posting, check notifications. Reply to every comment within 24 hrs.

**Posting voice rules:**
- First person, as Brian. Confident but warm. Never corporate.
- Short sentences. No jargon. Write like you're talking to a smart person who doesn't live online.
- Never use: "leverage," "synergy," "ecosystem," "revolutionize"
- OK to use: "Here's what I learned," "This is what works," "Real talk"

### 3. Facebook Mirror (5 min)
- Copy the LinkedIn post to Facebook. Same text, same image/carousel.
- Post to Brian's profile and any relevant FB groups (list below).
- That's it. Don't overthink Facebook. It's a mirror, not a strategy.

**FB Groups to post in:**
- [Brian to provide list of active groups]

### 4. Follow-Up & DMs (10 min)
- Check LinkedIn DMs. Respond to everything.
- Check LinkedIn profile viewers. If anyone relevant viewed the profile, send a connection request with a note:
  > Hey [Name], saw you checked out my profile — I run AI training programs for adults 50+ through libraries, senior centers, and workforce boards. Would love to connect.
- Check for any new connection accepts from previous outreach. Send a follow-up:
  > Thanks for connecting! Quick question — does your organization serve adults 50+? We're doing some interesting work with AI literacy I'd love to tell you about.
- Add every meaningful contact to HubSpot with a note about where they came from.

## Weekly: Grant Watch (30 min, once per week — pick a day)

### Where to Look
1. **SAM.gov** — search: "digital literacy," "workforce training," "older adults," "senior technology"
2. **Grants.gov** — same search terms
3. **Texas Workforce Commission** — check news/announcements page
4. **Local workforce board sites** — Workforce Solutions Capital Area, Rural Capital Area
5. **Google Alerts** — Brian has alerts set up; check the inbox for flagged opportunities

### What to Capture (add to Grant Tracker spreadsheet)
| Field | Example |
|-------|---------|
| Grant name | WIOA Title I Adult Training |
| Source | SAM.gov |
| Dollar amount | $25,000-$50,000 |
| Deadline | July 15, 2026 |
| Fit score (1-5) | 4 — workforce + digital literacy, perfect fit |
| Status | Flagged / Brian Reviewing / Submitted / Pending |
| Notes | Requires 501c3 or fiscal sponsor |

### Follow-Up on Submitted Grants
- Check status of anything marked "Submitted" or "Pending"
- If a decision was expected and hasn't come, email the contact person
- Update the tracker with every change

## Daily Status Report (End of Shift)

Send Brian a message (Slack, email, or shared doc — TBD) with exactly 3 lines:

1. **Posted:** [What you posted and where]
2. **Pipeline:** [Who you followed up with, any replies, any new contacts added]
3. **Flag:** [Anything that needs Brian's decision or attention]

Example:
> 1. Posted: LinkedIn carousel "5 AI Tools for Adults 50+" + mirrored to FB
> 2. Pipeline: Followed up with 3 stalled contacts, 1 replied and booked a call for Thursday. Added 2 new connections to HubSpot.
> 3. Flag: Found a WIOA RFP due July 20, $35K, strong fit. Need you to review.

If there's nothing to flag, write "No flags." Don't skip the report.

## Rules

1. **Never send a message Brian hasn't approved the tone of.** Use the templates above. If you need to go off-script, ask first — until Brian says you've earned freestyle.
2. **Every post gets a CTA.** No exceptions.
3. **Every contact goes in HubSpot.** No sticky notes, no "I'll remember."
4. **Never inflate numbers.** If you reference stats, use only what's in `06-CONTRACTS-AND-GRANTS/ELEVATE-2026/05-PROOF-POINTS.md`. When in doubt, lead with locations: "Across 9 in-person locations in Central Texas." Never use 264+, 347+, 13 locations, 3x completion, 74% confidence, or “only program in America.”
5. **Ask before you act** on anything outside this playbook. Better to ask a dumb question than make a wrong move.
