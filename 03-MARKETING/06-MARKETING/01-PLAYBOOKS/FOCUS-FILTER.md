# Brian's Focus Filter

## The One Identity
I train adults 50+ on AI through workforce contracts and community partnerships.

## The One Goal (Q3 2026)
Close 3 workforce contracts.

## The One Chain
LinkedIn → Leads → HubSpot → Discovery Call → Contract Signed

If it doesn't feed this chain, it waits.

---

## The Focus Filter — Run Every New Idea Through This

Before you start ANYTHING new, answer these 5 questions:

### 1. Does this lead to a signed contract in the next 90 days?
- YES → Do it.
- NO → Stop. Go to question 2.

### 2. Does this put me in front of someone who can sign a contract?
(Workforce board director, library system decision-maker, funder, senior center program manager)
- YES → Do it.
- NO → Stop. Go to question 3.

### 3. Is someone asking me for this and willing to pay?
- YES → Do it.
- NO → Stop. Go to question 4.

### 4. Will this take less than 1 hour and I can finish it today?
- YES → Fine, do it and move on. Do not let it become a project.
- NO → Put it on the SOMEDAY list and walk away.

### 5. Am I excited about this because it's strategic — or because it's new and shiny?
Be honest. If it's shiny, write it down and come back in 30 days. If you still care, reconsider. You probably won't.

# Contract Sprint — Close 3 Contracts by September 30, 2026

## Week 1: Rebuild Momentum (June 12-18)

### Monday
- [ ] Open HubSpot. List every contact who was warm before bereavement.
- [ ] Send a personal "I'm back" message to the top 5 warmest leads.
  > Hey [Name], I took some time away for a family matter and I'm back at it. Would love to pick up where we left off. Still interested in exploring AI training for your team? Here's my calendar: [Cal.com]
- [ ] Post on LinkedIn: "I took 3 weeks away. Here's what I came back to." (Honest, human, builds connection. End with CTA.)

### Tuesday
- [ ] Identify 10 target organizations you have NOT contacted yet:
  - 3 workforce boards (Texas Workforce Commission regions you haven't pitched)
  - 3 library systems (outside Austin — San Antonio, Houston, Dallas)
  - 2 senior living / CCRC communities
  - 2 Area Agencies on Aging
- [ ] Add all 10 to HubSpot as new deals in "Research" stage.

### Wednesday
- [ ] Send outreach to 5 of the 10 new targets. Use your existing outreach email templates.
- [ ] Post Upwork job for pipeline contractor.

### Thursday
- [ ] Send outreach to the remaining 5 new targets.
- [ ] Follow up on any replies from Monday's "I'm back" messages.

### Friday
- [ ] Review: How many conversations are active? Update HubSpot stages.
- [ ] Post LinkedIn content. End the week visible.

## Week 2: Fill the Funnel (June 19-25)

- [ ] Review Upwork applicants. Shortlist 3. Interview top 2.
- [ ] Send follow-up #1 to all 10 new targets from Week 1 (anyone who didn't reply).
- [ ] Book at least 2 discovery calls for Week 3.
- [ ] Run grant watch: flag any RFPs with deadlines in July-August.
- [ ] LinkedIn daily — do not skip a single day.

## Week 3: Start Closing (June 26 - July 2)

- [ ] Contractor starts (2-week trial). Hand off daily playbook.
- [ ] Hold discovery calls. Come with the one-pager and a specific proposal.
- [ ] For any call that goes well, send a proposal within 24 hours. Not next week. Tomorrow.
- [ ] Follow up on any flagged grants. Start application if deadline is within 30 days.

## Weeks 4-8: Maintain Pressure (July)

- [ ] Contractor handles daily pipeline + LinkedIn + grant watch.
- [ ] Brian focuses on: discovery calls, proposals, and closing.
- [ ] Target: 1 signed contract by end of July.
- [ ] Every Friday: review pipeline with contractor. How many active deals? What's stalled? What needs Brian's attention?

## Weeks 9-12: Close the Quarter (August - September)

- [ ] Target: 2 more signed contracts.
- [ ] Use first contract as social proof: "We just signed with [org]" → LinkedIn post → outreach leverage.
- [ ] Apply to at least 2 grants with contractor support.
- [ ] End of September: 3 contracts signed = revenue flowing = you earned the right to think about the book, podcast, and platform again.
