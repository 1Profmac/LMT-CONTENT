# Upwork Job Posting — Pipeline & Business Development Contractor

## Title
AI-Literate Business Development Contractor — 50+ EdTech Nonprofit (Part-Time, Remote)

## Description

### Who We Are
50+TechBridge (Learn More Technologies) is the only program in America teaching AI skills specifically to adults 50+. While everyone else is still teaching email, we teach AI. We are deployed across 13 locations in Central Texas — 9 library branches, 3 senior centers, and a veterans program — delivering free, hands-on AI training that is customized to each community we serve. We are WIOA-eligible, MBE-certified, and actively pursuing workforce contracts, government grants, and institutional partnerships. Our founder is a 65-year-old solo CEO who manages 30+ AI-powered workflows and is living proof that age is no barrier to innovation.

### What We Need
We are seeking a sharp, AI-literate pipeline operator — not an assistant, not a scheduler, not someone who "wants to learn." We need someone who already knows how to work a CRM, run LinkedIn outreach, track government RFPs, and follow up without being told. You will be the engine that keeps our business development moving every single day — so that when life happens, the pipeline never goes cold. This role exists because a solo founder cannot be the only point of failure. You are the insurance policy against lost momentum.

### What This Is Not
This is not an entry-level gig. This is not "post on social media and call it a day." You will be managing real deal flow in HubSpot, writing follow-ups as the founder, flagging grant opportunities with deadlines and dollar amounts, and keeping warm leads from going cold. If you don't already know what Claude is, how LinkedIn outreach actually works, or what a WIOA contract looks like — this isn't for you.

### What You'll Do

**Pipeline Management (HubSpot)**
- Work the deal pipeline daily — move contacts through stages, send follow-ups, flag stalled deals
- Add new contacts from LinkedIn conversations, inbound inquiries, and events
- Book discovery calls on Cal.com when leads are warm
- Send a daily 3-line status update: what you posted, who you followed up with, what needs a decision

**LinkedIn & Social (Buffer / Native)**
- Post pre-written content to LinkedIn and Facebook on schedule (content is already created)
- Respond to comments and DMs within 24 hours
- Send connection requests and follow-up messages to target contacts (workforce boards, library directors, senior center managers, funders)
- Monitor engagement and flag hot leads

**Grant Watch & Follow-Up**
- Monitor SAM.gov, Grants.gov, Texas Workforce Commission, and local RFP boards weekly
- Flag relevant opportunities with deadline, dollar amount, and fit score
- Track submitted applications and follow up on pending decisions
- Maintain a running grant calendar so nothing slips through the cracks

### Requirements — Non-Negotiable

- Proficient with Claude (Anthropic) — you use AI tools daily in your own work, not just "familiar with ChatGPT"
- Experienced LinkedIn operator — you've done outreach, built pipelines, or managed a founder's presence before
- HubSpot CRM experience (or equivalent — Salesforce, Pipedrive) — you know how to work a deal pipeline
- Grant research experience — you know where to look, how to read an RFP, and how to track deadlines
- Self-starter who can work independently with minimal supervision
- Strong written English — you'll be writing as the founder in DMs and follow-ups
- Available for a minimum of 10 hours/week, flexible schedule

### Novice need not apply. We need someone who can hit the ground running.

### Nice to Have
- Experience in workforce development, education, or nonprofit sectors
- Familiarity with WIOA-funded programs
- Experience with Buffer, Canva, or Mailchimp
- Located in the U.S. (not required, but helpful for timezone alignment)

## Details

- **Hours:** 10 hrs/week to start, potential to grow
- **Rate:** $20-30/hr depending on experience
- **Duration:** Ongoing, starting with a 2-week paid trial
- **Location:** 100% Remote
- **Tools we use:** HubSpot, LinkedIn, Buffer, Claude, Cal.com, WordPress, Canva, Google Workspace

Send a short note (not a cover letter novel) that answers:

1. What AI tools do you use daily and how?
2. Show me one example of LinkedIn outreach or pipeline work you've done.
3. Have you tracked or supported grant applications before? For what type of org?
