## THREE POWER PRACTICES

### 1. Hook in the First 2 Lines
LinkedIn cuts off after 2 lines with "...see more." Those 2 lines decide everything.

DON'T: "I'm excited to share our new article about deploying 50+TechBridge..."
DO: "13 organizations invited us in. Every one of them said the same thing before we started:"

The click on "see more" tells the algorithm your post is worth showing to more people.

### 2. Comments Are Content — Not "Nice Post"
Leave SUBSTANTIVE comments on other people's posts 3-5 times per day. Each comment puts you in front of their entire audience.

Example — on an AARP post about digital literacy:
"This is exactly why we built 50+TechBridge. The programs designed for younger learners don't work for adults 50+. We changed the model — peer instructors, project-based learning, no jargon — and the results speak for themselves. Book a call if your organization wants to deploy: https://cal.com/brian-mckinney-mrtu8q"

NEVER leave "great post!" or "love this!" — that gets you nothing.

### 3. Carousel Posts Get 3X More Engagement
Carousel/document posts get 2-3x more dwell time than text or image posts. The algorithm rewards time spent reading.

You already have carousels built (06-MARKETING/carousel_slides/). Post one per week. Each slide should end with a reason to swipe to the next one.
