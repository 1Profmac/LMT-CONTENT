## HOW TO SCHEDULE IN BUFFER

1. Go to buffer.com
2. Click "+ New" (green button, top left)
3. Select all 3 channels (LinkedIn, Facebook, YouTube) — or just LinkedIn + Facebook for text posts
4. Paste the post text
5. Set the date and time:
   - Monday: 9:00 AM CT
   - Tuesday: 10:00 AM CT
   - Wednesday: 9:00 AM CT
   - Thursday: 11:00 AM CT
   - Friday: 8:30 AM CT
6. Click "Schedule Post"
7. Repeat for each day

All 5 posts can be scheduled in one sitting (~15 minutes).
