## EPISODE 4: "Stop Writing Prompts"
**Platform: YouTube Short (90 sec) + LinkedIn post**

### YouTube Short Script

You don't need to write prompts. I know that sounds crazy. The whole internet is trying to teach you prompt engineering. Forget it.

Here's what you do instead. Build a prompt template. It's a simple set of instructions that you paste in every time. It says — read my About Me file, follow my style guide, write to my output folder, name the file this way.

Then instead of you writing a paragraph of instructions, just tell Claude what you need. Or better yet, let Claude ask you.

Say — I need a weekly report. Ask me questions before you start. Claude asks you five or six specific questions. You click your answers. Takes less than a minute. Then Claude builds it.

You don't need to be a prompt engineer. You're a decision-maker. That's a skill you've been building for thirty years. Turns out it's the most important AI skill there is.

**Platform: YouTube Short (90 sec) + LinkedIn post**

Install one. Just one. Pick the one that matches what you do most.

If you write content all day, install the Marketing plugin. If you live in spreadsheets, install the Data plugin. If you review contracts, install Legal.

Now Claude doesn't just work inside its own window. It reaches into your tools. It searches your email. It pulls from your documents. It references your notes in the middle of a task.

One plugin. One connector. That's today.
