# APPLY TO WORK WITH BRIAN — Pre-Qualification Form

**Purpose:** Filter prospects before they get Brian's calendar link. Saves time. Looks premium. Blocks looky-loos.
**Rule:** Calendly link is NEVER public. Brian sends it only to qualified applicants.

---

## GOOGLE FORM QUESTIONS

### Form Title:
**Apply to Work With Brian — 50+TechBridge**

### Form Description:
I work with a limited number of organizations and individuals at a time. This short application helps me understand your needs so I can tell you honestly if we're a fit. I review every application personally and respond within 48 hours.

---

### Q1: Your Name
- Type: Short answer
- Required: Yes

### Q2: Your Email
- Type: Short answer
- Required: Yes

### Q3: Organization Name
- Type: Short answer
- Required: Yes
- Help text: "Type 'Individual' if you're looking for private coaching"

### Q4: What does your organization do?
- Type: Short answer
- Required: Yes
- Help text: "One sentence is fine. Example: 'We're a public library system serving 3 counties in Central Texas.'"

### Q5: Which best describes what you need?
- Type: Multiple choice
- Required: Yes
- Options:
  - Partner training for my organization's members
  - A speaker or workshop for an event
  - Private one-on-one coaching
  - I'm not sure yet — I want to learn more

### Q6: How many adults 50+ does your organization serve?
- Type: Multiple choice
- Required: Yes
- Options:
  - Under 50
  - 50–200
  - 200–1,000
  - 1,000+
  - N/A — I'm looking for private coaching

### Q7: What's your timeline?
- Type: Multiple choice
- Required: Yes
- Options:
  - Ready now
  - Next 30 days
  - Next 90 days
  - Just exploring

### Q8: Anything else I should know?
- Type: Paragraph
- Required: No
- Help text: "Optional — but the more I know, the better I can help."

- Confirmation message: "Thanks for applying. I review every application personally and respond within 48 hours. If we're a good fit, I'll send you a link to schedule a call. — Brian"

---

## BRIAN'S FILTER (2 minutes per application)

When an application comes in, check Q5 + Q6 + Q7:

### HOT LEAD (respond same day)
- Q5 = Partner training OR Speaker/workshop
- Q6 = 50+ members
- Q7 = Ready now OR Next 30 days
- **Action:** Send personal email with Calendly link

### WARM LEAD (respond within 48 hours)
- Q5 = Partner training OR Private coaching
- Q7 = Next 90 days
- **Action:** Send personal email, suggest a call when they're ready

### NURTURE (add to newsletter)
- Q5 = "Not sure" OR "Just exploring"
- **Action:** Add to Mailchimp with tag `lead-nurture`, send free course link

### NOT A FIT (polite decline)
- Org doesn't serve 50+ adults
- **Action:** "Thanks for reaching out. We're focused on organizations serving adults 50+. Here's our free course if it helps: [link]"
