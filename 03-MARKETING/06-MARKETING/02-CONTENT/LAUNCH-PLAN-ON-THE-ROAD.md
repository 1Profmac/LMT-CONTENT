## What You Need to Build in Canva (1 batch session, ~2 hours)

### 7 Episode Carousels
Each episode gets its own carousel (8-10 slides each). Build all 7 in one sitting.

**Template to create in Canva:**
- Size: 1080 x 1350 (LinkedIn document)
- Background: Navy (#1B2A4A)
- Accent: Gold (#C8A42C)
- Font: Clean sans-serif (Montserrat or similar)
- Your headshot on slide 1 of each carousel
- Series badge on every slide: "HOW TO KEEP YOUR JOB OVER 50 | SERIES"

**Slide structure for each episode carousel:**

| Slide | Content |
|-------|---------|
| 1 | Hook + Episode # + series badge + your photo |
| 2-3 | The problem / stat / tension |
| 4-7 | The steps / how-to / breakdown |
| 8 | Key takeaway (bold, memorable) |
| 9 | CTA: follow #agentic50 / link to worksheets (Ep 3) / link to course (Ep 7) |

**Episode-by-episode carousel content** (pull from mini-series-scripts.md):

1. **"They're Coming for Your Job"** — 41% stat, who's really getting cut, "this week I'm showing you what to do about it"
2. **"You're Using AI Wrong"** — Chat vs Cowork, the mode nobody finds, "tomorrow: the setup that replaces 50 prompts"
3. **"Two Files That Replace 50 Prompts"** — about-me.md, anti-ai-style.md, banned phrases list, FREE WORKSHEET CTA
4. **"Stop Writing Prompts"** — prompt template, 4-line setup, let Claude prompt you, decision-maker mindset
5. **"One Plugin Changed Everything"** — plugins vs connectors, pick one, connect one, "comment which tool you'd pick"
6. **"I Woke Up to a Finished Deliverable"** — /schedule, automation, morning briefing story, "check my profile for the full series"
7. **"You're Not Too Old"** — recap all 7 days, transformation, course CTA with 3 price tiers

### Canva Batch Workflow
1. Create one carousel template with your brand (navy/gold/headshot/series badge)
2. Duplicate it 6 times (7 total)
3. Fill in each with the content above
4. Download all 7 as PDFs
5. Save to `06-MARKETING/master-claude-series/carousels/`
6. Done. You now have 7 days of content ready to post.
