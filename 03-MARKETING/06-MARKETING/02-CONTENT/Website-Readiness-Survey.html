Business Website Readiness Survey

Before you build — find out if you're truly ready.

A Website Is Like a New Puppy

Everyone wants one. It's exciting at first. But puppies need feeding, training, vet visits, walks, and daily attention — or they make a mess of your house. A website is no different. Without regular care, it becomes outdated, unsecured, and invisible to the people you're trying to reach.

Answer honestly. There are no wrong answers — only expensive surprises.

Not Ready? Here Are Your Three Low-Maintenance Options

If your score is below 110 — a full website will cost you money without results. Start here.

These are digital address strategies — Home, About, and Contact only. No plugins to update. No content strategy required. Just show up, look professional, and be findable.
Ordered by level of importance.

1
Google Business Profile
Free — runs on Google's own infrastructure
Most Important

Shows up in Maps and Search immediately. Includes your hours, photos, phone number, reviews, and a booking link. For most local businesses, this IS your website — and it outperforms many paid sites.

  • Completely free
  • Managed from your phone
  • Appears instantly in local Google search results
  • Customers can call, message, or book directly
  • No hosting, no maintenance, no monthly cost
2
Linktree or Carrd — Single-Page Link Hub
$0 – $9/month — 30 minutes to set up
High Importance

A single page with your name, what you do, and links to everything — phone, email, social profiles, booking link, and your Google Business Profile.

  • Linktree — social-first, built for Instagram and Facebook bios
  • Carrd — clean single-page site with a custom domain option
  • Both are mobile-friendly out of the box
  • Zero maintenance after initial setup
  • Works as a professional landing page until you're ready to grow
3
Squarespace or Wix — 3-Page Template Site
$16 – $23/month — only if you need a branded domain address
Moderate Importance

Pick a pre-built template. Launch it as-is with your name, logo, phone, and a photo. Do not customize beyond that. Resist the urge to add more until you're ready.

  • Squarespace — cleaner design, $16–$23/month, all-in-one hosting
  • Wix — more flexible templates, free tier available, $17+/month for custom domain
  • Built-in hosting, SSL, and mobile optimization included
  • Minimal maintenance if you skip the blog and store
  • Best for service businesses that just need to look credible online

The Bottom Line

A website is not a finish line — it's a starting gun. The businesses that win online are not the ones with the most expensive sites. They are the ones that show up consistently, update regularly, and treat their online presence like a living part of their business.

Start where you are. Grow into the rest.
