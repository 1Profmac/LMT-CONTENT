# Ageism Campaign Image — Canva AI Prompt

## The Powerful Version

A photorealistic image of a woman in her late 60s sitting alone in a dimly lit living room in a comfortable chair. On her left side, a nightstand is covered with prescription pill bottles, at least eight or ten of them. On her right side, a smartphone sits on the armrest, screen completely dark and untouched. In the background, a TV glows with a pharmaceutical commercial showing happy people running through a field. She is looking down at her hands, not at the TV, not at the phone. Warm but melancholy lighting. The mood is quiet isolation. Photorealistic, editorial style, cinematic lighting.

## Design Notes

- Do NOT make the person look frail or helpless — she should look capable but UNSEEN
- The pill bottles should feel excessive, almost absurd in quantity
- The phone should be close enough to touch but ignored
- This is not about pity — it's about injustice
