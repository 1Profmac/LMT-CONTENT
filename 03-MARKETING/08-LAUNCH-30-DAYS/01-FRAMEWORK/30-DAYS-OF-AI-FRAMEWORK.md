# 30 Days of AI — Challenge Framework

## The Concept

A 30-day guided challenge for adults 50+ to go from "I don't use my phone" to "I just used AI." Free entry. Companion podcast (Agentic50). Weekly newsletter. Daily micro-content on social.

**Tagline:** "30 days. One phone. A whole new you."

---

## The Structure

| Week | Theme | Lesson | Podcast Episode | Newsletter |
|------|-------|--------|-----------------|------------|
| Week 1 (Days 1-7) | Your Phone | Lesson 1: Make It Easier to Read | Ep 1: Your Phone is Smarter Than You Think | Edition: Lesson 1 recap + tool |
| Week 2 (Days 8-14) | Your Tools | Lesson 2: 3 Tools That Save You Time | Ep 2: 3 Tools I Wish Someone Showed Me | Edition: Lesson 2 recap + tool |
| Week 3 (Days 15-21) | Your Independence | Lesson 3: Stay Independent | Ep 3: How Tech Keeps You Independent | Edition: Lesson 3 recap + tool |
| Week 4 (Days 22-30) | Your AI | AI Intro: Meet ChatGPT | Ep 4: Meet AI — It's Not Scary | Edition: AI intro + next steps |
