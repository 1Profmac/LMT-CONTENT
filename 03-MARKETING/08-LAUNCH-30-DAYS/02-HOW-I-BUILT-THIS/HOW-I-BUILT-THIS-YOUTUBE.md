## SCRIPT (plain spoken words — no stage directions)

People were signing up for my course. Nobody was finishing.

I teach adults over 50 how to use their phones. Basic things. Making text bigger. Setting reminders. Downloading their pharmacy app. Skills that keep people independent.

But my lessons looked like a college course. Twenty-minute video of me talking. Paragraphs of text. A ten-question quiz at the end.

It felt like school. And people over 50 don't want to go back to school.

So I gave myself one weekend to rebuild everything using AI. Here's exactly what I did.

Tool number one. I used Claude, the AI assistant, to write my lesson scripts. But not in my voice. I wrote them in the voice of a fifty-five-year-old woman. Warm. Friendly. Like a neighbor showing you something over the fence.

Claude also helped me build a persona document. I stopped calling people students. I started calling them Digital Pioneers. That one change shifted how I wrote everything.

Tool number two. I used HeyGen to create an AI avatar instructor. Her name is Barb. She's in her late fifties. Southern accent. Blue sweater. She looks like the people taking the course.

I pasted the script into HeyGen. Three minutes later I had a video that sounds like a real person talking to you at your kitchen table.

Tool number three. I used Canva to build visual walkthroughs. Instead of recording my phone screen, which is always shaky and messy, I built each step as a slide. A phone mockup with gold arrows showing exactly where to tap. One action per slide.

The last slide has confetti falling and says You're a Digital Pioneer.

Nobody wants a quiz score of three out of three. But everybody wants confetti.

Here are the results. My old lessons took twenty minutes and almost nobody finished. My new lessons take five minutes and the completion rate is now at [INSERT NUMBER].

Here's what I learned. Number one. The content was never the problem. The format was the problem.

Number two. AI didn't replace my team. I never had a team. I'm a solo founder. AI made it possible to build what I couldn't build alone.

Number three. Ship one lesson first. Watch what happens. Build the rest based on what you learn, not what you imagine.

Number four. Celebrate louder than you test. I did it beats score three out of three every single time.

And number five. Give people an identity. Digital Pioneer finishes courses. Student number four thousand eight hundred twenty seven does not.

If you're building courses, training programs, or any kind of education, the tools I just showed you cost less than hiring one freelancer for one day. And they work overnight.

If you work with adults over fifty and you want help redesigning your training the way I just described, that's what my company does. Link is in the description.

I'm Brian McKinney. I run 50+TechBridge. We've trained over Digital Pioneers across 13 locations across twelve locations in Texas. And we're just getting started.

I'll see you in the next one.
