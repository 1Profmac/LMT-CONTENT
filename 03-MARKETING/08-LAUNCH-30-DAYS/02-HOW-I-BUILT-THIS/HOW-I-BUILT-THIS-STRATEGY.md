### What to Post in Groups

**DO NOT drop a link and leave. That gets you banned.**

Follow the 3-post sequence:

**Post 1 (Day 13) — Ask a question (no link):**
```
Honest question for this group: How much of your smartphone do you actually use? I've been teaching adults over 50 how to use their phones and most people tell me about 10%. Is that true for you?
```
Wait for comments. Reply to every single one.

**Post 2 (Day 14) — Share the story (soft link):**
```
I teach adults over 50 how to use their phones. Last week I completely rebuilt my first lesson because nobody was finishing it. Made it 5 minutes long with a friendly guide who walks you through it step by step. If anyone wants to try it, it's free. Happy to share the link.
```
Only share the link when someone asks for it in the comments.

**Post 3 (Day 15) — Share a result (with link):**
```
Update: [X] people tried our redesigned lesson this week. Takes 5 minutes. One person told me they made their text bigger for the first time in 3 years. If you want to try it: [LINK]. It's free and there's no signup required to look.
```

### Facebook Reels

Repurpose the same content from Instagram:
- Barb 30-sec intro clip
- Before/after phone screenshot (text size)
- Brian 60-sec "why we redesigned" selfie video

Post these as Facebook Reels — they get more reach than regular posts on FB right now.

## RULES FOR FACEBOOK GROUPS

1. **Join groups 1 week before launch** — most need admin approval
2. **Never post a link in your first post** — ask a question first
3. **Reply to every comment** — engagement = algorithm love
4. **Follow each group's rules** — some ban self-promotion, so frame it as helpful
5. **Max 2-3 groups per day** — don't spam, build presence
6. **Screenshot good comments** — use as testimonials later
7. **If a group admin contacts you** — offer to do a free live session in the group (this is gold)
