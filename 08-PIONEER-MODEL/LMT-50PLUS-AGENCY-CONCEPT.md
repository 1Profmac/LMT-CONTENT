# LMT Advisory — Exploratory Concept
## Positioning LMT as the Premier Agency for Marketing to 50+ Adults
*Drafted: August 21, 2026 | Status: Exploratory — Internal Use Only*

---

## The Idea

LMT Advisory positions itself as the only marketing intelligence firm built from the inside of the 50+ adult community — not studying it from the outside.

Most agencies that claim to "reach seniors" are guessing. They use stock photography, surface-level personas, and 30-year-old strategists who've never had a real conversation with this customer.

LMT's advantage: we don't guess. We work with this demographic every day.

---

## The Market Case

- 50+ adults control **70% of US disposable income**
- They represent a **$28 trillion** consumer market largely ignored by mainstream agencies
- Most brands either patronize this demographic or ignore it entirely
- No well-known boutique agency owns this space
- The window to claim this positioning is open right now

## The Differentiator

Brian McKinney has spent years running active programs with 264+ adults across 13 locations in Central Texas (9 libraries, 3 senior centers). He has watched — up close — how this demographic:

That is earned intelligence. It cannot be replicated with a panel, a survey, or a junior analyst.

## Brand Architecture Consideration

Current LMT brand flows inward — serving 50+ adults directly:
```
LMT (company) → 50+TechBridge (mission) → Digital Pioneer (brand)
```

This new positioning flows outward — serving companies that want to reach 50+ adults:
```
LMT Advisory → [Corporate Clients] → 50+ Adult Market
```

These two audiences must be kept separate. Options:
- **Sub-brand:** LMT Advisory (distinct from 50+TechBridge)
- **Separate landing page:** advisory.lmt.com or lmtadvisory.com
- **Clear language split:** 50+TechBridge = community mission | LMT Advisory = B2B consulting

Decision needed before going to market.

## Service Model (Lean / Solo-Founder-Compatible)

### Service 1 — The 50+ Audit
- **What:** Review of a company's existing 50+ marketing (messaging, visuals, UX, tone, assumptions)
- **Deliverable:** Written audit report + 60-minute debrief call
- **Time:** ~8–10 hours
- **Price:** $3,500
- **Best for:** Entry-level clients, first engagements, quick wins

### Service 2 — The 50+ Strategy Sprint
- **What:** 4-week engagement to build a 50+ go-to-market strategy
- **Deliverable:** Audience profile, messaging framework, channel recommendations, 90-day action plan
- **Time:** ~20–25 hours
- **Price:** $8,500–$12,000
- **Best for:** Companies ready to invest in this market

### Service 3 — Community Research Access
- **What:** Facilitated focus groups or structured research sessions with Brian's real 50+ community
- **Deliverable:** Session facilitation + summary report with direct quotes and behavioral insights
- **Time:** ~15 hours
- **Price:** $5,000–$7,500 per session
- **Best for:** Product teams, campaign planners, UX researchers

### Service 4 — Monthly Advisory Retainer
- **What:** Brian as an on-call 50+ market expert
- **Deliverable:** Two calls/month + async access
- **Price:** $2,000/month
- **Capacity:** Max 2 retainer clients at a time
- **Best for:** Long-term relationships with ongoing campaigns

## Sales Motion (Solo-Founder Reality)

**Do not try to run a full agency.** The model must be:
- High ticket, low volume (2–4 clients at a time maximum)
- Fixed scope, not open-ended retainers (except advisory)
- Deliverables producible with AI assistance
- No hiring required to launch

**Activation sequence:**
1. Build one-page landing page for LMT Advisory
2. Identify 3 dream clients by name and company
3. Write tailored cold outreach for each target vertical
4. Start with audit ($3,500) — easy yes, gets Brian in the door
5. Upsell to Sprint or Retainer from there
