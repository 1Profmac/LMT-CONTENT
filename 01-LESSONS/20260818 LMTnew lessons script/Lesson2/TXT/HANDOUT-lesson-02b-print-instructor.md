# Lesson 2b — Printed Handout
## How to Ask Your First Real Question with ChatGPT

**FOR INSTRUCTOR USE — Add to Instructor Guide**
Print one per student. Hand out at the start of Lesson 2b.

---

> **No ChatGPT yet?** Open the Barb App and follow along there.
> You will do the exact same steps — Barb is already set up for you.

---

**What you will need:** Your smartphone
**Time:** 5 minutes

---

**Step 1: Tap the message box**
You have ChatGPT open. Tap the long empty bar at the bottom of the screen that says "Message."

---

**Step 2: Type your starter prompt**
Type this into the message box. Replace "your topic" with something you actually want to know about:

> I'm new to AI and I'm over 50. Please explain [your topic] in five simple steps, using clear language.

**Not sure what to ask? Use one of these:**

- how to video call my grandchildren
- how to plan a weekend road trip
- how to start a vegetable garden

*There is no wrong answer. Use what matters to you right now.*

---

**Step 3: Send it**
Press Enter or tap the arrow button. Then watch.

*Text will start appearing word by word. That is AI writing a response just for you.*

---

**Step 4: Read the whole answer**
Scroll to the top. Read slowly. It may be a few paragraphs. That is fine.

---

**Step 5: Ask yourself one question**
Is this useful? Not perfect — just useful. Is there something here you can actually use?

*If something seems off, ask a follow-up. That is Lesson 2c.*

---

**Step 6: The more specific you are, the better**
The starter prompt works because it gives AI context. The more detail you give, the better the response.

---

**Stuck?**
If the app asks you to log in, tap "Log in" and enter the email and password you used when you created your account.

---

*You are doing something that most people your age have never done.*
*That is what Digital Pioneers do.*
