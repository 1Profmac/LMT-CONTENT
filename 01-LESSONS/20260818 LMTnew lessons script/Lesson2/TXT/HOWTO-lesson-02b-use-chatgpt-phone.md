# How to Ask Your First Real Question with ChatGPT
What you'll need: A smartphone with ChatGPT open
Time: 5 minutes

---

Step 1: Tap the message box
You have ChatGPT open in front of you. That message box is waiting. Tap the long empty bar at the bottom of the screen that says "Message."

Step 2: Type your starter prompt
This is the exact wording used in every 50+TechBridge workshop. It works because it tells AI three things at once — that you are new, that you are over 50, and exactly how you want the answer formatted.

Type this into the message box. Where it says "your topic," fill in something you actually want to know about:

▎ I'm new to AI and I'm over 50. Please explain [your topic] in five simple steps, using clear language.

Not sure what to ask? Use one of these — or pick something that matters to you right now:

- how to video call my grandchildren
- how to plan a weekend road trip
- how to start a vegetable garden

There is no wrong answer.

Step 3: Send it
Press Enter or tap the arrow button. Then watch.

You'll know it worked when: Text starts appearing on the screen, word by word. That is AI writing a response specifically for you, right now. It is not pulling up a saved article. It is responding to exactly what you asked.

Step 4: Read the whole answer
Scroll to the top and read slowly. It may be a few paragraphs. That is fine. Take your time.

Step 5: Ask yourself one question
Is this useful? Not is it perfect. Not is it exactly right about everything. Just — is there something here you can actually use?

If yes, great. If something seems off, that is okay. Ask a follow-up. That is Lesson 2c.

Step 6: The more specific you are, the better the response
"How do I use AI" gets a general answer. The starter prompt gets something useful and specific. AI responds to context. The more detail you give, the closer it gets to exactly what you need.
