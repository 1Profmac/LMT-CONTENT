## SCENE — The Newest Costume Is Your Feed

[ON SCREEN]
FTC 2025
$2.1 billion lost
started on social media

Here is the number the headlines buried.

The Federal Trade Commission's 2025 report found that **$2.1 billion** in fraud losses started on social media. Not email. Not a phone call. Your feed.

And Facebook beat text messages and email as the starting point for fraud volume. The platform where your family photos live is now the number-one place scammers go to find you.

[ON SCREEN]
Social media fraud
Facebook leads in volume
Source: FTC 2025

I am not telling you to close your accounts. I am telling you the costume changed.

[ON SCREEN]
The costume on social media
Fake ads that look like real stores
Fake profiles pretending to be family
DMs from "friends" whose accounts were taken over
Fake giveaways and prize claims

The same rules apply. Who started this? Did you ask for this? Does the link go somewhere you recognize?

[ON SCREEN]
Before you click anything in your feed
Who started this contact?
Does this store or person have a real history?
Is there urgency or a prize?

A fake ad can look identical to a real one. A message from a friend's account may be a scammer who took it over.

The check: go directly to the real website by typing it yourself. Call the real person on the number you already have. Do not click the link in the post.

[PAUSE]

Your feed is not safer than your inbox. Treat it the same way.
