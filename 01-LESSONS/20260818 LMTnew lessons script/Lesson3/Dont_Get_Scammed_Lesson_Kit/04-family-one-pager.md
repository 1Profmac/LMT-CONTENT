## The One Rule on the Fridge

**Unexpected + hurry + money or a code = hang up and verify.**

If a call, text, or message arrives that you did not start, and it creates urgency around money or a code — stop. Hang up. Look up the real number. Call back.

---

## Set a Family Code Word This Week

Voice cloning is real. A scammer can call and sound like your grandchild, your parent, or your sibling. A few seconds of video or voicemail is enough.

Pick a word or short phrase **not on any social media profile.** Share it only with the people you would actually send money to. If a panicked caller cannot give the word — hang up, then call your person on the number in your phone.

## Payments That Should Make Your Hand Stop

No legitimate government agency, bank, or family emergency requires:
- Gift cards
- Wire transfers
- Crypto or Bitcoin ATMs
- A courier at the door
- Remote access to a computer

If someone is asking for these — it is a scam, every time.

---

## If Money Already Moved

1. Stop sending immediately
2. Call the bank — number on the card
3. Say wire / gift card / crypto if that is what was used
4. Change email password, turn on 2FA
5. Report: ReportFraud.ftc.gov and ic3.gov
6. Tell one trusted person — you are not alone

Speed matters. Silence guarantees no chance of recovery.
