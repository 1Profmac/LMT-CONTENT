# How to Rebuild Lessons 1-3: From College Course to Interactive Experience

## The Problem

People sign up but don't finish. The lessons LOOK like college courses — long text, passive watching, no reason to keep going. Adults 50+ don't need a lecture. They need a WIN in 5 minutes.

## The Fix: Every Lesson Gets 6 Pieces

Each lesson becomes a short, multi-format experience that hits different learning styles. No piece takes more than 5 minutes to consume.

| Piece | What It Is | Learning Style | Tool to Build It |
|-------|-----------|---------------|-----------------|
| 1. Pathway Graphic | Visual flowchart of the steps | Visual | Canva |
| 2. HeyGen Video | 60-sec AI guide avatar intro | Auditory | HeyGen |
| 3. Screen Recording | 90-sec over-the-shoulder demo | Kinesthetic + Visual | Phone screen recorder |
| 4. Activity Card | One hands-on task with checkbox | Kinesthetic | LearnDash + Canva |
| 5. Social Prompt | Share/show someone what you did | Social/Interpersonal | LearnDash text block |
| 6. Progress Tracker | "You completed 1 of 3 lessons" | Logical/Sequential | LearnDash progress bar |

## STEP-BY-STEP BUILD INSTRUCTIONS

---

### PIECE 1: Pathway Graphic (Canva — 15 min per lesson)

**What it is:** A single image showing the 2-4 steps as a visual map. Not a wall of text. A PICTURE of what they're about to do.

**Where to build:** Canva.com → Create Design → Custom 1200x628px

**How to build each one:**

#### Lesson 1 Pathway Graphic
1. Open Canva → search template "process infographic"
2. Pick a simple horizontal layout (3-4 circles connected by arrows)
3. Change the circles to:
   - Circle 1: "Settings" (add phone icon)
   - Circle 2: "Display → Text Size" (add slider icon)
   - Circle 3: "Accessibility → Bold Text" (add toggle icon)
   - Circle 4: "Done — Easier to Read" (add checkmark icon)
4. Title at top: "Your 5-Minute Path"
5. Use brand colors (dark blue + gold from 50+TechBridge)
6. Export as PNG → save to `REVISED-LESSONS-2/LESSON-01/lesson1-pathway.png`

#### Lesson 2 Pathway Graphic
1. Same Canva template, but 3 columns layout:
   - Column 1: "Voice Reminder" — microphone icon → "Remind me to..." → checkmark
   - Column 2: "Weather App" — sun icon → "Open it" → checkmark
   - Column 3: "Download 1 App" — app store icon → "Search + Get" → checkmark
2. Title: "Your 3 Time-Savers"
3. Export → save to `REVISED-LESSONS-2/LESSON-02/lesson2-pathway.png`

#### Lesson 3 Pathway Graphic
1. Same template, 3 options layout (like a menu):
   - Option A: "Share Location" — map pin icon
   - Option B: "Grocery App" — cart icon
   - Option C: "Doctor Portal" — medical icon
   - Center text: "Pick ONE"
2. Title: "One Step Toward Independence"
3. Export → save to `REVISED-LESSONS-2/LESSON-03/lesson3-pathway.png`

**Where it goes in LearnDash:** FIRST thing on the page, above the video. Digital Pioneer sees the map BEFORE they watch anything.

---

### PIECE 2: HeyGen Intro Video (HeyGen — 5 min per lesson)

**What it is:** 60-second AI avatar video. Female guide character. Warm, conversational tone.

**Where to build:** HeyGen.com

**How to build:**

1. Open HeyGen → Create Video → select your female guide avatar
2. Paste the script from HEYGEN-SCRIPTS.md (one lesson at a time)
3. Preview → render
4. Download MP4 → save to each lesson folder as `lesson[X]-heygen-intro.mp4`

**Rules:**
- Avatar should be mature (55-65), warm face, casual-professional look
- Scripts are plain spoken words only — no annotations or stage directions
- Each script ends with "Five minutes. Let's go." — creates urgency

**Where it goes in LearnDash:** Right below the pathway graphic. Auto-plays or shows thumbnail with play button.

---

### PIECE 3: Over-the-Shoulder Screen Recording (Phone — 10 min per lesson)

**What it is:** Your actual phone screen being recorded while you talk through the steps.

**Where to build:** Your phone's built-in screen recorder

**How to record:**

**iPhone:**
1. Settings → Control Center → add "Screen Recording"
2. Swipe down from top-right → tap the record button
3. Talk out loud while you do each step (use scripts from V2 files)
4. Stop recording → video saves to Photos
5. AirDrop or email to yourself → save as `lesson[X]-screen-demo.mp4`

**Android:**
1. Swipe down notification shade → find "Screen Recorder"
2. Tap to start → select "Record audio from microphone"
3. Talk through each step
4. Stop → find in Gallery → transfer to PC

**Rules:**
- Keep under 2 minutes
- Go SLOW. Pause 2 seconds after each tap so viewer can follow
- Say what you're doing AS you do it: "Now I'm tapping Settings..."
- If you mess up, just keep going. Real is better than perfect

**Where it goes in LearnDash:** Below the Loom intro. This is the "follow along" part.

---

### PIECE 4: Activity Card (Canva + LearnDash — 15 min per lesson)

**What it is:** A single, visually clear card that says "DO THIS NOW" with numbered steps. Not a paragraph — a CARD.

**Where to build:** Canva → Custom size 800x600px

**How to build:**

1. Open Canva → blank 800x600 design
2. Background: light color (cream or light blue)
3. Top: Bold title — "YOUR TURN" or "DO THIS NOW"
4. Middle: 3-5 numbered steps in large, readable font (18pt minimum)
5. Bottom: "When you're done, click the button below"
6. Export as PNG

**Lesson 1 card text:**
```
YOUR TURN (2 minutes)
1. Open Settings
2. Tap Display → slide Text Size to the right
3. Go to Accessibility → turn on Bold Text
4. Look at your phone. Everything is bigger now.
```

**Lesson 2 card text:**
```
YOUR TURN (5 minutes)
1. Hold home button → say "Remind me to [anything] every day at [time]"
2. Open your Weather app → check tomorrow
3. Open App Store → download your pharmacy or bank app
```

**Lesson 3 card text:**
```
YOUR TURN — PICK ONE (5 minutes)
A) Share your location with one family member
B) Download a grocery delivery app
C) Find your doctor's patient portal
```

**Where it goes in LearnDash:** Below the screen recording video. This is the ACTION section. Make it impossible to miss.

---

### PIECE 5: Social Prompt (LearnDash text block — 2 min per lesson)

**What it is:** One sentence that asks them to SHOW someone or TELL someone what they did. This creates accountability and a dopamine hit.

**Where to build:** Directly in LearnDash lesson editor (text block below the activity)

**What to write:**

**Lesson 1:**
> "Show someone near you your phone screen. Say: 'Look — I just made my text bigger.' Watch their face."

**Lesson 2:**
> "When your reminder goes off tomorrow, screenshot it and reply to this email. I want to see it."

**Lesson 3:**
> "Text one family member right now: 'Check your phone — you can see where I am.' Watch their reaction."

**Where it goes in LearnDash:** Immediately AFTER the [I DID IT] button. They complete the task, then get the social nudge.

---

### PIECE 6: Progress Tracker (LearnDash settings — 5 min total)

**What it is:** A visible bar or counter showing "Lesson 1 of 3 Complete" that makes them want to finish.

**Where to build:** LearnDash → Course Settings

**How to set up:**

1. Go to WordPress Admin → LearnDash → Courses → your course
2. Click Settings tab
3. Under "Course Progress" make sure progress bar is ENABLED
4. Under each lesson, make sure "Mark Complete" button is active
5. Optional: Add a text block at the bottom of each lesson:

**Lesson 1:** "Lesson 1 of 3 complete. Tomorrow: 3 tools that save you time every week."
**Lesson 2:** "Lesson 2 of 3 complete. Tomorrow: the one that matters most — staying independent."
**Lesson 3:** "All 3 lessons complete. You did it. You're a Digital Pioneer."

**Why this works:** Logical learners need to see progress. Everyone needs to see the finish line is CLOSE (3 lessons, not 30).

## PAGE LAYOUT ORDER IN LEARNDASH

Every lesson page follows this exact order top to bottom:

```
1. PATHWAY GRAPHIC (image — they see the map first)
2. HEYGEN INTRO VIDEO (60 sec — AI guide avatar, builds trust)
3. SCREEN RECORDING (90 sec — follow along demo)
4. ACTIVITY CARD (image — clear "do this now" steps)
5. [I DID IT] BUTTON (LearnDash Mark Complete)
6. SOCIAL PROMPT (text — show/tell someone)
7. PROGRESS LINE (text — "Lesson X of 3 complete. Next up: ___")
8. QUIZ (3 questions — optional, below the fold)
```

Total time on page: 5-7 minutes. That's it.

---

## WHAT TO DELETE FROM CURRENT LESSONS

If your current LearnDash pages look like college courses, remove:

- Long paragraphs of explanation BEFORE the video
- Multiple videos stacked on one page
- Quizzes with more than 3 questions
- Any "reading assignment" or "additional resources" links
- Walls of text that explain WHY before showing WHAT
- Any section that takes more than 2 minutes to read

**Rule: If they can't finish the lesson while standing in their kitchen waiting for coffee, it's too long.**

## THE "COFFEE TEST"

Before you publish, test each lesson with this question:

> "Can someone finish this lesson in the time it takes to drink one cup of coffee?"

If yes — publish it.
If no — cut something.

## WHY THIS WORKS (the science, in plain English)

| Old Way (College) | New Way (Interactive) |
|---|---|
| Watch a 20-min video | Watch 60 seconds, then DO something |
| Read instructions | SEE a picture of the path first |
| Quiz at the end (feels like a test) | "I DID IT" button (feels like a win) |
| Alone in front of a screen | "Show someone" creates connection |
| No idea how long it takes | "5 minutes" is promised and delivered |
| No visible progress | "2 of 3 done" makes you want to finish |

People don't drop out because the content is bad. They drop out because it FEELS long, lonely, and unclear. Fix the feeling, fix the completion rate.
