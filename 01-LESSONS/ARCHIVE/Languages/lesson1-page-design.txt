=============================================
STYLE NAME OPTIONS (ranked by research)
=============================================

BEST (action-based, friendly, no jargon):
1. Show Me / Tell Me / Let Me Try  ← RECOMMENDED
   - Based on proven "Tell Me, Show Me, Let Me Try" training model
   - Instantly understood by any age
   - No academic language

GOOD ALTERNATIVES:
2. Watch It / Hear It / Do It
   - Slightly more direct
   - Very clear

3. See It / Listen / Hands On
   - Simple but "Hands On" might confuse for a phone lesson

4. The Watcher / The Listener / The Explorer
   - More personality but might feel childish

AVOID:
- Visual / Auditory / Kinesthetic (too academic)
- VAK (nobody knows what this means)
- Learning Style A/B/C (boring)

=============================================
DESIGN NOTES
=============================================

- Brand colors: Navy (#0E1C2F) + Gold (#C8942E) + White
- Cards have hover effect with gold border glow
- Selected card gets gold border + checkmark
- Mobile responsive: cards stack vertically
- Language flags use emoji or small flag icons
- After selection, page smoothly scrolls to lesson content
- Progress saved — if they leave and come back, their choices are remembered
