# HeyGen Scripts — Lessons 1-3 Face Intros

Paste each script directly into HeyGen. Plain spoken words only.
Avatar: Female guide character, 55-65, warm face, casual-professional (not Brian).
Voice: Like a neighbor who figured this out last year and is showing you over the fence.

---

## LESSON 1 — Your Phone is More Powerful Than You Think

Hey there, Digital Pioneer. Welcome. I'm so glad you're here. You know, I was exactly where you are not too long ago. I had this phone in my pocket and I knew it could do more, but nobody ever sat down and showed me. So that's what we're doing today. Together. I'm going to walk you through one small thing that makes your phone easier to use. Then you're going to do it yourself on your own phone. The whole thing takes about five minutes. You won't break anything, I promise. Ready? Let's do this together.

---

## LESSON 2 — Set Up 3 Tools That Save You Time Every Week

Hey Digital Pioneer, welcome back. Look at you, showing up again. That's what this is all about. So yesterday you made your phone easier to read. Today we're going to set up three little tools that honestly changed my week when I first learned them. Things like telling your phone to remind you about your medication instead of writing it on a sticky note. Or checking tomorrow's weather without turning on the TV. Or downloading your pharmacy app so you can refill a prescription from your couch. None of this is complicated. Your phone already knows how to do it. We just need to turn it on. Five minutes. Three tools. You and me. Let's go.

---

## LESSON 3 — Stay Independent: Tech That Helps You Live on Your Terms

Hey Digital Pioneer, welcome back. I have to tell you, this one is my favorite. Everything we've done so far, the bigger text, the reminders, the apps, it all comes down to this. Independence. Being able to order groceries when you don't feel like driving. Video calling your doctor instead of sitting in that waiting room for an hour. Letting your family know you're home safe without them calling every single evening. This isn't about being tech savvy. This is about living life on your terms. Today I'm going to show you three ways to do exactly that. Pick the one that matters most to you. Five minutes. This one's personal. Let's go.
