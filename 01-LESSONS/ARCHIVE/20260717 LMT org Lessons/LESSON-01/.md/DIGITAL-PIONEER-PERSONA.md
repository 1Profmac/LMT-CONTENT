# Digital Pioneer — User Persona

## Who They Are

**Name:** The Digital Pioneer
**Age:** 55-75
**Status:** Retired or semi-retired, living independently or with a spouse
**Tech level:** Owns a smartphone. Uses it for calls, texts, maybe Facebook. That's about it.

---

## Their Story

They bought the phone because their kids told them to. Or because the flip phone died. They use maybe 10% of it. They know there's more it can do, but nobody ever showed them — and they're not about to ask their 14-year-old grandchild for help again.

They're not stupid. They're not scared. They just never had a GUIDE.

---

## What They Want

| They say... | They actually mean... |
|-------------|----------------------|
| "I just want to know the basics" | "Don't overwhelm me" |
| "I'm not a tech person" | "I haven't had a good teacher" |
| "My kids handle that stuff" | "I want to do it myself but I don't know how" |
| "I don't want to break anything" | "I need permission to try" |
| "Is this going to take long?" | "I'll leave if this feels like school" |

---

## What They Fear

- Looking stupid in front of younger people
- Breaking their phone or "messing something up"
- Being sold something
- Sitting through a long class they can't keep up with
- Being talked down to

---

## What Makes Them Stay

- A WIN in the first 5 minutes (something visible changed on their phone)
- Being called a Digital Pioneer (identity, not a student number)
- Short lessons (coffee-length, not classroom-length)
- A friendly guide who says "we" not "you should"
- Proof that other people their age are doing this too
- Seeing progress: "2 of 3 done"

---

## What Makes Them Quit

- Wall of text before anything happens
- Video longer than 5 minutes
- Feeling like they're in college again
- No clear "what do I do RIGHT NOW"
- No acknowledgment that they completed something
- Being alone with no one to share it with

---

## Their Transformation

| Before (Day 1) | After (Day 7) |
|----------------|---------------|
| "I'm not a tech person" | "I'm a Digital Pioneer" |
| Uses phone for calls and texts | Uses voice reminders, apps, video calls |
| Depends on kids for tech help | Does it themselves, shows others |
| Nervous about trying new things | Confident — "I figured it out" |
| Isolated with their phone | Connected — sharing wins with family |

---

## How We Talk to Them

**Tone:** Warm. Direct. Zero jargon. Like a friendly neighbor over the fence.

**Words we use:**
- "Your turn" (not "assignment")
- "You did it" (not "correct")
- "Five minutes" (not "module")
- "Digital Pioneer" (not "student" or "user" or "participant")
- "Guide" (not "instructor" or "teacher")
- "We" (not "you should")

**Words we never use:**
- Module, curriculum, syllabus
- Interface, navigate, configure
- Submit, assessment, requirement
- Log in to your LMS
- Any acronym without explaining it first

---

## Their Daily Life (Context for Lesson Design)

- Morning routine: coffee, news, maybe a walk
- Phone is usually on the kitchen counter or in a pocket
- Checks it when it buzzes, doesn't browse
- Comfortable with: calling, texting, taking photos
- Uncomfortable with: downloading apps, settings, anything that asks for a password
- Biggest phone frustration: "The text is too small" and "I accidentally hit something and now it's different"

---

## The Identity Ladder

How "Digital Pioneer" grows with them:

1. **New Digital Pioneer** — Just signed up. Hasn't done anything yet.
2. **Active Digital Pioneer** — Completed at least 1 lesson. Changed something on their phone.
3. **Certified Digital Pioneer** — Completed all 3 lessons. Got the certificate.
4. **Pioneer Guide** — Helping someone else get started. Sharing what they learned.

This gives them somewhere to GO. They're not just taking a course — they're becoming someone.

---

## One Sentence Summary

> A Digital Pioneer is an adult 50+ who decided that technology works FOR them, not against them — and proved it by doing something on their phone today.
