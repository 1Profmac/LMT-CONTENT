## ACTIVITY (what they DO on their phone)

**Title:** Make Your Phone Easier to See

**Instructions (show on screen below the video):**

1. Open **Settings** on your phone
2. Tap **Display** (or Display & Brightness)
3. Find **Text Size** — slide it to the right until it's comfortable
4. Go back to **Settings** → **Accessibility** → **Display & Text Size**
5. Turn on **Bold Text**
6. Look at your phone. Everything is bigger and bolder now.

**That's it. You just customized your phone for the first time.**
