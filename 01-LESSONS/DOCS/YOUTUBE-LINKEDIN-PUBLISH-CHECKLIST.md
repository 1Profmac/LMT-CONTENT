# YouTube + LinkedIn Publish Checklist
# For: Welcome Lesson and all future 50+TechBridge videos

Last updated: 2026-08-30

---

## STEP 1 — YouTube Upload

- [ ] Go to YouTube Studio → Create → Upload video
- [ ] Select your MP4 file
- [ ] While uploading, fill in Details:
  - Title format: `Welcome to 50+TechBridge — [Tagline]`
  - Description (see template below)
  - Thumbnail: upload Canva PNG (1280x720 or 1200x720)
  - Playlist: 50+TechBridge Course
  - Audience: Not made for kids

### Description Template
```
Welcome to 50+TechBridge! You're not bad at technology — you've just never had the right community.
In this lesson, [one sentence on what they'll learn].

Start your free lesson: https://50plustechbridge.com/start-free-lessons/
Book a free session: https://cal.com/brian-mckinney-mrtu8q/speaking-inquiry
```

---

## STEP 2 — End Screens (last 20 seconds)

- [ ] Go to Video Elements → End Screen → Add
- [ ] Add: Subscribe element (Learn More Technologies)
- [ ] Add: Video element (Best for viewer)
- [ ] Save end screen

---

## STEP 3 — Thumbnail

- [ ] Open Canva → Custom size 1280x720px
- [ ] Background: navy #0A1628
- [ ] Heading (white, ~65pt): main message
- [ ] Subheading (orange #FF6B35, ~40pt): supporting line
- [ ] Logo: 50+TechBridge horizontal from Brand kit, bottom left
- [ ] Download as PNG → High quality
- [ ] Upload to YouTube video Details → Thumbnail

---

## STEP 4 — Playlist

- [ ] Confirm video is added to: 50+TechBridge Course playlist
- [ ] Playlist visibility: Public

---

## STEP 5 — Visibility

- [ ] Set to Public (or Schedule if batching content)
- [ ] Click Publish

---

## STEP 6 — LinkedIn Article

- [ ] Go to LinkedIn → Write article
- [ ] Title: match the YouTube video title
- [ ] Body: paste article content (see template in /10-MARKETING)
- [ ] Cover image: upload the same Canva thumbnail PNG
- [ ] Intro post line (in the publish box):
  "Just published my welcome lesson for 50+TechBridge. If you know an adult 50+ who is ready to learn how AI can support their independence and financial future — this is where they start."
- [ ] Publish
- [ ] Add first comment immediately (author comment to boost reach):
  "This is exactly the kind of resource our community needs. AI skills aren't just for the young — they're for anyone ready to take control of their future."

---

## STEP 7 — After Publishing

- [ ] Go back to YouTube → verify thumbnail is showing
- [ ] Copy YouTube link and add to 50plustechbridge.com welcome page
- [ ] Share LinkedIn article link in MailerLite newsletter (next send)
