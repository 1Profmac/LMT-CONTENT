The 5 Warning Signs of a Scam
  • 🚩
    They create urgency. "Act now or lose your account." Legitimate companies give you time to decide.
  • 🚩
    They ask for unusual payment. Gift cards, wire transfers, or cryptocurrency are scam red flags — always.
  • 🚩
    They called or emailed you first. Your bank, Social Security, and Medicare do not call you out of the blue to ask for information.
  • 🚩
    The offer seems too good. A prize you didn’t enter, a refund you didn’t request, or a deal that defies logic.
  • 🚩
    They ask you to keep it secret. Any real transaction can be verified with a family member. Scammers know this — that is why they tell you not to tell anyone.
The 3 Rules That Protect You
  • ✅
    Stop. Hang up. Call back. If you receive an unexpected call, hang up and call the organization directly using the number on their official website or your bill.
  • ✅
    Never give a code to anyone who calls you. Banks, Apple, Google, and Social Security will never call you and ask for a verification code they just texted you.
  • ✅
    When in doubt, do nothing. A real offer will still be there tomorrow. A scam will pressure you to act right now.
🚨 If you think you were scammed: Call your bank immediately, then report it to the FTC at 1-877-382-4357 or reportfraud.ftc.gov. You are not alone — this happens to millions of people every year. There is no shame in reporting it.
