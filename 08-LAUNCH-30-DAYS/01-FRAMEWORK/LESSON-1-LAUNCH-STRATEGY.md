# Lesson 1 Launch Strategy — 10-Day Social Blitz

## THE GOAL

Drive signups and completions for Revised Lesson 1: "Your Phone is More Powerful Than You Think." Measure engagement, views, and completion rate to validate the new format before building Lessons 2 and 3.

## RULES

1. Every post ends with a link or CTA — no "awareness" posts without action
2. Post at the same times every day — train the algorithm
3. Reply to EVERY comment within 2 hours — engagement breeds engagement
4. Never post and ghost — spend 10 min after posting commenting on other people's posts
5. If a post gets traction, boost it with $10-20 (Facebook/Instagram only)
6. Screenshot every good comment/reply for future social proof
7. Tag organizations you've worked with when relevant (not every post)
