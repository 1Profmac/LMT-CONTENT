# Cohort-Based Learning Model — Research & Strategy
**Date:** June 11, 2026
**Author:** Brian McKinney / Claude
**Purpose:** Why 50+TechBridge should run cohorts, not individual self-paced enrollment

---

## The Data: Cohorts vs. Self-Paced

| Metric | Self-Paced | Cohort-Based |
|---|---|---|
| Completion rate | 3–12% (MOOCs) | 85–96% |
| Engagement | 20% lower | 90% feel more engaged |
| Retention (follow-up tests) | Drops 21% | Drops only 6.5% |
| Social isolation | No impact | Measurable reduction in loneliness |
| Community after program | None | Alumni networks, ongoing support |

**Bottom line:** Cohorts complete. Self-paced quits.

---

## Why Cohorts Are Perfect for Adults 50+

1. **Social isolation is the #1 health risk for adults 50+.** Cohorts create community. A group of 6-8 adults meeting regularly showed a 3-point decrease in loneliness (NIH study).

2. **Accountability without shame.** Adults 50+ don't want to be tracked like students. But they do respond to "my group is counting on me to show up."

3. **Peer learning is your model.** You already believe in 50+ teaching 50+. Cohorts formalize that — learners help each other, not just consume content.

4. **Structured pace removes overwhelm.** Self-paced sounds good but creates decision fatigue. "We start Monday, we finish in 3 weeks" is simpler.

5. **Funders love cohorts.** Grant applications want measurable outcomes. "Cohort 1: 12 adults, 10 completed, 83% completion" is fundable. "247 signups, 24 active" is not.

## 10 Cohort-Based Programs to Learn From

### 1. altMBA (Seth Godin)
- **Format:** 4 weeks, 13 projects, 10:1 student-to-coach ratio
- **Completion:** 96%
- **Lesson for you:** Small groups + deadlines + projects = near-perfect completion. Your 3-lesson course could run as a 3-week cohort.

### 2. IDEO U
- **Format:** Cohort courses on creative leadership, 80,000+ students across 80 countries
- **Completion:** 70–80%
- **Lesson for you:** Even at scale, cohorts outperform self-paced. Design thinking approach — learn by doing, not watching.

### 3. Write of Passage (David Perell)
- **Format:** 5-week writing cohort, live sessions + async work
- **Price:** $4,000–$10,000
- **Lesson for you:** Premium cohorts command premium prices. Your free cohort for libraries is the entry point — paid cohorts for workforce agencies come later.

### 4. Maven Courses
- **Format:** Platform for cohort-based courses, live + interactive
- **Completion:** 85–95% across platform
- **Lesson for you:** Maven proves the cohort model works across every subject. They handle scheduling, reminders, and community — features you could replicate in LearnDash + BuddyBoss.

### 5. Senior Planet (AARP/OATS)
- **Format:** Scheduled Zoom classes — essentially cohorts without calling them that
- **Completion:** Not published, but 60+ classes/week shows demand
- **Lesson for you:** Your biggest competitor already runs group-based learning. You should name yours, brand them, and track completion per cohort.

### 6. Cyber-Seniors
- **Format:** Mentor-student pairs + group sessions
- **Completion:** Not published
- **Lesson for you:** Their intergenerational model works but lacks structure. Your cohort model with peer instruction is more systematic.

### 7. Circle of Friends (NIH-studied)
- **Format:** 6–8 older adults, 12 sessions over 3 months
- **Outcome:** Measurable decrease in loneliness
- **Lesson for you:** This is clinical proof that small groups of older adults meeting regularly improves health outcomes. Your cohort does the same thing AND teaches AI.

### 8. On Deck Fellowship
- **Format:** 10-week cohort for founders, operators, and creators
- **Completion:** 80%+
- **Lesson for you:** On Deck proves that cohorts build networks, not just skills. Your graduates become your ambassadors.

### 9. Reforge (Tech Growth Programs)
- **Format:** 6-week cohorts for marketing/product professionals
- **Completion:** 85%+
- **Lesson for you:** They pair live sessions with async case studies. Your model: live Zoom or in-person session + LearnDash lesson at home.

### 10. Close the Gap Foundation
- **Format:** Cohort-based digital literacy for underserved communities
- **Mission:** Digital equity through structured group learning
- **Lesson for you:** Closest to your model. They prove cohort-based digital literacy works for the same population you serve.

## How 50+TechBridge Cohorts Should Work

### Structure
| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| Cohort size | 8–12 adults |
| Duration | 3 weeks (1 lesson per week) |
| Format | In-person (library/center) OR Zoom |
| Schedule | Same day/time each week |
| Support | LearnDash for homework, BuddyBoss for group chat |
| Graduation | Certificate of completion — "Digital Pioneer, Cohort [#]" |

### Weekly Flow
- **Week 1:** Set Up Your Phone (Lesson 1) — group session + practice together
- **Week 2:** Voice Commands (Lesson 2) — group session + buddy check-ins
- **Week 3:** Navigate with Maps (Lesson 3) — group session + graduation

### Naming
- **"Digital Pioneer Cohort"** — not "class" or "session"
- Number them: Cohort 1, Cohort 2, etc.
- Graduates become "Digital Pioneers" — identity, not just a certificate

### What Changes From Current Model
| Current | Cohort Model |
|---|---|
| Sign up anytime, start anytime | Sign up for a specific cohort start date |
| Self-paced, alone | Group-paced, together |
| No accountability | Group shows up together |
| No graduation | Certificate + Digital Pioneer identity |
| Hard to measure | Completion tracked per cohort |
| "247 signups, 24 active" | "Cohort 7: 10 enrolled, 9 completed" |
