## The One Rule

Every lesson ends with the student realizing they just used AI. They came in thinking "I can't do AI." They leave thinking "I've been doing AI this whole time."

## Lesson 1: Wake Up the AI in Your Phone

**Current title:** "Your Phone is More Powerful Than You Think"
**New title:** "Wake Up the AI in Your Phone"
**New hook:** "Your phone has an AI assistant built in. Today we'll set it up so it works for YOU — starting with making everything easier to read."

### Steps (existing + new)

| Step | What They Do | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Find Settings | Stays as-is |
| 2 | Tap Display | Stays as-is |
| 3 | Make Text Bigger (slider) | Stays as-is |
| 4 | Go to Accessibility | Stays as-is |
| 5 | Turn on Bold Text | Stays as-is |
| **6 NEW** | Turn on Siri / Google Assistant | "While we're in Settings, let's wake up the AI that lives in your phone" |
| **7 NEW** | Say "Hey Siri, make text bigger" | Show them AI just did what they did manually — wow moment |

### Completion Message
**Current:** "You Did It, Digital Pioneer — Your phone is easier to read now."
**New:** "You Did It, Digital Pioneer — You just set up your phone AND activated your AI assistant. That's two things most people never do."

## Lesson 2: Your First AI Conversation

**Current title:** Voice commands (untitled in slides)
**New title:** "Your First AI Conversation"
**New hook:** "Today you're going to talk to an AI — and it's going to talk back. No typing. No screens. Just your voice."

### Steps (existing + new)

| Step | What They Do | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hold the side button (activate Siri/Google) | Reframe: "You're about to talk to an AI" |
| 2 | Say "What's the weather?" | Stays — already AI |
| 3 | Say "Call my pharmacy" | Stays — already AI |
| **4 NEW** | "Set a reminder to take my medicine at 9am" | AI-powered daily health reminder |
| **5 NEW** | "Read my last text message" | AI reads aloud — accessibility win |
| **6 NEW** | "What time does Walgreens close?" | AI answers real-time questions from the internet |

### Completion Message
**New:** "You just had a conversation with AI. You asked it questions. It answered. That's all AI is — asking and getting answers. You're already doing it."

## Cohort Session Framing

When teaching in a cohort, each week's session should open with:

- **Week 1:** "This week, we're waking up the AI that already lives in your phone."
- **Week 2:** "This week, you're going to have your first conversation with AI."
- **Week 3:** "This week, you're going to teach AI where you go — and it'll take you there."

End each cohort session with: "You just used AI. That's what we do here."
