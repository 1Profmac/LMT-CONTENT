## THE HEADLINE

### "The Gray Divide" Is Real — But It's Not What You Think

Here's the number everyone's talking about: **61% of workers over 50 believe AI will replace them.** That's from the latest AARP and Urban Institute research.

But here's what they're NOT telling you.

The same research shows that the workers MOST exposed to AI are actually the LEAST likely to lose their jobs. Why? Because AI doesn't replace experience. It replaces repetitive tasks. And if you've been working for 25 or 30 years, you have something AI will never have — judgment.

The real problem isn't that AI is coming for your job. The real problem is that **only 10% of workers over 50 have received ANY AI training at work.** Meanwhile, 47% say they WANT training but can't get it.

That's not a technology gap. That's a training gap. And that's exactly what we're here to fix.

**The bottom line:** AI isn't replacing experienced people. But it IS leaving behind the ones who don't learn how to use it. You reading this newsletter? That means you're not one of them.

## THE TOOL

### ChatGPT — Yes, That One. Here's What It Actually Does For You.

I know. You've heard about it. Maybe you've even tried it once and thought "what's the big deal?"

Here's the big deal: **ChatGPT is like having a smart assistant who never sleeps, never judges you for asking a "dumb" question, and works for free.**

**What it does:** You type a question or a request in plain English. It answers. That's it.

**Who it's for:** Everyone. Workforce, entrepreneurs, retirees, curious humans.

**Try this right now (3 steps):**
1. Go to chatgpt.com on your phone or computer
2. Create a free account (just an email address)
3. Type this exact sentence: *"I'm over 50 and new to AI. Give me 3 simple things I can use you for today."*

Read what it says. Then try one of them.

**Brian's take:** I use ChatGPT every single day. It writes my emails, helps me plan events, researches topics for me, and even helped me build the course you might be taking right now. It's not magic. It's a tool. And once you try it, you'll wonder why you waited.

## THE OPPORTUNITY

### Older Workers Are Getting Hired to Train AI — And You Could Too

Here's something most people don't know: AI companies need humans to train their systems. They need people who can read, evaluate, and correct AI responses. And they're specifically looking for **experienced adults** because you have real-world knowledge that a 22-year-old doesn't.

Companies like Scale AI, Outlier, and DataAnnotation are hiring remote workers to review AI-generated text, fact-check responses, and teach AI to be more accurate. Pay ranges from **$20 to $40 per hour**, and you can work from home on your own schedule.

Is it a dream job? No. But it's a real opportunity — especially if you're between jobs, semi-retired, or looking for flexible income. And the irony is beautiful: **you're getting paid to teach AI while learning how AI works.**

**Could I do this?** If you can read, write, and think critically — yes. Most platforms require no technical background. Just life experience. Which you have plenty of.

## THE CHECKLIST

### "Set Up AI on Your Phone in 10 Minutes"

You don't need to buy anything. You don't need to be technical. You just need your phone and 10 minutes.

- [ ] **Step 1:** Open your phone's app store (App Store or Google Play)
- [ ] **Step 2:** Search for "ChatGPT" and download the free app (it's made by OpenAI — look for the green logo)
- [ ] **Step 3:** Create an account with your email address
- [ ] **Step 4:** Tap the microphone icon and ask out loud: "What's a good recipe for dinner tonight using chicken and rice?"
- [ ] **Step 5:** Read the answer. Then ask a follow-up: "Can you make that easier? I'm not a great cook."
- [ ] **Step 6:** Try one more: "Write a text message to my friend canceling lunch plans politely"
- [ ] **Step 7:** Now try something YOU want to know: type any question about your life, your health, your hobbies, your work
- [ ] **Step 8:** Save the app to your home screen so you can find it easily
- [ ] **Step 9:** Tomorrow, open it again and ask: "What did we talk about yesterday?" (It remembers.)
- [ ] **Step 10:** Tell one friend what you just did. Show them on your phone.

**That's it. You just set up AI. You're officially a pioneer.**
