## THE TOOL

### ElevenLabs — AI Voice That Speaks Your Language

What if you could take any lesson, any article, any email — and hear it read to you in a voice that sounds like a real person? In English. Spanish. Vietnamese. Any language.

That's what ElevenLabs does. And it's how we built DR. Maya — one of three AI instructors in the new 50+TechBridge lessons.

**Why it matters for you:**
- Turn any document into an audiobook in seconds
- Listen to articles while you walk, cook, or drive
- Create voice messages in a language you don't speak

**Try this right now:**
1. Go to elevenlabs.io
2. Click "Text to Speech"
3. Paste any paragraph
4. Pick a voice and click Generate
5. Listen — it sounds real

**Brian's take:** We use ElevenLabs to create podcast episodes and lesson audio in multiple languages. One script, multiple voices, multiple languages. It's how a solo founder delivers training globally.
