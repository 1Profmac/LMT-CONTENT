# Client Application Form — "Apply to Work with Brian"
## Build in Google Forms or Typeform
## Link goes on LinkedIn, YouTube, carousels, workshops, email

---

## FORM TITLE
Apply to Work with Brian McKinney

## FORM DESCRIPTION
I work with 5 clients at a time. If you're an experienced professional or business owner who wants AI working for you — not just something you've heard about — tell me about your situation. I'll review every application personally and respond within 48 hours.

---

## QUESTIONS (4 questions only — keep it short)

### Question 1
**What's your name and what do you do?**
Type: Short text
Required: Yes
Placeholder: "Name, title, company or business"

### Question 2
**What do you want AI to do for your business or career?**
Type: Long text (paragraph)
Required: Yes
Placeholder: "Be specific — what's the problem you want solved?"

### Question 3
**What have you tried so far?**
Type: Multiple choice
Required: Yes
Options:
- I haven't started yet — I don't know where to begin
- I've tried ChatGPT or another AI tool but it didn't stick
- I use AI occasionally but want to go deeper
- I use AI regularly and want expert guidance to level up

### Question 4
**Are you ready to invest in yourself and start this month?**
Type: Multiple choice
Required: Yes
Options:
- Yes — I'm ready now
- I'm interested but need to learn more first
- I'm exploring for my organization, not just myself

## WHAT HAPPENS AFTER THEY APPLY

1. Application comes to your email (Google Forms notifies you)
2. Review it — takes 30 seconds
3. Decide: fit or not fit

**If fit:**
- Reply: "Thanks for applying. I'd like to learn more. Here's my calendar for a 15-minute call: [calendly link]"
- Add them to HubSpot → Deal stage: "Meeting Scheduled"
- Discovery call → present the right offer (Quickstart $750 / Transformation $2,500 / Advisory $5,000)

**If not fit (yet):**
- Reply: "Thanks for applying. Based on what you shared, I'd recommend starting with our free course at learnmoretechnologies.com/join-now. Once you've gone through it, reapply and we'll talk."
- Add them to HubSpot → Contact only (no deal)
- They join your newsletter funnel → may come back later

**If organization:**
- Reply: "Thanks for reaching out. I'd like to schedule a 30-minute workforce consult to learn about your organization. [calendly link]"
- Add to HubSpot → Deal stage: "Meeting Scheduled"
- Different conversation — org pricing, not individual pricing

---

## THE PSYCHOLOGY

- They apply to YOU — not the other way around
- You respond within 48 hours — professional, not desperate
- Discovery call is 15 minutes — short, you're busy
- Calendly link is only shared AFTER they apply — never public
- No prices anywhere public — only discussed on the call

## GOOGLE FORMS BUILD STEPS

1. Go to forms.google.com
2. Click + Blank form
3. Title: "Apply to Work with Brian McKinney"
4. Description: paste the form description above
5. Add 4 questions (types listed above)
6. Settings → Responses → collect email addresses (ON)
7. Settings → Presentation → confirmation message (paste above)
8. Click Send → copy the link
9. Paste link into LinkedIn About, YouTube description, etc.
