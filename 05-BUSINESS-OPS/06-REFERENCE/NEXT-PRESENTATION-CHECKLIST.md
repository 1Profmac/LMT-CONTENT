# Next Presentation Checklist — LMT / 50+TechBridge
## Do these at your next library, senior center, or speaking event

### Photos to Capture (have someone take these)
- [ ] You presenting to the group (wide shot, audience visible)
- [ ] You one-on-one helping someone with their phone (close-up)
- [ ] Participants working on their devices (candid)
- [ ] The room — people engaged, screens visible (wide shot)
- [ ] A candid moment — someone smiling when they "get it"
- [ ] You at the podium or front of room (headshot-style, for LinkedIn)
- [ ] Group photo with participants at the end

### Video to Capture
- [ ] 30-second clip of you presenting (for LinkedIn/YouTube Shorts)
- [ ] 15-second clip of a participant saying what they learned (testimonial)
- [ ] B-roll of hands on phones/tablets (for video production)

### Materials to Bring
- [ ] Business cards
- [ ] One-pager / flyer (print from `06-MARKETING/05-ONE-PAGERS/LunchLearn-Flyer.html`)
- [ ] Sign-up sheet (name, email, phone) — every attendee goes into HubSpot
- [ ] QR code linking to learnmoretechnologies.com/join-now/

### Content to Create After the Event
- [ ] Upload photos to `04-ASSETS/demo-photos/`
- [ ] LinkedIn post with best photo + event recap
- [ ] Add attendee contacts to HubSpot
- [ ] Send follow-up email within 24 hours
- [ ] Update verified numbers in memory if new participants trained

### Equipment
- [ ] Fully charged phone (for backup photos/video)
- [ ] Laptop with presentation loaded
- [ ] Ask venue: do they have a projector/screen?
- [ ] Ask someone to be your photographer (staff, volunteer, attendee)
