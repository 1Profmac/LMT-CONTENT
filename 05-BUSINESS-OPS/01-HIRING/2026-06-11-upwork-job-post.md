## Job Title

AI-Literate Business Coordinator — Nonprofit AI Education (Part-Time, Remote)

## Job Description

We're 50+TechBridge — the only program in America teaching AI skills specifically to adults 50+. Our founder runs the entire company with 30+ AI-powered workflows. We need a coordinator who works the same way.

This is NOT a traditional VA role. You will use AI tools (Claude, ChatGPT) every single day to draft emails, generate content, analyze data, and manage operations. If you don't already use AI daily, this isn't the right fit.

**What You'll Do:**

- Draft and send 50+ personalized outreach emails per week using Claude/ChatGPT
- Manage cohort registrations and scheduling (Google Forms, Cal.com)
- Build and maintain prospect pipeline in Google Sheets
- Format and send weekly newsletter via Mailchimp
- Create and post social media content from AI-generated templates
- Set up and manage automated email sequences in Mailchimp
- Generate reports and summaries using AI tools
- Support founder with AI-powered administrative tasks

**Requirements (Non-Negotiable):**

- You use ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or similar AI tools DAILY
- You can write effective prompts AND edit AI output (not just copy/paste)
- Google Workspace proficient (Sheets, Forms, Docs)
- Mailchimp or similar email platform experience
- Strong written English
- Self-directed — you'll work independently with minimal supervision
- Reliable internet and availability during US business hours (CST)

**Bonus Skills:**

- Canva
- HeyGen
- Buffer or Hootsuite
- HubSpot CRM
- Nonprofit or education experience
- LinkedIn content management

**To Be Considered — Complete This Task:**

Use ChatGPT or Claude to write a 3-sentence outreach email to a library director about hosting a free AI training cohort for adults 50+. In your proposal, include:
1. The prompt you used
2. The AI output
3. Your edited final version

If you skip this task, your proposal will not be reviewed.

## Screening Questions (Add in Upwork)

1. Which AI tools do you use daily? List them.
2. Describe a task you completed this week using AI — what was the prompt and what was the result?
3. Have you used Mailchimp? If yes, describe what you built.
4. Are you available during US Central Time business hours?

## How to Filter Proposals

| Green Flag | Red Flag |
|---|---|
| Completed the outreach email task | Skipped the task |
| Named specific AI tools with examples | "I'm willing to learn AI" |
| Showed their prompt AND edited the output | Copy/pasted AI output without editing |
| Mentioned nonprofit or education experience | Generic "I can do anything" proposal |
| Gave specific Mailchimp examples | "I'm familiar with email marketing" |
| Available during CST hours | Timezone never mentioned |

## Interview Process

1. Review proposals — filter using green/red flags above
2. Shortlist top 3-5
3. Send a paid test task ($25): "Write 5 outreach emails to senior centers in Austin using this template. Research the centers, personalize each email."
4. Pick the best one
5. Start with a 2-week trial (10 hrs/week)
6. If it works, extend to ongoing
