## AI Literacy Requirements

This is non-negotiable. The coordinator must be able to:

| Skill | What It Means |
|---|---|
| Prompt AI tools | Write clear prompts in ChatGPT or Claude to generate emails, posts, and reports |
| Edit AI output | Know when AI gets it wrong and fix it — not just copy/paste blindly |
| Use AI for research | Pull data, find contacts, summarize articles using AI |
| Automate with AI | Set up basic Mailchimp sequences, Google Sheet formulas, and templated workflows |
| Learn new AI tools fast | Pick up HeyGen, Canva AI, Buffer, HubSpot without hand-holding |
| Explain AI to others | If asked, can help cohort students understand what AI is in plain language |

### Interview Test

Ask them: "Use ChatGPT or Claude to write a 3-sentence outreach email to a library director about hosting a free AI training cohort for adults 50+. Show me the prompt you used and the result."

If they can't do this on the spot, they're not the right hire.
