## The Role in One Sentence

You execute what AI builds. You don't write, strategize, or create — you log in, click send, and keep the system moving.

## What You Do (8 Tasks, ~5-7 hrs/week)

### Daily (30 min)

| # | Task | Platform | Time |
|---|------|----------|------|
| 1 | Post today's LinkedIn content (provided to you) | LinkedIn | 3 min |
| 2 | Copy same post to Facebook | Facebook | 2 min |
| 3 | Reply to LinkedIn comments and DMs (drafts provided) | LinkedIn | 5 min |
| 4 | Send outreach emails (drafts provided) | Mailchimp | 5 min |
| 5 | Update HubSpot — move deals, log touches, add new contacts | HubSpot | 10 min |
| 6 | Check LinkedIn profile viewers, send connection requests (template provided) | LinkedIn | 5 min |

### Weekly (1-2 hrs)

| # | Task | Platform | Time |
|---|------|----------|------|
| 7 | Send newsletter (content provided) | Mailchimp | 30 min |
| 8 | Update grant tracker with flagged opportunities (list provided) | Google Sheets | 30 min |

### End of Day (2 min)

Send Brian a 3-line status update:
1. **Posted:** What went out and where
2. **Pipeline:** Who you contacted, any replies
3. **Flag:** Anything that needs Brian's decision
