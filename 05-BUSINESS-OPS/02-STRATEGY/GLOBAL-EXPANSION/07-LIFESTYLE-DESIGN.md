## Visa Pathways

### Spain — Digital Nomad Visa (Primary Base)
- **Duration:** 1 year (from abroad) or 3 years (from within Spain)
- **Renewable:** Up to 5 years total → eligible for permanent residency
- **Income requirement:** ~€2,850/month (~$3,100)
- **Tax:** Flat 24% on foreign income (first 4 years under Beckham Law)
- **Healthcare:** Must have private health insurance
- **Timeline:** 4–6 months processing from US consulate
- **Best cities:** Madrid (central, conferences), Valencia (affordable, coast), Seville (culture, lower cost)

### Portugal — D8 Digital Nomad Visa
- **Duration:** 1 year, renewable to 2 years
- **Income requirement:** ~€3,680/month (~$4,000)
- **Tax:** NHR regime may offer reduced rates
- **Healthcare:** Private insurance or National Health Service (after residency)
- **Best cities:** Lisbon (tech hub, Web Summit), Porto (affordable, beautiful)
- **Bonus:** Gateway to Africa (direct flights to Angola, Mozambique, Cape Verde)

### Panama — Pensionado Visa
- **Duration:** Permanent residency
- **Income requirement:** $1,000/month pension income (Social Security counts)
- **Tax:** NO tax on foreign-earned income
- **Currency:** US Dollar
- **Healthcare:** Affordable private healthcare
- **Best cities:** Panama City (business hub), Boquete (expat community, mountains)
- **Bonus:** At 65, Brian qualifies automatically for Pensionado discounts (flights, hotels, restaurants, entertainment — 25–50% off)

### Africa — Short-Term Deployments
- **Model:** 1–3 month deployments, not permanent residence
- **Ghana:** Visa on arrival or e-visa for US citizens, 30–60 days
- **Kenya:** E-visa, 90 days
- **South Africa:** 90 days visa-free for US citizens
- **Approach:** Fly in, deliver Train-the-Trainer, fly out. Local team sustains.
