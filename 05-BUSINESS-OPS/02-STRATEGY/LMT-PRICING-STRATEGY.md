## The Rule

**Never list prices on the website, LinkedIn, or in articles.**
Prices are shared only after a discovery call.
The only dollar amount that appears publicly is "$0" — because the entry point is free.

## The Model: Free → Consult → Custom

```
Free Lessons (3 online)          → builds trust, proves content
        ↓
Free Lunch & Learn (60 min)      → gets you in the room, demonstrates expertise
        ↓
Discovery Call (15 min)           → you screen THEM
        ↓
Custom Proposal                   → pricing shared here, not before
```

## TIER 1: FREE — Lead Generation (No Revenue)

### 3 Free Online Lessons
- Self-paced on learnmoretechnologies.com/join-now
- No login required
- Purpose: prove the content, build email list
- CTA at end: "Want the full program? Book a call."

### Free 60-Minute Lunch & Learn (Organizations Only)
- On-site or virtual, up to 20 people
- You bring everything — no prep needed from them
- Available to: libraries, senior centers, workforce boards, employers, chambers, Rotary

**What they get in 60 minutes:**
- Set up ChatGPT on their phone
- One live demo solving a real problem
- Q&A
- Checklist handout
- CTA: "If you want to go deeper, let's schedule a consult."

**What they DON'T get:**
- Full curriculum
- Custom prompts
- Ongoing support
- The 6-module program
- Anything that replaces the paid program

**Why free:** A free Lunch & Learn costs you 60 minutes. A paid cohort contract from that room is $4,900-$50,000+. You're not giving away free — you're investing 60 minutes to land five-figure contracts.

## HOW TO SELL

### Step 1: Attract
- Agentic50 newsletter (weekly — builds trust)
- LinkedIn posts (daily — builds authority)
- Free 60-min Lunch & Learn (fills the funnel)
- WordPress articles (SEO — Google finds you)

### Step 2: Screen
- Interested people apply via intake form or book a call
- YOU decide if they're a fit — not them
- Cal.com link: https://cal.com/brian-mckinney-mrtu8q

### Step 3: Discovery Call (15 min)
- Listen, don't pitch
- Ask: "What's the one thing you wish AI could do for your business?"
- If fit: "I have something that might work. Let me send you the details."
- If not fit: "Here's my free course. Stay in touch."

### Step 4: Proposal
- Send custom proposal with pricing
- Follow up once in 3 days. Then let it go.

### Step 5: Deliver
- Deliver so well they tell 3 friends
- Ask for testimonial at end
- Ask for Google review: https://g.page/r/CYS5efh1dTMBEAE/review
- Ask: "Who else do you know that needs this?"

## WHAT TO NEVER DO

1. Never list prices on LinkedIn or the website
2. Never sell time — sell outcomes
3. Never take unlimited clients — "I work with 5 advisory clients at a time"
4. Never skip the discovery call
5. Never give away the 6-module program for free — give away 3 lessons and 60-minute Lunch & Learn only
6. Never quote $98/person or any per-person rate publicly
7. Never send a proposal without a discovery call first
