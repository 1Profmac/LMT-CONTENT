# Defend & Grow Strategy — 50+TechBridge
## Build It. Protect It. Monitor It. Get Paid.
Updated: May 2026

---

## THE PROBLEM

You're building something valuable in an open space. If you give away too much, competitors copy you. If you share too little, nobody finds you. The goal: **show enough to attract, protect enough to get paid.**

---

## THE RULE: WHAT / WHY / HOW

| Level | What You Share | Where | Cost |
|-------|---------------|-------|------|
| **FREE (the WHAT)** | The problem exists, the stats, the stories, why it matters | LinkedIn, YouTube, Podcast, Newsletter | $0 |
| **GATED (the WHY)** | Why your approach works, proof points, framework overview | Email list, lead magnets, webinars | Email address |
| **PAID (the HOW)** | Step-by-step curriculum, Barb lessons, customized training | LearnDash, workforce contracts, consulting | $47 - $50K+ |

### What This Means in Practice:

**Give away freely:**
- "200 adults over 50 learned AI skills in 12 weeks" (the result)
- "Most programs fail because they use one-size-fits-all" (the problem)
- "Your phone is more powerful than you think" (the hook)
- Stats, stories, inspiration, proof it works

**Gate behind email:**
- The 30 Days of AI challenge framework
- The checklist PDFs
- The worksheets
- "How we customize for each agency" overview

**Protect behind payment:**
- Full Barb lesson videos
- Agency-customized curriculum
- Step-by-step implementation
- Consulting and coaching calls
- The actual course content

## LAYER 2: PROTECT — Guard Your IP

### Legal Protection
- [ ] Copyright notice on all course materials: "© 2026 Learn More Technologies LLC. All rights reserved."
- [ ] Terms of Use on website — define what can/can't be shared
- [ ] Terms & Conditions for course enrollment — no redistribution
- [ ] NDA template for agency partners who see customized curriculum
- [ ] Register copyright with U.S. Copyright Office for course content ($65)

### Technical Protection
- [ ] LearnDash content behind login — not publicly accessible
- [ ] Watermark Barb videos with "50+TechBridge" overlay
- [ ] Disable right-click/download on lesson pages (LearnDash settings)
- [ ] Don't post full lesson videos on YouTube — teasers only
- [ ] Keep customized agency curricula off the public internet entirely

### Content Protection
- [ ] Never publish the HOW for free — only the WHAT and WHY
- [ ] Never share your agency customization process publicly
- [ ] Never post full lesson scripts on social media
- [ ] Podcast discusses concepts, not curriculum steps
- [ ] LinkedIn posts show results, not methods

### What to NEVER Give Away
1. The full Barb lesson videos (those are the product)
2. The agency customization framework (that's your moat)
3. Your partner contact list (that's your pipeline)
4. Your pricing structure for workforce contracts
5. Your HeyGen/ElevenLabs production workflow

## LAYER 4: MONETIZE — Get Paid Before You Share

### The Funnel (nothing free below the line)

```
FREE: LinkedIn post / YouTube video / Podcast episode
  → "Want the checklist? Get it free at learnmoretechnologies.com"
    → GATED: Email capture → lead magnet delivered
      → NURTURE: Newsletter (Agentic50 Weekly)
        → OFFER: Free intro call (Calendly)
          → PAID: Course ($47-$297) or Contract ($4,900-$50K+)
```

### Revenue Tiers
| Offer | Price | What They Get |
|-------|-------|--------------|
| DIY Course | $47-$297 | Self-paced Barb lessons on LearnDash |
| Lunch & Learn | $500-$1,500 | 1-hour workshop at their location |
| Launchpad | $750 | Group coaching + course access |
| Consulting | $3K-$5K | Custom AI readiness assessment |
| Workforce Contract | $4,900-$50K+ | Full customized program for agency |

### The Rule: Always Capture Before You Give
- No free PDF without an email
- No free workshop without collecting attendee info
- No free consultation without a HubSpot contact created
- No speaking engagement without collecting audience emails
