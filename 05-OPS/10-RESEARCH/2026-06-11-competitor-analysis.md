# Competitive Analysis — Digital Literacy for Adults 50+
**Date:** June 11, 2026
**Author:** Brian McKinney / Claude
**Purpose:** Compare 50+TechBridge against the top 10 similar organizations

---

## Top 10 Competitors

### 1. Senior Planet (OATS/AARP)
- **Site:** https://seniorplanet.org/
- **What:** 60+ free weekly Zoom classes, 4 physical centers (NYC, Denver, Miami, Plattsburgh), 400+ licensed partner sites
- **Strength:** Massive scale, AARP backing, community platform for 60+
- **Your edge:** You teach AI skills, they mostly teach basic digital literacy. You customize per location.

### 2. Cyber-Seniors
- **Site:** https://cyberseniors.org/
- **What:** Teen mentors teach seniors tech skills. 1,200+ tutorials. Phone support in English, Spanish, French
- **Strength:** Intergenerational model, free phone support
- **Your edge:** Peer-to-peer (50+ teaching 50+) is more relatable than teenagers teaching elders.

### 3. Oasis Connections
- **Site:** https://connections.oasisnet.org/
- **What:** 30+ courses at community sites nationwide. 65,000+ trained over 20 years
- **Strength:** Established curriculum, wide partner network (YMCAs, libraries, faith orgs)
- **Your edge:** They don't cover AI. Your content is current, theirs is legacy.

### 4. NCOA + AT&T Connected Learning
- **Site:** https://www.ncoa.org/page/connected-learning/
- **What:** In-person workshops at 50 senior centers, online courses, AT&T-funded
- **Strength:** Corporate funding, trusted brand, national reach
- **Your edge:** You're local, hands-on, and solo-founder nimble. They're slow-moving institutional.

### 5. The Senior Source (Dallas)
- **Site:** https://theseniorsource.org/classes-and-training/technology-education/
- **What:** Free in-person tech classes in Dallas, 18,000+ older adults served annually
- **Strength:** Deep local roots since 1961, Senior Planet partner
- **Your edge:** They're Dallas only. You're building a model that deploys anywhere.

### 6. AARP Digital Skills Ready@50+
- **Site:** https://programsforelderly.com/contributing-digital-skills-ready-at-50-technology-training-older-adults-aarp.php
- **What:** Workforce-focused digital skills for 50+, especially women and people of color
- **Strength:** Employment pipeline, equity focus
- **Your edge:** You cover AI + daily life skills, not just employment.

### 7. GCFLearnFree.org (Goodwill)
- **Site:** https://edu.gcfglobal.org/
- **What:** 2,000+ free lessons on Microsoft Office, internet basics, social media
- **Strength:** Massive content library, self-paced, completely free
- **Your edge:** Not built for 50+. No community. No human instruction. Generic content.

### 8. TechBoomers
- **Site:** https://techboomers.com/
- **What:** Free video tutorials on popular apps (Airbnb, Skype, YouTube, eBay)
- **Strength:** Practical app-by-app tutorials, easy to browse
- **Your edge:** They're content-only, no live instruction, no community, no AI content.

### 9. Spectrum Digital Education
- **Site:** https://corporate.charter.com/newsroom/spectrum-digital-education-empowers-seniors-through-digital-literacy
- **What:** ISP-funded digital literacy at senior centers, library partnerships
- **Strength:** Free devices + internet bundled with training
- **Your edge:** Corporate program — no heart, no customization. You build relationships.

### 10. Skillful Senior
- **Site:** https://skillfulsenior.com/
- **What:** Interactive online training — mouse skills, keyboard, internet basics, email
- **Strength:** True beginner level, gentle pace
- **Your edge:** They stop at basics. You take people all the way to AI.

## Key Takeaway

50+TechBridge is the only organization in this space teaching AI to adults 50+. Every competitor is still focused on basic digital literacy (email, browsers, mouse skills). The AI gap is the competitive advantage — use it in every pitch, grant, and partner conversation.
