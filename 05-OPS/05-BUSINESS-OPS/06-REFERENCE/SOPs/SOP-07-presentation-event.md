# SOP-07 — In-Person Presentation / Event
## Library, senior center, or speaking event checklist

**Purpose:** Run a smooth in-person session and capture everything needed for follow-up content and contacts.
**Trigger:** Any in-person LMT/50+TechBridge event.
**Owner:** Brian McKinney
**Time required:** Before (30 min prep) + During + After (60 min follow-up)
**Frequency:** Every in-person session

---

## QUICK REFERENCE
1. Bring: laptop, business cards, sign-up sheet, QR code, one-pager
2. Capture: group photo, 30-sec video clip, one testimonial clip
3. After: upload photos, LinkedIn post, HubSpot contacts, follow-up email within 24 hrs

---

## BEFORE THE EVENT

### Materials to Bring
- [ ] Business cards
- [ ] One-pager / flyer (print from `06-MARKETING/05-ONE-PAGERS/`)
- [ ] Sign-up sheet (name, email, phone) — every attendee goes into HubSpot
- [ ] QR code linking to learnmoretechnologies.com/join-now/
- [ ] Laptop with presentation loaded and tested
- [ ] Phone fully charged (backup photos/video)

### Confirm with Venue
- [ ] Do they have a projector/screen?
- [ ] Is there WiFi? Get the password.
- [ ] Ask a staff member or volunteer to be your photographer

---

## DURING THE EVENT

### Photos to Capture
- [ ] You presenting to the group (wide shot, audience visible)
- [ ] You one-on-one helping someone with their phone (close-up)
- [ ] Participants working on their devices (candid)
- [ ] The room — people engaged, screens visible (wide shot)
- [ ] Candid moment — someone smiling when they "get it"
- [ ] You at the front (headshot-style, for LinkedIn)
- [ ] Group photo at the end

### Video to Capture
- [ ] 30-second clip of you presenting (LinkedIn/YouTube Shorts)
- [ ] 15-second testimonial from a participant ("What did you learn today?")
- [ ] B-roll of hands on phones/tablets (for video production)

---

## AFTER THE EVENT (within 24 hours)

### Content
- [ ] Upload best photos to `06-ASSETS/PHOTOS/`
- [ ] Post on LinkedIn: best photo + 3-sentence event recap + booking link
- [ ] Mirror LinkedIn post to Facebook

### Contacts
- [ ] Enter all sign-up sheet names/emails/phones into HubSpot
- [ ] Tag them: "In-Person Attendee — [Location] — [Date]"
- [ ] Send follow-up email within 24 hours

### Numbers
- [ ] Count total attendees — update verified participant count if it changes documented numbers
- [ ] Note location for grant documentation

---

## COMMON MISTAKES
- Forgetting the sign-up sheet (lose contact info forever)
- Not asking someone to take photos early enough (miss the best moments)
- Skipping the LinkedIn post — event content gets the best engagement
- Waiting more than 24 hrs to follow up (people forget who you are)

## ESCALATION
- AV fails at the venue → pivot to handout-based session. Always bring printed one-pagers as backup.
- Low turnout → still run the session. Document and photograph. One person well-served is worth it.
