# Train-the-Trainer Program Design

## The Core Idea
Brian doesn't teach 50,000 people. Brian trains 500 facilitators who each train 100.

---

## Who Becomes a Facilitator?
- Librarians already running tech programs
- Senior center activity coordinators
- Community health workers
- Retired professionals who want to give back
- NGO program staff
- Adults 50+ who completed the program (pioneers training pioneers)

## Certification Program

### Module 1: The Mission (Day 1 Morning)
- Why digital literacy for 50+ matters
- The ageism problem — global data
- The 50+TechBridge philosophy: no jargon, no judgment, hands-on
- Brian's story (keynote format — sets the tone)

### Module 2: The Curriculum (Day 1 Afternoon)
- Walk through all lesson modules
- How to customize for local audience
- Using Barb (AI avatar) videos as instruction anchors
- Adapting for language, culture, and tech access level
- What to do when someone has never touched a computer

### Module 3: Facilitation Skills (Day 2)
- Teaching adults vs. teaching kids — andragogy basics
- Handling frustration and fear (the emotional side of tech training)
- Classroom setup: pairs, small groups, 1-on-1 support
- Accessibility: vision, hearing, mobility, cognitive considerations
- Managing mixed skill levels in one room

### Module 4: Logistics & Reporting (Day 3 Morning)
- Setting up a workshop at a library/senior center/community org
- Sign-up sheets, attendance tracking, follow-up
- Reporting to funders (what to measure, how to document)
- Using the 50+TechBridge facilitator dashboard (future build)

### Module 5: Certification Assessment (Day 3 Afternoon)
- Teach-back: each facilitator delivers a 15-minute mini-lesson
- Peer feedback
- Certification checklist sign-off
- Welcome to the 50+TechBridge Facilitator Network

---

## Post-Certification Support
- Monthly virtual community call (all facilitators globally)
- Slack/WhatsApp group for real-time support
- Quarterly curriculum updates pushed to all facilitators
- Annual re-certification (light — submit impact report + attend 1 refresher session)

## Pricing

| Format | Participants | Price | Brian's Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Online self-paced + live cohort | 1 person | $500 | Recorded + 1 live session |
| In-person cohort (local) | 10–20 people | $5,000–$10,000 | Brian delivers 3-day intensive |
| In-person cohort (international) | 10–20 people | $10,000–$25,000 | Brian travels + delivers |
| Institutional (org buys for staff) | 20–50 people | $15,000–$25,000 | Brian + local co-facilitator |

## The Facilitator Network = Your Moat
Once you have 500 certified facilitators across 20+ countries, you have something no one else has:
- A global distribution network for 50+ digital literacy
- Local trust and relationships in every market
- Real-time feedback loop for curriculum improvement
- A community that sells itself (facilitators recruit facilitators)
- Grant applications that show SCALE and IMPACT

This is what makes you fundable at the $500K–$1M level.
