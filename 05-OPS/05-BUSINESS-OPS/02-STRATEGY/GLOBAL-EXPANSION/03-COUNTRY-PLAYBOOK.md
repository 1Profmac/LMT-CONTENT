# Country Expansion Playbook

## Expansion Criteria
Before entering a country, answer YES to at least 3:
1. Is there a large 50+ population underserved by digital literacy?
2. Is there grant/government funding available for digital inclusion?
3. Is there an existing institutional network (libraries, community centers, NGOs)?
4. Can Brian live/work there legally (visa pathway)?
5. Does Brian want to be there? (Quality of life matters.)

---

## Phase 1 Countries (2027) — Prove the International Model

### Spain (Home Base #1)
- **Why:** Digital Nomad Visa (1–5 years), EU base, growing 50+ population, UNESCO/EU grant access
- **Visa:** €2,850/mo income requirement
- **Language:** Spanish (curriculum already being translated)
- **Entry point:** Partner with local libraries or ayuntamientos (town halls)
- **Funding:** EU Horizon Europe digital inclusion grants, Spanish government digital skills programs
- **Live:** Lisbon → Madrid → Valencia → Seville (move with the work)
- **Bonus:** Web Summit is in Lisbon (train from Madrid)

### Portugal (Home Base #2)
- **Why:** D8 Digital Nomad Visa, lower cost of living, massive expat community, EU access
- **Visa:** €3,680/mo income requirement
- **Language:** Portuguese
- **Entry point:** Expat community organizations, Câmaras Municipais (local government)
- **Funding:** EU digital equity grants, Portuguese government digitalization programs
- **Live:** Lisbon or Porto
- **Bonus:** Web Summit (Lisbon), huge African diaspora community = bridge to Africa

## Phase 2 Countries (2027–2028) — Scale with Funding Partners

### Ghana (Africa Entry Point)
- **Why:** Mastercard Foundation HQ for Africa programs, English-speaking, growing tech hub
- **Funding:** Mastercard Foundation EdTech Fellowship ($60K), USAID digital development
- **Entry point:** Partner with Mastercard Foundation grantees, local NGOs
- **Model:** Train-the-Trainer — certify local facilitators
- **Live:** Accra (1–3 month deployments, not permanent)

### Kenya
- **Why:** Largest tech hub in East Africa, Mastercard Foundation active, English-speaking
- **Funding:** Mastercard Foundation, USAID, World Bank digital skills programs
- **Entry point:** eMobilis (Mastercard Foundation partner), libraries, community centers
- **Model:** Train-the-Trainer
- **Live:** Nairobi (1–3 month deployments)

### Panama (Americas Hub)
- **Why:** No tax on foreign income, US dollar economy, Pensionado visa, bridge to Latin America
- **Visa:** Friendly Nations Visa or Pensionado (age 65+ = automatic qualification)
- **Language:** Spanish
- **Entry point:** Expat community, Panama City senior centers, AARP International contacts
- **Funding:** Inter-American Development Bank, USAID Latin America
- **Live:** Panama City or Boquete (expat community)

## The Pattern (Repeat for Every Country)

1. **Scout:** Research 50+ population, institutional partners, funding sources
2. **Connect:** Find 1–2 local partner organizations (library system, NGO, government agency)
3. **Pilot:** Deliver Train-the-Trainer to 10–20 local facilitators (Brian goes in person)
4. **Fund:** Apply for local/regional grants with local partner as co-applicant
5. **Scale:** Local facilitators run the program. Brian supports remotely.
6. **Measure:** Track learners trained, report to funders, document impact
7. **Move:** Brian moves to next country. Local team sustains.
