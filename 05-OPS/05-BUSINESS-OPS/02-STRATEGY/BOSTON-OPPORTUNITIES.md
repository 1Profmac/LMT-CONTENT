## Why Boston

- City of Boston has an **active Digital Equity Fund** (annual grants for digital training nonprofits)
- Boston Public Library runs **Tech Goes Home** — proven model, proposal window opens Oct 1
- **Age Strong Commission** = Boston's Area Agency on Aging — funds nonprofit tech training partners
- Massachusetts Broadband Institute has a **statewide Digital Literacy Initiative** (LMT qualifies now)
- Massachusetts Councils on Aging connects all senior centers statewide
- WIOA Title I funding: $2.2M flowing through MA metro region
