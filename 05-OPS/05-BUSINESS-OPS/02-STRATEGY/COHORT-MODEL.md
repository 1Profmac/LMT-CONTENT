## Why Cohorts (The Research)

| Model | Completion Rate | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Self-paced online courses | 3-5% | Industry average |
| Self-paced with deadlines | 10-20% | DigitalDefynd |
| Cohort-based learning | 85-96% | UC study, Wharton |
| **50+TechBridge (peer cohort)** | **3X industry rate** | Internal data |

### Why cohorts work specifically for adults 50+
- Peer models reduce tech anxiety — older adults learning from same-age peers outperform those learning from younger instructors (Frontiers in Sociology, 2024)
- Social connectedness matters more than self-efficacy at post-training stage (MDPI, 2020)
- Accountability through shared deadlines and group discussions
- Community beats isolation — self-paced fails older adults because they learn alone

## Cohort Flow (6-Week Standard)

```
Week 0:  Intake + onboarding (assign cohort, set expectations)
Week 1:  Module 1 + first peer meetup (virtual or in-person)
Week 2:  Module 2 + peer check-in
Week 3:  Module 3 + mid-point confidence survey
Week 4:  Module 4 + peer project sharing
Week 5:  Module 5 + LinkedIn profile workshop
Week 6:  Module 6 + completion ceremony + post-survey
Week 7:  Results report to organization
Week 8:  Scale conversation — next cohort
```

## The "Each One Teach One" Multiplier

After completing a cohort, graduates become peer mentors for the next cohort.

- 1 cohort of 20 produces 3-5 peer mentors
- Those mentors reduce facilitator load and increase next cohort's completion rate
- This is how you scale from 12 locations to 50,000 Pioneers without hiring an army of trainers
- Inspired by the model that already works: "When Shirley learns AI on Tuesday, she teaches Marcus Thursday. Marcus teaches his sister. One lesson reaches twelve people monthly."

### Scaling Math
- Year 1: 10 cohorts x 25 people = 250 Pioneers + 50 peer mentors
- Year 2: 50 peer mentors enable 25 cohorts x 30 people = 750 Pioneers + 150 peer mentors
- Year 3: 150 mentors enable 75 cohorts x 30 people = 2,250 Pioneers
- By Year 5: 50,000 is achievable with partner network
