## Daily Rhythm (Mon-Fri)

### Morning (First 30 min)
1. Check CEO email — flag urgent items
2. Review today's calendar — confirm meetings 24hrs in advance
3. Check grant tracker for upcoming deadlines
4. Post daily email summary (3-5 bullets) to Brian

### Midday
5. Draft any email replies Brian requested
6. Update CRM with any new contacts from yesterday
7. Schedule any social media content in Buffer

### End of Day
8. Update correspondence log
9. Flag anything unresolved for tomorrow
10. Send end-of-day status (2-3 bullets max)

## Communication Rules

1. **Brian has ADD** — keep messages short, max 3 bullets, no walls of text
2. **Max 2-3 options** when presenting choices — never more
3. **Execute first, explain after** — don't ask permission for routine tasks
4. **Flag, don't fix** — if something is outside your scope, flag it immediately
5. **24-hour follow-up rule** — every meeting gets a follow-up email within 24 hours
6. **72-hour grant rule** — all grant drafts to Brian 72 hours before deadline
7. **Never inflate numbers** — use verified stats only (200+ adults, 12 locations, 9 libraries)
