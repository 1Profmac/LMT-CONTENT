## The Role in One Sentence

You fill and move the pipeline so Brian can show up to close — nothing more, nothing less.

## Task Breakdown (~11 hrs/week)

### Prospecting (3 hrs/week)

- Research 10+ new partner targets per week
- Target types: public libraries, senior centers, workforce boards, community colleges, corporate HR/DEI teams, Area Agencies on Aging, faith-based community orgs
- For each contact: get director name, email, phone, org mission, population served
- Log everything in HubSpot before contacting — no contact without a CRM record

### Outreach (3 hrs/week)

- Use Claude `/outreach-email` to draft every email — no generic templates
- Personalize each outreach to the org's specific mission and population
- Send 50+ touches per week across email and LinkedIn
- Execute follow-up cadence: first touch → 3-day follow-up → 7-day follow-up → archive if no response

### Pipeline Management (2 hrs/week)

Maintain HubSpot with 6-stage discipline:

| Stage | Definition |
|-------|-----------|
| **Prospect** | Identified, not yet contacted |
| **Contacted** | Outreach sent, awaiting reply |
| **Warm** | Responded and interested |
| **Discovery Scheduled** | Call on Brian's calendar |
| **Proposal Sent** | Waiting on decision |
| **Closed Won / Closed Lost** | Done — log outcome and reason |

Rules:
- Every touch logged same day
- Warm lead = flag Brian immediately, never let it sit
- Closed Lost = log the reason (budget, timing, not a fit, no response)

### Discovery Call Prep (1 hr/week)

Before every call Brian takes, prepare a 1-page brief:
- Who they are and what they do
- Why they're a fit for 50+TechBridge
- Last interaction and any open promises
- 3 recommended talking points
- Proof points to use: 264+ adults, 13 locations, 9 libraries, 3 senior centers

Send brief to Brian 30 minutes before the call. No brief = call doesn't happen.

### Post-Call Follow-Up (1 hr/week)

- Draft follow-up email within 2 hours of every call
- Brian approves, you send
- Attach relevant materials: capability statement, sample curriculum, partner one-pager
- Update HubSpot: notes, stage change, next step, follow-up date
- If no response in 5 days, flag Brian — do not let warm leads go cold

### Proposal Support (1 hr/week)

- Use Claude to draft partnership proposals and service agreements
- Customize every proposal — no copy/paste
- Maintain a proposal library: save every approved version for reuse
- Send proposals within 24 hours of Brian's instruction to follow up
