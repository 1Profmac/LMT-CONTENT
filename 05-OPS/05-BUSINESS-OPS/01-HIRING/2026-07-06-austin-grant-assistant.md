## Application Strategy (READ THIS BEFORE DRAFTING ANYTHING)

The founder met with City of Austin Grant Coordinator Maya on 06/13/26. Her guidance shapes every narrative section:

**Framing:**
- Frame participants as **creative artists** delivering a creative end product — not just students learning technology
- The AI tools they learn are the medium; the **cohort graduation showcase** is the creative end product
- Frame the ask as expanding an **existing, proven series of cohorts** — not launching something new

**Structure:**
- Itemize every step in the creative process, from enrollment through to the public showcase event
- Each cohort (4-6 total) has its own **graduation/public showcase event** where participants present their work
- Public events = eligibility activities — these must be clearly documented

**Voice & Tone:**
- Write in **past/present tense throughout**: "We have built," "We achieved," "Our cohorts produce" — NOT "We will do" or "We plan to"
- Use **bold formatting** on key terms, outcomes, and milestones throughout the application

**Team:**
- Reviewers want to see a **team, not a solo founder** — explicitly name guest instructors, 5-6 volunteers, and any mentors
- Emphasize mentorship structure within cohorts — what participants learned and showcased

**Eligibility:**
- Every activity described must be **publicly accessible**
- Keep returning to eligibility activities — do not let a section drift into internal operations
