## Lead Qualification & Pipeline Management

- Maintain HubSpot sales pipeline with clear stage discipline:
  - **Prospect** — identified, not contacted
  - **Contacted** — outreach sent, awaiting response
  - **Warm** — responded, interested
  - **Discovery Scheduled** — call on calendar
  - **Proposal Sent** — waiting on decision

- Send brief to Brian 30 minutes before the call
