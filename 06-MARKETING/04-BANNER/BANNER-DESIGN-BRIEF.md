# Retractable Banner Design Brief
## Learn More Technologies / 50+TechBridge

---

## Overview
One retractable pull-up banner for use at conferences, speaking events, community events, WIOA workforce meetings, and partner pitches. Must work for both community audiences and institutional decision-makers.

## Print Specs
- **Size:** 33.5" wide x 79" tall (standard retractable)
- **Resolution:** 150-180 PPI minimum (viewed from 4-10 feet)
- **File format:** Print-ready PDF (preferred) or high-res PNG
- **Bleed:** 0.25" on all sides
- **Color mode:** CMYK

- Bold sans-serif throughout (Arial, Montserrat, or similar)
- All caps for company name and mission subhead
- High contrast: white and gold text on navy background

## Layout (Top to Bottom — 6 Zones)

### ZONE 1: Company Name + Mission (top)
- **"LEARNMORETECHNOLOGIES.COM"** — white, bold, all caps
- Gold horizontal rule line underneath (full width of text block)
- **"CLOSING THE DIGITAL DIVIDE"** — gold, smaller, all caps, centered below rule
- Generous padding on all sides — this is the brand anchor

### ZONE 2: Custom QR Stamp (centered)
- Circular badge design based on existing Agentic50 Pioneers Podcast stamp
- **Outer ring:** "THE PIONEERS PODCAST" (top, curved), "#AGENTIC50" (bottom, curved)
- **Gold stars** on left and right sides of ring
- **Gold border** around circle, navy background inside
- **CENTER: Replace the microphone icon with a functional QR code**
  - QR must be at least 1" x 1" on the printed banner
  - QR links to: [Brian to provide Cal.com or landing page URL]
  - White background behind QR for scan contrast
- Stamp should print at approximately 3-4" diameter

### ZONE 3: Brand Promise (centered text)
- **"Digital literacy & workforce readiness — powered by AI"**
- "Digital literacy" and "workforce readiness" in gold/bold
- Rest in white
- Font size readable from 6 feet away

### ZONE 4: Speaking Photo + CTA
- **Photo:** brianSpeaking20260615 V2.png (provided)
  - Brian McKinney at podium, audience visible, 50+TechBridge slide on screen behind him
  - Rectangular crop, full width of banner content area
  - Rounded corners (8px radius at mockup scale)
  - Subtle gold border (2px)
- **"LET'S CONNECT"** — gold, bold, all caps, centered below photo
  - Large enough to read from 6+ feet

### ZONE 5: Aspiration (centered text)
- **"50plustechbridge.com"** — gold, bold, prominent
- **"Empowering 50,000 Adults to Thrive in the Digital Age"**
  - "50,000" in large gold bold
  - Rest in white

### ZONE 6: Gold Wave (bottom)
- Organic curved gold (#C8942E) wave shape filling the bottom
- Clean — NO text on the wave currently
- This space is reserved for future sponsor logos
- Wave should be approximately 4-5" tall on the printed banner
