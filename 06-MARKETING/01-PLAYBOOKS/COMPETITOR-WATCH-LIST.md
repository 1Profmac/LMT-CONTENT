# Competitor Watch List — 50+ Tech Training Space
Updated: May 2026

## Direct Competitors

| Competitor | What They Do | Reach | Threat Level |
|-----------|-------------|-------|-------------|
| Senior Planet / AARP | Free digital skills, massive brand | Millions | High |
| GetSetUp | Peer-to-peer learning for 55+, library/govt partnerships | National | High |
| OATS (Older Adults Technology Services) | Community-based tech training | Regional | Medium |
| Cyber Seniors | Teen-to-senior mentoring | 5,000+ participants | Low |
| OLLI (Osher Lifelong Learning) | University-based, 125 locations in 50 states | National | Medium |
| Road Scholar | Nonprofit education, 6 million served since 1975 | National | Medium |
| NCOA | Partnering with 50 senior centers for tech training | National | Medium |
| AARP Foundation Digital Skills Ready@50+ | Free job-focused digital training for low-income 50+ | National | High |
| Coursera | University-partnered online courses, 65% senior enrollment increase | Global | Low (not 50+ specific) |

## What They All Have in Common
- Nonprofit or grant-funded
- Generic curriculum (not customized per agency)
- No AI-powered instruction
- No bilingual content
- No podcast presence
- No solo founder brand story

## 50+TechBridge Differentiators
1. AI avatar instructor (Barb) — none of them have this
2. Course customized per agency — key differentiator for workforce contracts
3. Bilingual content (English + Spanish) — underserved market
4. Solo founder brand (Brian built this with AI, no VC)
5. Podcast (#AGENTIC50 — The Pioneers Podcast)
6. Project-based learning, not lecture-based
7. Direct partnership model with libraries, senior centers, churches
8. Revenue model includes workforce contracts (WIOA-funded)
