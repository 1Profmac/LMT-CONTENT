## The YouTube SEO Package (7 items per video)

### 1. TITLE (most important)
- Under 60 characters
- Front-load the keyword (put the searchable phrase first)
- Use a number or data point when possible
- Create curiosity gap — make them need to click

**Formula:** [Keyword/Topic] + [Hook/Benefit] + [Qualifier]

**Good examples:**
- "AI Training for Adults 50+ | What 13 Locations Learned"
- "The $850 Billion Cost of Ageism | Data Nobody Talks About"
- "$76 Trillion Market Nobody's Building For | Adults 50+"
- "WIOA Funding for AI Training | How to Deploy This Quarter"

**Bad examples:**
- "My Thoughts on AI" (no keyword, no hook)
- "50+TechBridge Video #3" (nobody searches for this)
- "Watch This!" (clickbait with no substance)

### 4. THUMBNAIL

Thumbnails get the click. The title gets the search ranking. Both matter.

**Rules:**
- Your face, large, with an expression (surprise, intensity, confidence)
- 3-5 words MAX as text overlay
- High contrast colors (gold text on navy, or white on dark)
- NO small text — must be readable on a phone
- ONE clear focal point

**Template options:**
- Your face + "$850 BILLION" in large gold text
- Your face + "THEY LIED" in red text
- Your face + "AI AFTER 50" in gold text
- Your face pointing at a stat + "23 ORGS" in gold

**Build in Canva:** 1280x720, export as PNG

---

### 5. CHAPTERS (timestamps)

Add chapter markers in the description. YouTube shows these in the progress bar and in search results.

**Format:**
```
0:00 Introduction
0:15 The Problem
1:02 What We Found
2:15 The Solution
3:30 How It Works
4:20 Next Steps
```

Chapters must start at 0:00 and each must be at least 10 seconds.

---

### 6. END SCREEN + CARDS

**End screen (last 20 seconds of every video):**
- Subscribe button
- Link to your best-performing video
- Link to your newest video
- Link to your website (if monetized/eligible)

**Cards (clickable popups during the video):**
- Add 1-2 cards linking to related videos
- Place them at moments when a viewer might click away (keeps them on your channel)

Set these up in YouTube Studio after upload.

---

### 7. PLAYLIST

Every video goes into a playlist. Playlists rank separately in YouTube search.

**Your playlists:**
- "How to Keep Your Job Over 50" (series)
- "AI Training for Adults 50+"
- "Workforce Development & WIOA"
- "The Numbers Nobody Told You" (data/stats series)
- "50+TechBridge Course Preview"

Create these playlists now in YouTube Studio. Add videos as you publish.
