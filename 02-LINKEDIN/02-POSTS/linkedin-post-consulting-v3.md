## POST 4: The Teaching Moment

Everyone tells you to "learn AI."

Nobody tells you WHERE to start.

Here's where: one tool. One task. One week.

Pick the task you do most often at work. The boring one. The repetitive one.

Open ChatGPT. Type: "Help me do [this task] faster."

Try what it gives you. Adjust. Try again.

By Friday you'll have saved 3 hours. By next month you'll wonder how you ever did it the old way.

That's it. That's how you start.

## THE SYSTEM

**Monday/Wednesday:** Post 1-5 (rotate — authority, insight, story, teaching)
**Thursday:** Post 6 (newsletter teaser — always on Thursday when newsletter drops)

**Never in any post:**
- Prices
- Package names
- "Buy" or "sign up"
- Links to a sales page

**In every post:**
- Value
- Story
- Your voice
- #agentic50

**What happens when people DM you:**

If they DM "WORKSHOP":
"Great — tell me about your organization, how many people, and what dates work. The workshop is free."

If they DM anything else about hiring you:
"Thanks for reaching out. Before we talk, can you tell me:
1. What's your business or profession?
2. What's the one thing you wish AI could do for you?
3. Have you tried any AI tools yet?

I'll review and if it looks like a fit, I'll set up a 15-minute call."

**The selling happens on the call. Never in the post.**
