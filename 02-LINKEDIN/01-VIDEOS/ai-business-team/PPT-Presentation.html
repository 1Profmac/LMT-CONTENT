Build Your Plan & Brand — in 50 Minutes

Step 2 — Business Plan (30 min)

Open ChatGPT or Claude. Type:

"I want to start a small business helping [what you do]. My customers are [who needs it]. Help me build a simple one-page business plan."

You get: service name · pricing · target customer · first 5 marketing ideas

Step 3 — Brand (20 min)

Same tool. Next prompt:

"Give me 5 business names, a one-sentence description of what I offer, and a short professional bio."

You get: business name · tagline · bio ready to use anywhere
